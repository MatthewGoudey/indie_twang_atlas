**Blood Oranges** were an American [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country") band that formed in the late 1980s.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-allmusic-1)

The founding members were Jim Ryan (acoustic and electric [mandolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandolin "Mandolin"), guitar, and [vocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing "Singing")), Bob Kendall (bass guitar) then replaced by Liz Wood (later Liz Crawley) ( [bass guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar "Bass guitar"), [vocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing "Singing")), Andy Churchill ( [guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar "Guitar")) later replaced by Mark Spencer, and Ron Ward ( [drums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_kit "Drum kit")).[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-2) [Cheri Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheri_Knight "Cheri Knight") took over on bass guitar in 1990 after Crawley moved to Wisconsin. Between 1990 and 1994 they released two full albums ( _[Corn River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corn_River "Corn River")_, _[The Crying Tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crying_Tree "The Crying Tree")_) and one EP ( _[Lone Green Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Green_Valley?action=edit&redlink=1 "Lone Green Valley (page does not exist)")_). _[Trouser Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trouser_Press "Trouser Press")_ described them as "one of America's finest and least formulaic roots-rock combos."[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-3) The group disbanded in 1994.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-allmusic-1)

In 1995, Mark Spencer left the band to tour with [Lisa Loeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Loeb "Lisa Loeb").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-allmusic-1) Jim Ryan and Cheri Knight have gone on to other musical projects, and the band reunited to record the song "Gathering Flowers for the Master's Bouquet" for the 2003 soundtrack of the movie _[The Slaughter Rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Slaughter_Rule "The Slaughter Rule")_.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Oranges_(band)#cite_note-4)
