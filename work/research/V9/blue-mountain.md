**Blue Mountain** is an American [alt-country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt-country "Alt-country")/ [roots rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roots_rock "Roots rock") band formed in 1991 in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_MS "Oxford, MS"), [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi "Mississippi"), by [Cary Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary_Hudson "Cary Hudson") (guitar and vocals) and [Laurie Stirratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurie_Stirratt "Laurie Stirratt") (bass and harmony vocals), who is the twin sister of [John Stirratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stirratt "John Stirratt"), the bass player for the like-minded Americana band [Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco").

## History

After the dissolution of their former band, [The Hilltops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hilltops?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Hilltops"), Hudson and Stirratt moved back to Oxford and, joined by drummer Matt Brennan, began performing and recording [roots rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roots_rock "Roots rock"). With this line-up, the band released their first self-titled album on their own 4-Barrel Records in 1993. The band was signed by the independent [Roadrunner Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadrunner_Records "Roadrunner Records") label and released their second album, _Dog Days_, with new drummer [Frank Coutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Coutch?action=edit&redlink=1 "Frank Coutch") in 1995. The album comprised many songs from their first eponymous release and garnered the band a fair amount of critical and commercial success in [alt-country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt-country "Alt-country") circles, featuring the band's best-known song, "Blue Canoe." Two more albums followed on [Roadrunner Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadrunner_Records "Roadrunner Records"), _Home Grown_ in 1997, and _Tales of a Traveler_ in 1999. An album of all public-domain [roots music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_music "Traditional music") covers, simply titled _Roots_ followed in 2001, before their final farewell live release, _Tonight It's Now or Never_ in 2002.

Blue Mountain reunited in the summer of 2007. They played the [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest "South by Southwest") Music Festival in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"), in mid-March 2008, and as one of the main acts at the 2008 Double Decker Arts Festival on April 26, 2008, in Oxford, Mississippi. On August 19, 2008, they released _Midnight in Mississippi_, an album of original material, spanning 12 tracks including 3 songs from solo albums released by Hudson, as well as an album of re-recorded Blue Mountain originals entitled _Omnibus_, which strips away the distortion of the original releases and allows for a crisper, cleaner take on the 14 songs. The albums are released on [Broadmoor Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadmoor_Records "Broadmoor Records").

In 2013 Hudson and Stiratt dissolved the band following their last appearance at the North Mississippi Hill Country Picnic on June 29, 2013.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_(band)#cite_note-1)
