**Freakwater** is an American [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country") band from [Louisville, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky "Louisville, Kentucky"), with one co-founding member living in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-NYTimes-MercuryLounge-1998-1) Freakwater is known for the lead vocals of Janet Bean and Catherine Irwin, who mix harmony and melody in idiosyncratic dissonant country-folk that is reminiscent of the [Carter Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Family "Carter Family").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-FADER-Scheherazade-2016-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-ChicagoTrib-Hideout-2013-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-TrouserPress-Freakwater-2007-4)

## History

### Thrill Jockey

In 1989, Janet Beveridge Bean (of rock band [Eleventh Dream Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleventh_Dream_Day "Eleventh Dream Day")) and Catherine Irwin founded the band, and they have been supported by several musicians since then, including members of [Califone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Califone "Califone") (2005 _Thinking of You_ tour). Bassist David Wayne Gay, formerly of [Stump The Host](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stump_The_Host "Stump The Host"), is another long-time member of the band. They released their records on Chicago's [Thrill Jockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrill_Jockey "Thrill Jockey") label.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-ChicagoReader-OldPaint-1995-5) From 2006 to 2013, Bean and Irwin worked on other projects. A reissue of 1993's _Feels Like the Third Time_ as a 20-year anniversary restarted the duo playing together as Freakwater.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-ChicagoTrib-Interview-2013-6) In 2014, the band went out on the road, touring and playing the record as their main set.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-DOA-JanetBeanInterview-2015-7)

### Bloodshot Records

In February 2016, Freakwater released the record _Scheherazade_ on [Bloodshot Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloodshot_Records "Bloodshot Records"). In advance of the full-length record—-the duo's first record since 2005-—Freakwater released a single called "The Asp And The Albatross".[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-Stereogum-Scheherazade-2015-8)

### Freakons

In 2013, and again in September 2017 and July 2021, Janet Bean and Catherine Irwin of Freakwater joined with [Jon Langford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Langford "Jon Langford") and [Sally Timms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Timms "Sally Timms") of the [Mekons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekons "Mekons") to be the Freakons, performing original and cover songs about [coal mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal_mining "Coal mining") in [Appalachia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia "Appalachia"), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England "England"), and [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales "Wales"), to support the non-profit organization [Kentuckians for the Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentuckians_for_the_Commonwealth "Kentuckians for the Commonwealth"). Each time, the Freakons performed at the [Hideout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hideout_Inn "The Hideout Inn") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), and elsewhere in [Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin "Wisconsin"). In 2013, they also performed at [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco")'s [Hardly Strictly Bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardly_Strictly_Bluegrass "Hardly Strictly Bluegrass") festival. In 2017, they were accompanied by violinists [Jean Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ida_(band) "Ida (band)") of [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") and Anna Krippenstapel of [Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky "Louisville, Kentucky") ( [The Other Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Other_Years?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Other Years"), [Joan Shelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley "Joan Shelley"), etc.), and, only in Chicago, by Chicago/Louisville guitarist [James Elkington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Elkington "James Elkington") ( [The Horse's Ha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Horse's_Ha?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Horse's Ha"), etc.). In 2021, the same line-up, without Elkington, performed, and they released the live album _Freakons_, recorded at the 2017 Chicago performances.

## Artistry

Hilary Saunders of _[Paste Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paste_(magazine) "Paste (magazine)")_ assessed the band's musical style: " _Old Paint_ musically looks backwards. It has the jauntiness of [barn dances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barn_dance "Barn dance"), complete with [fiddles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiddle "Fiddle"), [dobros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobro "Dobro"), and [tambourines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambourine "Tambourine"). Yet, Irwin and Bean's voices, content in their different [ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocal_range "Vocal range"), somehow meld to tell [country tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_literature "Oral literature") that still sound ahead of their time."[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater#cite_note-16)
