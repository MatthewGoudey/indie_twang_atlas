**The Honeydogs** is a band from [Saint Paul, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul,_Minnesota "Saint Paul, Minnesota") that opened at [First Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Avenue_(nightclub) "First Avenue (nightclub)") in 1994.

## History

The band's popularity grew after signing with record label [Mercury Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Records "Mercury Records")[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-1) and their major label debut album release, _[Seen a Ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seen_a_Ghost "Seen a Ghost")_ in 1997.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-ghost1-2) In June 1998 the band fired guitarist Tommy Borscheid. Two months later bass player Trent Norton had an almost fatal asthma attack and fell into a coma for three days.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-cmj01-3)

The band left Mercury after the executives who signed them left the label when Mercury's parent [PolyGram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PolyGram "PolyGram") merged with Universal. The merger meant the band was on the new [Island Def Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Island_Def_Jam_Music_Group "The Island Def Jam Music Group") label. It took seven months (August 1999 to March 2000) to leave IDJ. They signed with Palm Records in May 2000 to release their next album, _Here's Luck_.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-cmj01-3)

## Film

Adam Levy and producer Rick Fuller worked together to make a film version of the album _10,000 Years_ which is a feature-length music video.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-4)

## Honors and awards

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Honeydogs_-_First_Avenue_Star.jpg/500px-Honeydogs_-_First_Avenue_Star.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Honeydogs_-_First_Avenue_Star.jpg) Star honoring the Honeydogs on the outside mural of the Minneapolis nightclub [First Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Avenue_(nightclub) "First Avenue (nightclub)")

The Honeydogs were honored with a star on the outside mural of the [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis "Minneapolis") nightclub [First Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Avenue_(nightclub) "First Avenue (nightclub)"),[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-FirstAveStars-5) recognizing performers which have played sold-out shows or have otherwise demonstrated a major contribution to the culture at the iconic venue.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-StarTribune2019-6) Receiving a star "might be the most prestigious public honor an artist can receive in Minneapolis," according to journalist Steve Marsh.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honeydogs#cite_note-MSPMag2019-7)
