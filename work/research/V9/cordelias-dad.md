**Cordelia's Dad** is a [folk punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_punk "Folk punk") band from [Northampton, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northampton,_Massachusetts "Northampton, Massachusetts"), that combines [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music") and [punk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk_rock "Punk rock") influences and was instrumental in the creation of the genre later to be dubbed " [No Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country")".\[ _[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_\] The band formed in 1987 and was active until 1998, when the members relocated to different parts of the country. After releasing an album of older material in 2002 the band reunited in 2007 for their twentieth anniversary.

## Career

Cordelia's Dad was founded in 1987 by [Amherst College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst_College "Amherst College") students [Tim Eriksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Eriksen "Tim Eriksen") (bass, vocals), Peter Irvine (drums), Tom King (guitar). Although all three had previous experiences with [traditional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_music "Traditional music") American music their initial sound was closer to that of [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock") bands like [Dinosaur Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur_Jr. "Dinosaur Jr.") and accordingly they recorded their first, [eponymous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eponymous "Eponymous") album at [Fort Apache Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Apache_Studios "Fort Apache Studios") in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston"). The main distinguishing characteristic from their Fort Apache peers was that with few exceptions, their early recorded output consisted entirely of Anglo-American traditional folk tunes recorded as rock songs. Their second album _How Can I Sleep_, recorded by [Dave Schramm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Schramm_(musician) "Dave Schramm (musician)") continued the prominent use of traditional material, but increased the share of songs played as acoustic folk songs, starting a trend to call the sound of the band " [schizophrenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schizophrenic "Schizophrenic")". The transition to folk outfit was completed in 1993 with the recording of the limited release mini-album _The Joy Fun Garden_ for German mail order label Return to Sender (a sublabel of [Normal Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_Records?action=edit&redlink=1 "Normal Records"), which released all Cordelia's Dad albums in Germany). King left after the recording to be replaced by bassist Cath Oss. Eriksen switched to guitar and their third regular album, _Comet_ (1995) continued the conversion toward an acoustic sound, with only a single song, the closer "Jersey City", being played with rock instruments.

In 1996, the band released the punk-infused live album _Road Kill_ (1996) as a farewell to their rock side, which for the next years would continue only as a side project named "io". The members of the main, now fully acoustic band had previously taken up [shape note](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_note "Shape note") singing, and the new interest made an appearance on the [Steve Albini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Albini "Steve Albini") produced album _Spine_ (1998), for which folk fiddler [Laura Risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Risk "Laura Risk") joined the band. _Spine_ would become their most critically acclaimed album, but also the last for four years, as band members moved away from their Western Massachusetts home base. Irvine moved to [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon") to become a music lawyer, Oss moved to [Newcastle Upon Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_Upon_Tyne "Newcastle Upon Tyne"), later joining [Spraydog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spraydog "Spraydog"), while Eriksen moved to [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis "Minneapolis") and later participated in creating the [soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Mountain_(soundtrack) "Cold Mountain (soundtrack)") for the movie _[Cold Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Mountain_(film) "Cold Mountain (film)")_.

The album _What It Is_, while released in 2002, contained music recorded during two sessions in 1997–98, and the band remained in hiatus until Eriksen and Irvine moved back to Massachusetts, and to celebrate their 20th anniversary, decided to reunite for local shows, playing both acoustic and electric sets.
