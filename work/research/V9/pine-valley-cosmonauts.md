Musical artist

**Pine Valley Cosmonauts** (PVC) are a musical ensemble from [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois "Illinois").

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The group was initiated by [Jon Langford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Langford "Jon Langford") (also of the [Waco Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waco_Brothers "Waco Brothers") and [The Mekons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mekons "The Mekons")) as a covers group, with both repertory and cast of backing members constantly shifting. The name was first used for Langford's 1995 album of [Johnny Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Cash "Johnny Cash") cover songs. In 1998, Langford enlisted the help of a number of prominent alt-country musicians (including [Neko Case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Case "Neko Case"), [Alejandro Escovedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Escovedo "Alejandro Escovedo"), and [Robbie Fulks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbie_Fulks "Robbie Fulks")) for a full-length tribute album to [Bob Wills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Wills "Bob Wills"). The PVC served next as the backing band for [Kelly Hogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Hogan "Kelly Hogan")'s second album, _Beneath the Country Underdog;_ Hogan had provided vocals on the Wills tribute. Their next album, _The Executioner's Last Songs_, was released in 2002 and is a collection of songs about death. Following this record's success, the group recorded two more albums of songs about death as benefits for the Illinois Coalition Against the Death Penalty. These albums featured such guests as [Steve Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Earle "Steve Earle") and [Mark Eitzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Eitzel "Mark Eitzel"). The group rarely performs live, though it occasionally does special performances in the Chicago area. In 2007 the group appeared as backing band on [Chumbawamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chumbawamba "Chumbawamba") member [Danbert Nobacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danbert_Nobacon "Danbert Nobacon")'s solo album _The Library Book of the World_, as well as playing live with Nobacon.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-PVC-ClosingTime_ChicagoReader2021-07-08-1)

The PVC appeared at the [Old Town School of Folk Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Town_School_of_Folk_Music "Old Town School of Folk Music") (OTSFM) in Chicago, Illinois, on October 10, 2009, where the band was originally scheduled to back [Australian Aboriginal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Aboriginal "Australian Aboriginal") singer [Roger Knox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Knox "Roger Knox"), but Knox's US visa was denied.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-Australian-Survivor-2013-2) Instead, the band played a number of Knox's songs accompanied by singer [Sally Timms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Timms "Sally Timms"). A second set had the band supporting a performance by [Rosie Flores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosie_Flores "Rosie Flores"). The Knox-Langford tour finally occurred in 2012, including performances at OTSFM and the [Hardly Strictly Bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardly_Strictly_Bluegrass "Hardly Strictly Bluegrass") festival in [San Francisco, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco,_California "San Francisco, California").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-Australian-Survivor-2013-2)

On July 17, 2021, PVC performed at Chicago's [Hideout Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hideout_Inn "Hideout Inn") to release their "The Closing Time 2020" album, a song-for-song cover of/tribute to [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits "Tom Waits")'s 1973 first album _[Closing Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closing_Time_(album) "Closing Time (album)")_. The album and performances were co-produced and financed by [Greg Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goose_Island_Brewery "Goose Island Brewery")'s [Virtue Cider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtue_Cider?action=edit&redlink=1 "Virtue Cider (page does not exist)") brewery, to support the [Chicago Independent Venue League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Independent_Venue_League?action=edit&redlink=1 "Chicago Independent Venue League (page does not exist)") (CIVL), a partner and predecessor of the [National Independent Venue Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Independent_Venue_Association "National Independent Venue Association") (NIVA). Guests on the album include " [Andrew Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Bird "Andrew Bird"), [Sally Timms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Timms "Sally Timms"), [Jeff Tweedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Tweedy "Jeff Tweedy"), [Rosie Flores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosie_Flores "Rosie Flores") and [Janet Bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freakwater "Freakwater")".[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-PVC-ClosingTime_ChicagoReader2021-07-08-1)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Valley_Cosmonauts#cite_note-5)
