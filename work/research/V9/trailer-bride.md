**Trailer Bride** was a [Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel_Hill,_North_Carolina "Chapel Hill, North Carolina"), [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina "North Carolina")–based [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country") rock band signed to [Bloodshot Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloodshot_Records "Bloodshot Records").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-bs-1) Formed in 1993, the band consisted of Melissa Swingle (vocalist, guitarist), Robert Mitchener (bass guitar), Brad Goolsby (drummer), Bryon Settle (guitarist) and Scott Goolsby (guitarist).[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-amg-2) In the summer of 1997, after the release of their first album, Daryl White replaced Mitchener as bassist.

The band is known for a [southern gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_gothic "Southern gothic") sound, with lyrics reminiscent of the works of [Flannery O'Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flannery_O'Connor "Flannery O'Connor").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-iw-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-bp-4) Their songs are described as "spooky", and often contained dark themes of death, sin and sex.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-bs-1) The first album, _[Trailer Bride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride_(Trailer_Bride_album) "Trailer Bride (Trailer Bride album)")_, features songs of local character that celebrate [Chatham County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatham_County,_North_Carolina "Chatham County, North Carolina"), and the main route to the nearby town of Chapel Hill, [US 15-501](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_15-501 "US 15-501"). The first track of their album _[High Seas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Seas_(album) "High Seas (album)")_, "Jesco" is a homage to [Jesco White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesco_White "Jesco White"), the [Appalachian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia "Appalachia") "Dancing Outlaw". The band met White while playing a show with [Hasil Adkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasil_Adkins "Hasil Adkins"). White told Swingle, "I don't get out much on account of being famous", which was used as a line in the song.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-knox-5)

Swingle started playing music when she was pregnant with her child, Isabel. She learned that fumes from her oil painting could affect the baby, so while looking for another creative outlet, she began to play guitar. Isabel's artwork is on the album _[High Seas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Seas_(album) "High Seas (album)")_.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_Bride#cite_note-knox-5)
