**The Sadies** are a Canadian [rock and roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_and_roll "Rock and roll") / [country and western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_and_western "Country and western") band from [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario "Ontario"). The band consists of Travis Good, Sean Dean and Mike Belitsky. [Dallas Good](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Good "Dallas Good"), a founding member, died in 2022. Dallas and Travis are the sons of Margaret and Bruce Good, and nephews of Brian and Larry Good, who are members of the Canadian [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") group [The Good Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Brothers "The Good Brothers").

## Background

In 1994, Dallas Good co-founded The Sadies as a two-piece with bassist Sean Dean.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-1) They were joined by a series of drummers: Ted Robinson, followed by [Andrew Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Scott_(drummer) "Andrew Scott (drummer)") (later of [Sloan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_(band) "Sloan (band)")), and finally [Mike Belitsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Belitsky "Mike Belitsky").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-2) Eventually, Dallas's brother Travis Good joined on guitar and fiddle. In 1998, the group released their first album, _Precious Moments_. Several more albums followed, including _In Concert Vol. 1_ in 2006.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-Inc.2006-3)

In addition to their own recordings, the Sadies often collaborate with other artists, such as [Blue Rodeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Rodeo "Blue Rodeo"), [Jon Langford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Langford "Jon Langford"), [Gord Downie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gord_Downie "Gord Downie"), [Andre Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Williams_(musician) "Andre Williams (musician)") (on the 1999 country-influenced _Red Dirt_ and the 2012 release entitled "Night and Day"), [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young "Neil Young") (track "This Wheel’s on Fire" on the album _Garth Hudson Presents a Canadian Celebration of The Band_[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-4)) and have recorded, written and toured extensively as the backing band for singer [Neko Case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Case "Neko Case"). They have also toured and recorded with Jon Spencer and Matt Verta-Ray as [Heavy Trash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_Trash "Heavy Trash"), as well as [John Doe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Doe_(musician) "John Doe (musician)"). They are considered honorary members of [The Mekons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mekons "The Mekons"). The 2005 documentary _Come on Down: Searching for the American Dream_ features music by the Sadies.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-5) The Sadies' music is featured in many films and television shows, for example, [Adult Swim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_Swim "Adult Swim")'s _[12 oz. Mouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12_oz._Mouse "12 oz. Mouse")_. An instrumental performed by the band can be heard in episode 5 of season 1, during a scene in which Rhoda the bartender is murdered. On 18 May 2010, the Sadies released _[Darker Circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darker_Circles "Darker Circles")_ CD/LP through Outside Music and Yep Roc Records. _Darker Circles_ was a shortlisted nominee for the [2010 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Polaris_Music_Prize "2010 Polaris Music Prize") and in 2012, won a [Juno Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award "Juno Award") with Mike Roberts for best video.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-AuxPolaris-6)

The Sadies are collectively members of the group [The Unintended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Unintended "The Unintended"), along with [Greg Keelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Keelor "Greg Keelor") of Blue Rodeo and [Rick White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_White_(musician) "Rick White (musician)") of Eric's Trip/Elevator. They also make multiple contributions to [Garth Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garth_Hudson "Garth Hudson")'s 2010 album _Garth Hudson Presents: A Canadian Celebration of The Band_, by performing " [The Shape I'm In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shape_I'm_In_(The_Band_song) "The Shape I'm In (The Band song)")", and also by [backing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup_band "Backup band") [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young "Neil Young"), for " [This Wheel's On Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Wheel's_On_Fire "This Wheel's On Fire")", and [Mary Margaret O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Margaret_O'Hara "Mary Margaret O'Hara"), for "Out of the Blue".[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-7) All performances on that album featured Hudson on keyboards. The Sadies have performed with other members of the Good Brothers intermittently over the years using the name "The Good Family", and in 2013 released the album _The Good Family Album_, as well as performing live under that name.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-8)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/The_Sadies_at_BSOMF_2011-3.jpg/500px-The_Sadies_at_BSOMF_2011-3.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Sadies_at_BSOMF_2011-3.jpg) The Sadies performing at [Burlington's Sound of Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington's_Sound_of_Music_Festival "Burlington's Sound of Music Festival") Festival in 2011

Among the band's last studio releases are _Internal Sounds_, released on 17 September 2013 through [Outside Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outside_Music "Outside Music"); _[And the Conquering Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And_the_Conquering_Sun "And the Conquering Sun")_, their 2014 collaboration with Gord Downie on [Arts & Crafts Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_&_Crafts_Productions "Arts & Crafts Productions"); and _Northern Passages_, released in February 2017 on [Yep Roc Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yep_Roc_Records "Yep Roc Records").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-9)

Dallas Good died unexpectedly on February 17, 2022, at the age of 48.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-Hudson-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-11) The cause was a recently detected heart condition.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-12) _[Colder Streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colder_Streams "Colder Streams")_, the last Sadies album completed before his death, was released in July 2022,[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-13) to critical acclaim.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-14) The album was the winner of the [Juno Award for Adult Alternative Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award_for_Adult_Alternative_Album_of_the_Year "Juno Award for Adult Alternative Album of the Year") at the [Juno Awards of 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Awards_of_2023 "Juno Awards of 2023"),[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-15) and was shortlisted for the [2023 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Polaris_Music_Prize "2023 Polaris Music Prize").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-16)

In 2024, the band released their first new album recorded without Dallas Good, a collaboration with Rick White entitled _Rick White and the Sadies_.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-17) The album was longlisted for the [2025 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polaris_Music_Prize "2025 Polaris Music Prize").[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-18) White, a longtime friend of the band's, had previously recorded his own album of Sadies covers, _Rick White Plays the Sadies_, in 2022 following Dallas Good's death.

In 2025, the band appeared on "Now That You're Gone", a tribute song to Dallas Good recorded by The Good Brothers.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-19)

_Live at 6 O'Clock_, compiling live performances from their 2014 tour with [Gord Downie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gord_Downie "Gord Downie"), was released in February 2026.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies#cite_note-20)
