**The Handsome Family** is an American music duo consisting of husband and wife Brett and Rennie Sparks formed in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), Illinois, and as of 2001 based in [Albuquerque, New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albuquerque,_New_Mexico "Albuquerque, New Mexico").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-1) They are perhaps best known for their song " [Far from Any Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_from_Any_Road "Far from Any Road")" from the album _[Singing Bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing_Bones "Singing Bones")_, which was used as the main title theme for the first season of the 2014 crime drama _[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective")_. The band's tenth album, _[Unseen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unseen_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Unseen (The Handsome Family album)")_, was released in 2016.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Wall_Street_Journal_2016-07-13-2) The band's 11th studio album _[Hollow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_(album) "Hollow (album)")_, was released on September 8, 2023.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-FolkRadioUK2023-3)

## History

Husband-and-wife duo Brett Sparks (vocals, guitar, keyboards) and Rennie Sparks (bass, banjo, vocals) formed the band in 1993, along with drummer Mike Werner. The band would later revolve around Rennie, who writes the lyrics, and Brett, who writes the music.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-4) Guest musicians complete the band line-up for recordings and live work.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Strong-5)

Regarding the band name, Brett said in an interview: "It's just kind of a stupid name. We used to have this really obnoxious drummer, and he used to call me 'Handsome', that was his nickname for me, I think for sarcastic reasons... And he wanted to call it the Handsome Family... and we thought it was funny, too. We thought it was a good name."[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-6)

Brett is originally from [Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa,_Texas "Odessa, Texas"), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas "Texas") and Rennie from [Suffolk County, Long Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island "Long Island").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7) The band toured extensively throughout both America and Europe in support of early releases _[Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Odessa (The Handsome Family album)")_ (1994) and _[Milk and Scissors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_and_Scissors "Milk and Scissors")_ (1996).[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Strong-5) During that time, Brett suffered an emotional breakdown, resulting in his hospitalization and diagnosis of [bipolar disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_disorder "Bipolar disorder").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7)

They wrote their third full-length album, _[Through the Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Through_the_Trees "Through the Trees")_, in the aftermath of these events, and included a song, "My Ghost", inspired by Brett's experiences in the psychiatric unit.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-LATimes2014-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-9) They recorded the album in 1998, using [Jeff Tweedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Tweedy "Jeff Tweedy")'s mobile studio,[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Strong-5) and brought the band to a wider audience. _[Uncut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncut_(magazine) "Uncut (magazine)")_ named it the "Best New Country Album of the Year".[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7)

A growing following and raised profile allowed Brett and Rennie to give up their day jobs and work on the band full time. They toured America and Europe again in support of the 2000 album _[In the Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Air_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "In the Air (The Handsome Family album)")_.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7) That year, Rennie's book of short stories, _Evil_, was released by Chicago publisher Black Hole Press.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-10) In 2001, they moved to [Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albuquerque,_New_Mexico "Albuquerque, New Mexico"), New Mexico, and released _[Twilight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Twilight (The Handsome Family album)")_, with Allmusic opining that the album leads "the listener down a lovely yet dark trail few would dare tread twice".[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-11) In 2002, they released a live album, _[Live at Schuba's Tavern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_at_Schuba's_Tavern "Live at Schuba's Tavern")_, a recording from the _In the Air_ tour from Chicago in December 2000.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Strong-5)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7)

The duo appeared in the 2003 documentary _[Searching for the Wrong-Eyed Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searching_for_the_Wrong-Eyed_Jesus "Searching for the Wrong-Eyed Jesus")_,[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-infoBox-12) as well as the soundtrack album released two years later.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-meowgonSoundtrack-13)

They have subsequently released the albums _[Singing Bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing_Bones "Singing Bones")_ (2003), _[Last Days of Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Days_of_Wonder "Last Days of Wonder")_ (2006), _[Honey Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey_Moon_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Honey Moon (The Handsome Family album)")_ (2009), _[Wilderness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilderness_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Wilderness (The Handsome Family album)")_ (2013), and _[Unseen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unseen_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Unseen (The Handsome Family album)")_ (2016) as well as the collections _[Smothered and Covered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smothered_and_Covered "Smothered and Covered")_ (2003) and _Scattered_ (2010).[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-14) Their latest album, _[Hollow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Hollow (The Handsome Family album)")_, was released in September 2023.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-15)

The track "Far from Any Road", from the album _[Singing Bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing_Bones "Singing Bones")_, was used as the main title theme song for HBO's 2014 crime drama _[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective")_ by the show's music director, [T Bone Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_Bone_Burnett "T Bone Burnett"). _[The Sydney Morning Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sydney_Morning_Herald "The Sydney Morning Herald")_ included season one's opening sequence in their list of the "Ten of the Best" title sequences on television.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-16) The song later appeared in an episode of _[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons "The Simpsons")_, and was used as intro music during [Guns N' Roses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guns_N'_Roses "Guns N' Roses")' 2014 tour.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7)

The band's tenth album, _[Unseen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unseen_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Unseen (The Handsome Family album)")_, was released on September 16, 2016, the first new release on the band's own label Milk & Scissors Music[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Wall_Street_Journal_2016-07-13-2) and through long-time label [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music") in Europe. In a departure from recent prior albums, the main duo of Brett and Rennie Sparks were joined by three guest musicians: David Gutierrez (mandolin and dobro), Alex McMahon (guitar, baritone guitar, pedal steel), and Jason Toth (drums).[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-HFUnseen-17)

The band's 11th studio album _[Hollow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_(album) "Hollow (album)")_, was released on September 8, 2023. The songs were written during the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"). The chorus of the album's lead single, "Joseph", was inspired by a nightmare of lyricist Rennie Sparks, who began talking in her sleep one night, shouting, "Come into the circle, Joseph. There’s no moon tonight!" Brett wrote the words down, and the duo fleshed out the song the next day.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-FolkRadioUK2023-3)

## Musical style

The Handsome Family's music has been classified as [gothic country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_country "Gothic country") or gothic [Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_music "Americana music"),[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-18)[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-19)[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Compton-20)[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Plagenhoef-21) [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country"),[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-22)[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-23)[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-AM-24) avant-country,[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Plagenhoef-21) [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"),[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Cober-Lake-25) [country blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_blues "Country blues"),[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Compton-20) [country rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rock "Country rock"),[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-avclub-26) [gothic folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_folk "Gothic folk"),[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Cober-Lake-25) [roots rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roots_rock "Roots rock")[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Plagenhoef-21) and traditional country.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-AM-24) The Handsome Family's style is a blend of [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), [bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_music "Bluegrass music"), and [murder ballads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_ballad "Murder ballad").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7) Early recordings have [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") elements, but these were less evident from 1996's _Milk and Scissors_ onwards.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Layne-7) The duo's penchant for a tongue-in-cheek, macabre approach has been noted by many critics; Andy Fyfe of _[Mojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojo_(magazine) "Mojo (magazine)")_ called them "Americana's ghostly [Sonny & Cher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonny_&_Cher "Sonny & Cher")."[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Mojo-27) A.V. Club reviewer Christopher Bahn compared their music to "a collaboration between [Hank Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Williams "Hank Williams") and [Edgar Allan Poe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Allan_Poe "Edgar Allan Poe")."[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-avclub-26)

Rennie's lyrics have a strong [storytelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storytelling "Storytelling") component, drawing on themes from [Gothic fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_fiction "Gothic fiction"), as well as [American folk music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_folk_music "American folk music"), and often involving macabre subjects as murders, suicides and ghosts. Some songs are also based on actual historical figures or events, including the lives and deaths of [Amelia Earhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amelia_Earhart "Amelia Earhart") ("Amelia Earhart vs. the Dancing Bear", on _[Milk and Scissors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_and_Scissors "Milk and Scissors")_), [Emily Shore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Shore "Emily Shore") ("Emily Shore 1819–1839", on _Milk and Scissors_), [Robert Wadlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Wadlow "Robert Wadlow") ("The Giant of Illinois", on _[Through the Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Through_the_Trees "Through the Trees")_), [Natalie Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Wood "Natalie Wood") ("Natalie Wood", an [outtake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outtake "Outtake") from _[Twilight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "Twilight (The Handsome Family album)")_, which appears on _[Smothered and Covered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smothered_and_Covered "Smothered and Covered")_), and [Nikola Tesla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikola_Tesla "Nikola Tesla") ("Tesla's Hotel Room", on _[Last Days of Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Days_of_Wonder "Last Days of Wonder")_). The title of _Last Days of Wonder_ is a reference to [Puritan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puritan "Puritan") scientist and witch-hunter [Cotton Mather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Mather "Cotton Mather")'s 1693 book _[Wonders of the Invisible World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonders_of_the_Invisible_World "Wonders of the Invisible World")_, which Rennie found intriguing because of what she called its "madness brimming under the surface of things."[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-avclub-interview-28)

## Covers

The Handsome Family's songs have been covered repeatedly by musician [Andrew Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Bird "Andrew Bird"), including a full-length album of covers released in 2014, _[Things Are Really Great Here, Sort Of…](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Things_Are_Really_Great_Here,_Sort_Of%E2%80%A6 "Things Are Really Great Here, Sort Of…")_

[Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco")’s Jeff Tweedy regularly performs "So Much Wine" in concert.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-LATimes2014-8)

[Sally Timms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Timms "Sally Timms") of [the Mekons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mekons "The Mekons") covered three Handsome Family songs—"The Sad Milkman", "Snowbird", and "Drunk By Noon"—on her 1999 album _[Cowboy Sally's Twilight Laments for Lost Buckaroos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowboy_Sally's_Twilight_Laments_for_Lost_Buckaroos "Cowboy Sally's Twilight Laments for Lost Buckaroos")_.[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-29)

In 2022, Bird also contributed to [Phoebe Bridgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Bridgers "Phoebe Bridgers")'s holiday cover of their song "So Much Wine" from _[In the Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Air_(The_Handsome_Family_album) "In the Air (The Handsome Family album)")_ alongside Irish actor [Paul Mescal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Mescal "Paul Mescal") and bandmates Marshall Vore and Harrison Whitford. The proceeds from sales of the cover, as with all of Bridgers's holiday covers, go to the Los Angeles LGBT Center.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Pitchfork2023-30)

## Honors and awards

In 2014, Chicago city alderman [Robert Fioretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Fioretti "Robert Fioretti") sponsored a resolution declaring September 5, 2014 as "Brett and Rennie Sparks Day" in Chicago "for their exceptional and distinctive talents and noteworthy artistic contributions".[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handsome_Family#cite_note-Chicago2014-31)
