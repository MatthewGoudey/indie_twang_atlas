Bandcamp page description text says "released 25 July 2022" (original quiet self-release, no PR/label). Later digital re-release Nov 20 2022 and vinyl pressing Jan 17 2023 via Canvasback/Transgressive. Debut LP, 10 (+2 bonus) tracks, produced/mixed by Noah Kesey and Greg Freeman in Burlington VT 2020-2022. Tracks: Horns, Right Before the Last Waves Took Vestris, Long Distance Driver, Colorado, Come and Change My Body, Tower, etc.
