Studio albums: yep definitely (2018, Self-released); I Was Trying to Describe You to Someone (2020, Orindal Records); Twin Plagues (2021, Orindal Records); Mowing the Leaves Instead of Piling 'em Up (2022, Orindal Records, covers); Rat Saw God (2023, Dead Oceans); Bleeds (2025, Dead Oceans).
Live: Wednesday on Audiotree Live (2021, Audiotree).
EPs: How Do You Let the Love Into the Heart That Isn't Split Wide Open (2018, Sub-Fi); Wednesday (2019, Manic Static); Guttering (2021, Super Enema).
Band formed 2017 Asheville NC as Karly Hartzman's solo project w/ Daniel Gorham; MJ Lenderman joined as full member on Twin Plagues; left Orindal for Dead Oceans after Mowing the Leaves; Bleeds released Sept 19 2025.
