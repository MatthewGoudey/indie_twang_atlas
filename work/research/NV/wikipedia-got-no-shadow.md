Mary Lou Lord, Got No Shadow, released early 1998 on Work Records (Sony Music division), her only major-label full-length.
Tracks: His Lamest Flame; Western Union Desperate; Lights Are Changing; Seven Sisters; Throng of Blowtown; The Lucky One; She Had You; Some Jingle Jangle Morning (When I'm Straight); Shake Sugaree; Two Boats; Supergun; Down Along the Lea; Subway.
