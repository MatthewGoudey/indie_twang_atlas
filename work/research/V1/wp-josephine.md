_**Josephine**_ is the third and final studio album by [Magnolia Electric Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Electric_Co. "Magnolia Electric Co.") For the recording of the album, the band teamed up with frequent collaborator [Steve Albini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Albini "Steve Albini") at his [Electrical Audio Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Audio "Electrical Audio") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephine_(album)#cite_note-Quietus-Molina-2014-9) The record was conceived as a tribute to late Magnolia Electric Co. bassist Evan Farrell,[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephine_(album)#cite_note-DiS_review-4)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephine_(album)#cite_note-Pitchfork_review-7) and took two weeks to write and record.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephine_(album)#cite_note-Quietus-Molina-2014-9) _Josephine_ would also be the penultimate album released during frontman [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina")'s lifetime – the last being a [collaboration with Will Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molina_and_Johnson "Molina and Johnson") – before his death in 2013.
