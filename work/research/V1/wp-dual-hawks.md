_**Dual Hawks**_ is an album by [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") and [South San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band) "South San Gabriel (band)").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_Hawks#cite_note-6) It consists of 23 tracks. Will Johnson is the principal songwriter for both bands.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_Hawks#cite_note-7)
