**Arbouretum** is an American [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock") band started by musician [Dave Heumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Heumann?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dave Heumann (page does not exist)") in 2002.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbouretum#cite_note-1) They have released ten albums, four of them on [Thrill Jockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrill_Jockey "Thrill Jockey") Records.
