_**Navigational**_ is the second full-length album by [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic"). The album was recorded by [Matt Pence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Pence "Matt Pence") in Milstadt, Illinois, at [Jay Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Farrar "Jay Farrar")'s studio.
