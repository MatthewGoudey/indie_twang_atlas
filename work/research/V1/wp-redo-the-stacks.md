_**Redo The Stacks**_ is the first full-length album by [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic"). The album was recorded and mixed in Denton, TX by [Matt Pence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Pence "Matt Pence"). All songs were written and performed by [Will Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician) "Will Johnson (musician)").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redo_the_Stacks#cite_note-1)

## Reception

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Redo_the_Stacks&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Reception")\]

Malcolm Mayhew wrote in the _[Fort Worth Star-Telegram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Worth_Star-Telegram "Fort Worth Star-Telegram")_ that the "intentionally grainy _Redo the Stacks_ was recorded in, among other places, Johnson's bedroom, and while listening to it, you can't help but wonder if this is just [Funland's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#Funland "Will Johnson (musician)") old stuff playing on his stereo in the background, with the speakers filtered through a four-track".[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redo_the_Stacks#cite_note-2) In his review for the _[Dallas Morning News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Morning_News "Dallas Morning News")_, Thor Christensen wrote that the album is a "masterful fusion of pop and punk into bare-bones rock 'n' roll" and that "Johnson and his co-horts perform their songs with haphazard glee".[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redo_the_Stacks#cite_note-3)
