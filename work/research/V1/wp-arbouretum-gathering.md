_**The Gathering**_ is a 2011 album by the American band [Arbouretum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbouretum "Arbouretum"). _[Mojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojo_(magazine) "Mojo (magazine)")_ placed the album at number 27 on its list of "Top 50 albums of 2011"[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gathering_(Arbouretum_album)#cite_note-1) while _[Uncut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncut_(magazine) "Uncut (magazine)")_ placed it at number 49 on its list.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gathering_(Arbouretum_album)#cite_note-uncut2011list-2)
