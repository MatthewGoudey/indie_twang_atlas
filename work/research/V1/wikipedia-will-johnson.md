Musical artist

**Will Johnson** (born March 23, 1971)[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1) is an American musician, singer-songwriter, author, and painter who is the lead singer of the bands [Centro-matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-matic "Centro-matic") and [South San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band) "South San Gabriel (band)"). Called "one of the most prolific artists in American indie rock" by [AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic"),[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-AllMusic-Bio-2) Johnson has also released solo records and is a member of the bands [Monsters of Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk "Monsters of Folk"), [New Multitudes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Multitudes "New Multitudes"), and [Overseas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_(band) "Overseas (band)"), and he has performed as part of the [Undertow Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertow_Orchestra "Undertow Orchestra").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-TexasMonthly-SixDegrees-2012-3) He is currently a member of [Jason Isbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Isbell "Jason Isbell")'s band, [the 400 Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Isbell#The_400_Unit "Jason Isbell").

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Johnson was born in [Kennett, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennett,_Missouri "Kennett, Missouri"). When he was 11 years old, his mother remarried, and the family moved to [Killeen, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killeen,_Texas "Killeen, Texas").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1)

Johnson attended the [University of North Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas "University of North Texas"), where he was involved in [Denton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Texas "Denton, Texas")'s music scene. He double-majored in English and elementary education.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-AllMusic-Bio-2)

## Music career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Music career")\]

### Centro-Matic

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Centro-Matic")\]

[Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") started in 1995 as Johnson's side-project while he was still in college, and he released the cassette _Non-Directional Jetpack Race_ the same year. The full-length album _[Redo the Stacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redo_the_Stacks "Redo the Stacks")_ followed in 1996, and by 1997, the project had evolved into a full band.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-5) As of 2014, the group had released 11 studio albums, and they announced their breakup that year.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-6)

### South San Gabriel

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: South San Gabriel")\]

With his Centro-Matic bandmates, Johnson formed [South San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band) "South San Gabriel (band)") in 2001, and their lineup also includes a rotation of guests. Their first album, _[Songs/Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_Songs/Music "South San Gabriel Songs/Music")_, was credited to Centro-Matic on its US release and to South San Gabriel in the Netherlands.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-7)

South San Gabriel released _[Welcome, Convalescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome,_Convalescence "Welcome, Convalescence")_ and _[The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Carlton_Chronicles_%E2%80%93_Not_Until_the_Operation's_Through "The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through")_ in 2003 and 2005, respectively.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-10)

In 2008, the band issued _[Dual Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_Hawks "Dual Hawks")_, a split double-album with Centro-Matic.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-11)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-12)

### Molina and Johnson

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Molina and Johnson")\]

In 2009, Johnson collaborated with the songwriter [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina") on the album _[Molina and Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molina_and_Johnson "Molina and Johnson")_. They recorded the album over a week and a half in early 2008.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1) Johnson describes Molina as a "whirlwind of dedication and activity".[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1) Another collaboration was discussed, but it never came to fruition, before Molina's death in 2013.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1)

In 2025, Johnson and former members of Molina's backing group [Magnolia Electric Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Electric_Co. "Magnolia Electric Co.") formed the Magnolia and Johnson Electric Co., a tribute band.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-bloom-13)

### Undertow Orchestra

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Undertow Orchestra")\]

Johnson has performed as part of the [Undertow Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertow_Orchestra "Undertow Orchestra") with [David Bazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bazan "David Bazan"), [Vic Chesnutt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Chesnutt "Vic Chesnutt"), and [Mark Eitzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Eitzel "Mark Eitzel"). Johnson first met Chestnutt in 1999 in Dallas. Chesnutt was a fan of Eitzel's music, and the two were longtime friends.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1)

According to Johnson, Bob Andrews, the manager for the four musicians, had the idea in 2005 to create a band called the Undertow Orchestra from members of the [Undertow Music Collective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertow_Music "Undertow Music"). Johnson said Chesnutt was adamant that the shows not feature any solo performances, with the members working to play each other's songs as backing musicians.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1)

### Monsters of Folk

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Monsters of Folk")\]

Johnson performed with indie supergroup [Monsters of Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk "Monsters of Folk") as the drummer on a 2009 tour. The lineup included [Jim James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_James "Jim James"), [Conor Oberst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Oberst "Conor Oberst"), [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward"), and [Mike Mogis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Mogis "Mike Mogis"). The band toured the US and Europe and performed on the [PBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBS "PBS") show _[Austin City Limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_City_Limits "Austin City Limits")_ in October 2010. Johnson became an official member of the band during that time.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-WOXY-LoungeAct-Recap-2010-14)[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-WOXY-LoungeAct-Audio-2010-15)

### New Multitudes

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: New Multitudes")\]

From 2009 to 2012, Johnson collaborated with [Jay Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Farrar "Jay Farrar"), Jim James, and [Anders Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Parker "Anders Parker") on the [Woody Guthrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Guthrie "Woody Guthrie") archive project _[New Multitudes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Multitudes "New Multitudes")_, which involved the four songwriters setting music to Guthrie's unrecorded lyrics. A tribute album was released in 2012, with a short US tour following.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-16)

### Overseas

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Overseas")\]

Johnson formed the band [Overseas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_(band) "Overseas (band)") in 2009 with David Bazan and [Matt and Bubba Kadane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_and_Bubba_Kadane "Matt and Bubba Kadane"). An eponymous album was released in 2013.

### The 400 Unit

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: The 400 Unit")\]

In 2023, Johnson joined Jason Isbell's backing band, [the 400 Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Isbell#The_400_Unit "Jason Isbell").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-17)

## Writing

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Writing")\]

Johnson has authored a novel, _If or When I Call_, which was published by Goliad Media in 2021.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-MaudlinHouse-Interview-18)

## Painting

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Will_Johnson_(musician)&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Painting")\]

Since 2008, Johnson has produced [folk art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_art "Folk art") paintings, mostly centering on the subject of [baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball "Baseball") and its history. Commissions from friends eventually led to his work being exhibited in group and solo art shows.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician)#cite_note-ThanksDamnOtisGibbs-Podcast-2013-1)
