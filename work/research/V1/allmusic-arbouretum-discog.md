## [Arbouretum Biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/arbouretum-mn0000935130\#biography "Arbouretum Biography")

### Arbouretum Biography  by   [Jason Lymangrover](https://www.allmusic.com/author/jason-lymangrover "View more by Jason Lymangrover")

[![The Gathering](https://fastly-s3.allmusic.com/release/mr0003287554/front/220/hNEOKSwdJq3TqEG4uCrVDwSijaXJlYnq0St31qpAJWo=.jpg)](https://www.allmusic.com/album/the-gathering-mw0002099115 "The Gathering")
Centered around singer/songwriter [Dave Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/dave-heumann-mn0000632535), Arbouretum became an outlet for [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535)'s poetic, often mystical folk sounds. Throughout the 2000s and beyond, [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535) led a cast of Baltimore based musicians as the band crafted delicate and somewhat cosmic albums like 2011's [The Gathering](https://www.allmusic.com/album/the-gathering-mw0002099115).

[![Rites of Uncovering](https://fastly-s3.allmusic.com/release/mr0000698375/front/220/Wg6IG61k9JGa_CvW_Ra-j1WnbEN5fCjifro6xhIBuB4=.jpg)](https://www.allmusic.com/album/rites-of-uncovering-mw0000771084 "Rites of Uncovering")[Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535) had already backed up musicians like [Bonnie "Prince" Billy](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/bonnie-prince-billy-mn0000094764) and [Cass McCombs](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/cass-mccombs-mn0000162943) when he started Arbouretum in the early 2000s. The band was comprised of [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535)'s friend [Walker David Teret](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/walker-david-teret-mn0002070471) on guitar, ex- [Lungfish](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/lungfish-mn0000805156) member [Mitchell Feldstein](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mitchell-feldstein-mn0001232919) on drums, and [Corey Allender](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/corey-allender-mn0001413755) on bass. Arbouretum's debut, [Long Live the Well-Doer](https://www.allmusic.com/album/long-live-the-well-doer-mw0001570932), was released in 2004, and their second album, [Rites of Uncovering](https://www.allmusic.com/album/rites-of-uncovering-mw0000771084), came out three years later. The latter was recorded in part by [Paul Oldham](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/paul-oldham-mn0000023057) and, according to [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535), was influenced by the works of writer [Paul Bowles](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/paul-bowles-mn0001509652). Solidifying themselves with [Corey Allender](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/corey-allender-mn0001413755) on bass, [Daniel Franz](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/daniel-franz-mn0001056053) on drums, and [Steve Strohmeier](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/steve-strohmeier-mn0001056052) on guitar, [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535) reentered the studio with [Rob Girardi](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/rob-girardi-mn0000281863) again in 2008 and finished [Song of the Pearl](https://www.allmusic.com/album/song-of-the-pearl-mw0000811737) in only two months. Thrill Jockey released the record in February of 2009. The band toured the United States in support and immediately reentered the studio with producer [Matt Boynton](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/matt-boynton-mn0000686633), who had recorded much of 2007's [Rites of Uncovering](https://www.allmusic.com/album/rites-of-uncovering-mw0000771084). Arbouretum underwent a lineup change in 2010: guitarist [Steve Strohmeier](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/steve-strohmeier-mn0001056052) was replaced by keyboardist [Matthew Pierce](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/matthew-pierce-mn0003355949). Following the inspiration of archetypal psychologist [Carl Jung](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/carl-jung-mn0001804328)'s The Red Book (at least the images that led to his writing it), Arbouretum released the expansive, mythology-drenched effort [The Gathering](https://www.allmusic.com/album/the-gathering-mw0002099115) in 2011. The album was the first to feature new drummer [Brian Carey](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/brian-carey-mn0003355950). Around this time, [Heumann](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/heumann-mn0000632535) formed a friendship with [Hush Arbors](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/hush-arbors-mn0001016979)' [Keith Wood](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/keith-wood-mn0002304521). The two bands planned a joint European tour, and in 2012 released a split LP, [Aureola](https://www.allmusic.com/album/aureola-mw0002328395), to showcase their like-minded sounds. In 2013, they followed it with the full-length [Coming Out of the Fog](https://www.allmusic.com/album/coming-out-of-the-fog-mw0002437964) and nine months later the four-track, 26-minute [Gourd of Gold](https://www.allmusic.com/album/gourd-of-gold-mw0002561498) EP; the latter entirely comprised [Gordon Lightfoot](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/gordon-lightfoot-mn0000667794) covers. After a four-year layoff from recording, Arbouretum returned with their eighth studio album, 2017's [Song of the Rose](https://www.allmusic.com/album/song-of-the-rose-mw0003020925). Ninth album [Let It All In](https://www.allmusic.com/album/let-it-all-in-mw0003350794) took a turn more toward psychedelic folk and country blues, arriving in 2020 again on Thrill Jockey.
