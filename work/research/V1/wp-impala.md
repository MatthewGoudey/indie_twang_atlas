_**Impala**_ is the second album by [Songs: Ohia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impala_(album)#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impala_(album)#cite_note-3) It was released in 1998 via Happy-Go-Lucky and [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian").

## Critical reception

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Impala_(album)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Critical reception")\]

[AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic") called _Impala_ "a lovely, peeling, chipped record of emotional decay."[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impala_(album)#cite_note-auto-1) _[Tucson Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson_Weekly "Tucson Weekly")_ deemed it "spare, organ-based melancholy pop."[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impala_(album)#cite_note-4)
