**South San Gabriel** is an American [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country") band based in [Denton, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Texas "Denton, Texas").

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=South_San_Gabriel_(band)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The band is named after the San Gabriel [river fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_fork "River fork") north of [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"). The group consists of the same players from [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") with the inclusion of guests, but focuses on more introspective and subdued offerings from their repertoire. Lead singer [Will Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician) "Will Johnson (musician)"), has alternated between the two projects, while also releasing solo albums.

The first South San Gabriel album, _[Songs/Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_Songs/Music "South San Gabriel Songs/Music")_, was credited to Centro-Matic on its US release (it was credited to South San Gabriel on the Netherlands release on Munich Records).[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-1)

South San Gabriel released _[Welcome, Convalescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome,_Convalescence "Welcome, Convalescence")_ and _[The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Carlton_Chronicles_%E2%80%93_Not_Until_the_Operation's_Through "The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through")_ in 2003 and 2005, respectively.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-4)

In 2008 the band released _[Dual Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_Hawks "Dual Hawks")_ a split double-album with Centro-matic.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band)#cite_note-6)

## Discography

**Studio albums**

- _[Songs/Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_Songs/Music "South San Gabriel Songs/Music")_ (Munich, 2000) \[Netherlands; credited to [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") on U.S. release\]
- _[Welcome, Convalescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome,_Convalescence "Welcome, Convalescence")_ (Munich, 2003) \[Netherlands\]
- _[The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Carlton_Chronicles_%E2%80%93_Not_Until_the_Operation's_Through "The Carlton Chronicles – Not Until the Operation's Through")_ (Misra, 2005)
- _[Dual Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_Hawks "Dual Hawks")_ split double album with [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") (Misra, 2008)

**Singles and EPs**

- "Stark Miami Mines" split 7-inch with Okkervil River (Tight Spot, 2002)
- _I Am Six Pounds of Dynamite_ \+ 3 (Munich, 2005)
- _Eyas_ split EP with [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic") (2010) \[digital-only release on Amazon and iTunes\]

**Compilation appearances**

- "Evangeline" on _Awesome_ (Munich, 2001) \[compilation also features Centro-Matic\]
- "One Hundred Thousand Bridesmaids" on _Esto No Es Un Cactus_ (Sinedin, 2001) \[Spain; compilation also features Centro-Matic\]
- "Smelling Medicinal" on _All Areas, Vol. 41_ (Visions Magazine, 2003) \[Germany\]
- "I Am Six Pounds of Dynamite" on _Gimme Danger_ (Uncut Magazine, 2005) \[United Kingdom\]
- "I Feel Too Young to Die" on _We Have the Technology_ (Misra, 2006) \[compilation also features Centro-Matic\]
- "I Feel Too Young to Die" on _New Colors_ (Misra, 2007) \[compilation also features Centro-Matic and Will Johnson\]
- "Emma Jane" on _Houston Party 10 Aniversario_ (Sinedin, 2008)
