_**Love You Just the Same**_ is a 2003 [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") album by Texan rock band [Centro-Matic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic "Centro-Matic").

The album was rated 4 out of 5 stars by [AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_You_Just_the_Same#cite_note-1)
