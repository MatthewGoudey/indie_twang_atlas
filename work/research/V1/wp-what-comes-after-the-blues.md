_**What Comes After the Blues**_ is the first full-length recording by what became the [Magnolia Electric Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Electric_Co. "Magnolia Electric Co.") touring line-up, and the second album released by [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina") under that name. It was recorded in November 2003 by [Steve Albini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Albini "Steve Albini") at his [Electrical Audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Audio "Electrical Audio") studio in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago").
