_**Sojourner**_ is [Magnolia Electric Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Electric_Co. "Magnolia Electric Co.")'s 2007 [box-set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box-set "Box-set") release, comprising: three full-length [albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album_(music) "Album (music)"), one four-song [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play"), one [documentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary_film "Documentary film") movie on [DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD "DVD") called _The Road Becomes What You Leave_; a [celestial map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_map "Celestial map") and a medallion; all within a wooden box. The band's 2006 album, _[Fading Trails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fading_Trails "Fading Trails")_, was compiled from the four recording sessions included in their entirety on _Sojourner_.
