_**Pyramid Electric Co.**_ is the first full-length solo album by [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina"), released in 2004 on [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian") Records. It was recorded by [Mike Mogis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Mogis "Mike Mogis") and received universal critical acclaim according to [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") (with an 81 aggregate score). While critics found it hard going: "Pyramid is not [Songs: Ohia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia") but the musical equivalent of [A Season in Hell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Season_in_Hell "A Season in Hell"), not something one can take in often, but which is beautiful for the fact that it was completed at all",[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid_Electric_Co.#cite_note-9) they also commented on the sincerity of the delivery: "The delivery is too matter-of-fact, too genuine to evoke pity or sadness. It is lethargic, yet not dreary — it grabs you violently and lays you down so, so gently."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid_Electric_Co.#cite_note-10) An unusual feature of the album was that it was released as a vinyl [LP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_play "Long play") only, but each copy also contains the CD version of the album in miniaturized artwork.
