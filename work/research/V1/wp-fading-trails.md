_**Fading Trails**_ is the second studio album by [Magnolia Electric Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Electric_Co. "Magnolia Electric Co."), a project of indie musician [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina"). It is a compilation of tracks from four different recording sessions, including recordings at [Electrical Audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Audio "Electrical Audio") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), engineered by [Steve Albini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Albini "Steve Albini"), Sound of Music Studio in [Richmond, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia "Richmond, Virginia"), produced by [David Lowery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lowery_(musician) "David Lowery (musician)"), and [Sun Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Studios "Sun Studios") in [Memphis, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis,_Tennessee "Memphis, Tennessee"), engineered by James Lott. All of _Fading Trails'_ songs are featured on the boxset _[Sojourner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sojourner_(album) "Sojourner (album)")_.
