_**Protection Spells**_ is a tour-only album by [Songs: Ohia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protection_Spells#cite_note-3) It is a collection of 9 [improvised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improvisation_(music) "Improvisation (music)") pieces recorded by [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina") whilst on tour in 1999. It was limited to 500 copies and released by [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian") in 2000.

## Track listing

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Protection_Spells&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Track listing")\]

All songs written by Jason Molina.

1. "Trouble Will Find You"
2. "The Moon Undoes It All"
3. "Darkness That Strong"
4. "Keep Only One of Us Free"
5. "The World at the End of the World"
6. "Fire on the Shore"
7. "Mighty Like Love, Mighty Like Sorrow"
8. "The One Red Star"
9. "Whenever I Have Done a Thing in Flames"
