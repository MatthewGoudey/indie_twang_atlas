_**Didn't It Rain**_ is the sixth regular album by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans "Americans") musician [Songs: Ohia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia").

The album is named after " [Didn't It Rain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didn't_It_Rain "Didn't It Rain")", a traditional song popularized by [Sister Rosetta Tharpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Rosetta_Tharpe "Sister Rosetta Tharpe") in 1948 and [Mahalia Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahalia_Jackson "Mahalia Jackson") in 1954.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didn't_It_Rain_(Songs:_Ohia_album)#cite_note-:0-9) Written and published by Henry Thacker Burleigh (1866 - 1949).

It was recorded by Edan Cohen at Soundgun Studios in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia "Philadelphia") and released by [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian") on March 5, 2002. The [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") website gave the album a composite rating of 85, ranking it thirteenth among the best albums of 2002.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didn't_It_Rain_(Songs:_Ohia_album)#cite_note-10)

_Didn't It Rain_ was recorded as a live [studio album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_album "Studio album").[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didn't_It_Rain_(Songs:_Ohia_album)#cite_note-Pitchfork-11)

- [Jennie Benford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennie_Benford "Jennie Benford")
- [Mike Brenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Brenner "Mike Brenner")
- [Jim Krewson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Krewson "Jim Krewson")
- Recorded by Edan Cohen

The album title is a reference to the song of the same name by [Sister Rosetta Tharpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Rosetta_Tharpe "Sister Rosetta Tharpe").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didn't_It_Rain_(Songs:_Ohia_album)#cite_note-:0-9)
