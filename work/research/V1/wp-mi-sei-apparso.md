_**Mi Sei Apparso Come Un Fantasma**_ ( _[English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "English language"): You Came to Me as a Ghost_) is a live album by [Songs: Ohia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia"). It was recorded at Barchessone Vecchio in [Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modena "Modena"), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy "Italy") on September 27, 2000. The album received mixed reviews, with a [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") score of 60.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_Sei_Apparso_Come_Un_Fantasma#cite_note-5) While _[Pitchfork Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Media "Pitchfork Media")_ wrote that the album "...offers a better introduction to Songs: Ohia than the last couple of proper albums, which seemed like transitional or exploratory releases",[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_Sei_Apparso_Come_Un_Fantasma#cite_note-6) _The Wire_'s reviewer was underwhelmed, feeling that the disc "\[s\]till sounds like the work of someone desperate to gain the approval of the Drag City clique".[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_Sei_Apparso_Come_Un_Fantasma#cite_note-Wire-3)
