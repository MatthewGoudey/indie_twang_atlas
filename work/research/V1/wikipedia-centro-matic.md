**Centro-matic** was an American [band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_(music) "Band (music)") based in [Denton, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Texas "Denton, Texas").

## History

Centro-matic started in 1995 as a side-project for [Will Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Johnson_(musician) "Will Johnson (musician)"). It released a few singles that year and grew into a full-fledged group in 1997.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic#cite_note-1) The initial 60 songs recorded in a [Millstadt, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millstadt,_Illinois "Millstadt, Illinois") studio supplied the material for the quieter, more emotional _Navigational_ on [Idol Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idol_Records "Idol Records") and the louder, lo-fi _The Static vs. the Strings, Vol. 1_ on Quality Park Records.

Throughout his career, Johnson has had a reputation for being prolific and dynamic. So much so, that he built an entirely separate band, the much quieter side-project, [South San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_San_Gabriel_(band) "South San Gabriel (band)"), named after the San Gabriel [river fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_fork "River fork") north of [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"). The group consists of the same players from Centro-matic with the inclusion of guests, but focuses on more introspective and subdued offerings from Johnson's repertoire. Will has alternated between the two projects, while also releasing solo albums. Centro-matic released _Distance and Clime_ on Idol in 2001 and _Love You Just the Same_ on [Misra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misra_Records "Misra Records") in 2003. South San Gabriel released _Welcome, Convalescense_ and _The Carlton Chronicles_ in 2003 and 2005, respectively. Johnson's solo albums, _Murder of Tides_ and _Vultures Await_, appeared in 2002 and 2004. Centro-matic followed up _Love You Just the Same_ with _Fort Recovery_ in March 2006. The two-disc set _Dual Hawks_ was released in 2008, with one disc featuring the songs of Centro-matic and the other featuring South San Gabriel.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic#cite_note-2)

Johnson was recently introduced as an "official member" of the band [Monsters of Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk "Monsters of Folk"),[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic#cite_note-3) also consisting of [Conor Oberst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Oberst "Conor Oberst"), [Jim James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_James "Jim James"), and [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward"), on an episode of _[Austin City Limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_City_Limits "Austin City Limits")_ the group performed on. On the taping, and on tour, Johnson serves as the band's drummer, provides backup vocals, as well as performing some material of his own.

Johnson was also a member of the group [The Undertow Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Undertow_Orchestra "The Undertow Orchestra"), featuring [David Bazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bazan "David Bazan"), [Mark Eitzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Eitzel "Mark Eitzel") and the late [Vic Chesnutt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Chesnutt "Vic Chesnutt").
[https://web.archive.org/web/20140925033455/http://www.centro-matic.com/2014/december-tour-dates/](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925033455/http://www.centro-matic.com/2014/december-tour-dates/)
Johnson and Scott Danbom have performed as part of [Drive-By Truckers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive-By_Truckers "Drive-By Truckers") frontman Patterson Hood's band, during his solo tours.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic#cite_note-4)

In September, 2014 the band announced that it would cease performing following their December, 2014 tour.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Matic#cite_note-5) They played their final show at Dan's Silverleaf in Denton, Texas on December 21, 2014.

Centro-matic reunited on January 18, 2025, in Mexico City, Mexico, at Lunario Auditorio Nacional. The band opened for Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit. Jason played guitar on several songs with Centro-matic as did Sadler Vaden.
Will Johnson returned to the stage as part of the 400 Unit where he played drums, guitar, and wail on the gong.
