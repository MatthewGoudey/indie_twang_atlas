_**Let Me Go, Let Me Go, Let Me Go**_ is the second full-length solo album by [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina"), released under his own name.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let_Me_Go,_Let_Me_Go,_Let_Me_Go#cite_note-6) It was recorded in [Bloomington, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana "Bloomington, Indiana"), at The Projects by [Jim Zespy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Zespy?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jim Zespy (page does not exist)") during July 2004. The record was released on August 22, 2006, on [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian") Records.
