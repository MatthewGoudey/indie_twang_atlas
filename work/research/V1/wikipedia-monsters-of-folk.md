_**Monsters of Folk**_ is the first and only [studio album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_album "Studio album") by American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") [supergroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergroup_(music) "Supergroup (music)") [Monsters of Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk "Monsters of Folk"), a group consisting of the artists [Jim James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_James "Jim James"), [Conor Oberst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Oberst "Conor Oberst"), [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward"), and [Mike Mogis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Mogis "Mike Mogis").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk_(album)#cite_note-avannouncement-3) Originally slated for release in 2010,[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk_(album)#cite_note-4) the album was released through [Shangri-La Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangri-La_Music "Shangri-La Music") and [Rough Trade Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Trade_Records "Rough Trade Records") on September 22, 2009.

Professional ratings

### Critical

The album, whose title was a play on the concert series " [Monsters of Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Rock "Monsters of Rock")", was well received by critics. Music magazines _[Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_(magazine) "Q (magazine)")_, _[Mojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojo_(magazine) "Mojo (magazine)")_, and _[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")_ each awarded it four stars out of five. [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") newspapers _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ and _[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times "The Times")_ also gave the album a four-out-of-five rating, the latter stating that "this supergroup really is super."[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk_(album)#cite_note-13) _[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today "USA Today")_ called the album a "harmonious and occasionally electrified blend of folk-rock, country and white soul."[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk_(album)#cite_note-14)

However, _[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")_ magazine gave the album two-and-a-half out of four stars, saying that "There's so much talent in this supergroup—M. Ward, [My Morning Jacket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Morning_Jacket "My Morning Jacket")'s Jim James, and [Bright Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Eyes_(band) "Bright Eyes (band)")' Conor Oberst and Mike Mogis—that you kind of expect more from them", but, "Still, dreamy tunes like the soulful 'Dear God (Sincerely M.O.F.)' will help tame the beast within".[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters_of_Folk_(album)#cite_note-15)

### Commercial

_Monsters of Folk_ peaked at number 15 on the [_Billboard_ 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"), and charted in the Top 10 of five other _Billboard_ charts.
