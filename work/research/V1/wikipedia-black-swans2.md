**The Black Swans** are an American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band. Jerry DeCicca ( [vocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singing "Singing") and [acoustic guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitarist "Guitarist")) and Noel Sayre ( [viola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola "Viola") and [violin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violin "Violin")) form the core of the band. Their debut, _[Who Will Walk in the Darkness with You?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who_Will_Walk_in_the_Darkness_with_You%3F "Who Will Walk in the Darkness with You?")_ features Milan Karcic ( [electric guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_guitar "Electric guitar")), Matt Surgeson ( [electric bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar "Bass guitar"), [double bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_bass "Double bass")), Joe Peppercorn ( [piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano "Piano")), and Jovan Karcic ( [drums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_kit "Drum kit")). The _Sex Brain_ EP features Canaan Faulkner ( [bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar "Bass guitar")), Chris Forbes (electric guitar), Keith Hanlon (drums, electric guitar), Sarah Jurcyk (backing vocals), and Horace Roscoe ( [alto saxophone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alto_saxophone "Alto saxophone")). Amy Alwood and Cassie Lewis are also listed as group members. The Black Swans have toured America with such notable indie artists as [Okkervil River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okkervil_River_(band) "Okkervil River (band)"), [Magnolia Electric Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs:_Ohia "Songs: Ohia") and [Early Day Miners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Day_Miners "Early Day Miners"). Their 2nd full-length album, _[Change!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change! "Change!")_, was released on November 6, 2007, by [La Société Expéditionnaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_Exp%C3%A9ditionnaire "La Société Expéditionnaire") on CD and LP. It features the same core band of DeCicca, Sayre, Faulkner, Forbes and Hanlon that appeared on _Sex Brain_, as well as appearances by Peppercorn on [piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano "Piano") and Amy Alwood on [backing vocals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backing_vocalist "Backing vocalist"). It was recorded in Forbes' living room by Hanlon and mixed by Hanlon and DeCicca at Gidget Sound in Columbus, Ohio.

Founding member Noel Sayre died of an apparent heart attack on July 3, 2008, aged 37. He had been on life-support since July 1 after almost drowning in a community pool in Wheelersburg, Ohio.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Swans#cite_note-1)

In 2010, The Black Swans regrouped to release a limited edition album entitled _Words Are Stupid_, a concept record about the fallibility of human communication, for [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian")'s St. Ives imprint.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Swans#cite_note-2) In September 2010, they released a split 45" single with [Bonnie 'Prince' Billy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnie_'Prince'_Billy "Bonnie 'Prince' Billy") dedicated to the late singer-songwriter [Larry Jon Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Jon_Wilson "Larry Jon Wilson") with each band contributing a cover of one of his songs.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Swans#cite_note-3)

The Black Swans released their fourth full-length, _Don't Blame the Stars_, on [Misra Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misra_Records "Misra Records") in April 2011. It is a concept album about placing faith in friendships, music, and oneself. It includes spoken word introductions, as it is modeled after [Willie Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Nelson "Willie Nelson")'s album _[Yesterday's Wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yesterday's_Wine "Yesterday's Wine")_. _Don't Blame the Stars_ was recorded months before Noel Sayre's death and includes his final recordings.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Swans#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Swans#cite_note-5)

There is another Black Swans band, more in the performance art genre, that has been performing since 1985 in San Francisco.
