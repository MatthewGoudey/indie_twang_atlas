**Giant Sand** (formerly **Giant Sandworms**) is an American musical group from [Tucson, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona "Tucson, Arizona").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-LarkinGE-1) Its most constant member is singer-songwriter [Howe Gelb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb "Howe Gelb").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-LarkinGE-1) The groups have developed idiosyncratic sound rooted in [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country"), but touching on a wide range of other styles and featuring Gelb's [beatnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatnik "Beatnik")-influenced vocals and songwriting.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-2) Since about 2012, they have also performed as **Giant Giant Sand** when featuring a larger ensemble than their traditional four to six musicians.

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Giant_Sand&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

Howe Gelb, also a prolific solo artist, started the group as Giant Sandworms in the late 1970s in [Tucson, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona "Tucson, Arizona"). In 1980, an EP was released entitled _Will Wallow and Roam After the Ruin._ After which Gelb sacked the other members.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-LarkinGE-1) 1983 saw the release of _Valley of Rain_ on [Enigma Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enigma_Records "Enigma Records") with the shortened name of Giant Sand. It had Scott Garber on bass, Winston Watson on drums for most tracks, with Tommy Larkins drumming on the others.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-3) By 1990, John Convertino had become the band's drummer. Multi-instrumentalist Joey Burns joined around that time. Convertino and Burns formed [Calexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calexico_(band) "Calexico (band)") in 1996, later leaving the group to concentrate on that project. In 2004, saw the first release without Convertino and Burns; the _Is All Over the Map_ album.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-:0-4)

## Members

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Giant_Sand&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Members")\]

Members have included keyboardist Chris Cacavas (of [Green on Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_on_Red "Green on Red")),[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-GS-NoDepression-2016-5) bassist Paula Jean Brown (who was briefly a member of [The Go-Go's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Go-Go's "The Go-Go's") and was married to Gelb at the time),[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-6) Mark Walton (of [The Dream Syndicate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dream_Syndicate "The Dream Syndicate") and [Continental Drifters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Drifters "Continental Drifters")), drummer Tom Larkins (later to become a [Jonathan Richman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Richman "Jonathan Richman") sideman[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-GS-HighRoadTouring-7)) who rejoined Giant Sand in 2019[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-8) (without quitting Richman's band), and [Iain Shedden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iain_Shedden "Iain Shedden"), drummer with Australian band [The Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Saints_(Australian_band) "The Saints (Australian band)"). For a long while the band's [rhythm section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_section "Rhythm section") consisted of [John Convertino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Convertino "John Convertino") and [Joey Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Burns "Joey Burns"). In the early 2000s Howe Gelb reinvented the band again – this time with players from Denmark.

Guest artists over the last three decades have included [Victoria Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Williams "Victoria Williams"), [Neko Case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Case "Neko Case"), [Juliana Hatfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliana_Hatfield "Juliana Hatfield"), [PJ Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PJ_Harvey "PJ Harvey"), [Vic Chesnutt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Chesnutt "Vic Chesnutt"), [Steve Wynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Wynn_(songwriter) "Steve Wynn (songwriter)"), [Vicki Peterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicki_Peterson "Vicki Peterson"), [Rainer Ptacek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainer_Ptacek "Rainer Ptacek"), [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward"), [Isobel Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobel_Campbell "Isobel Campbell"), [Ilse DeLange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilse_DeLange "Ilse DeLange") of [The Common Linnets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Common_Linnets "The Common Linnets"), nearly all members of the band [Poi Dog Pondering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poi_Dog_Pondering "Poi Dog Pondering"), and Indiosa Patsy Jean (Gelb and Brown's daughter).[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand#cite_note-9)
