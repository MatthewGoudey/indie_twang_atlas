Musical artist

**Cass McCombs** (born November 13, 1977) is an American singer-songwriter.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-5)

## Career

McCombs has played in numerous bands in the [Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_Area "Bay Area") during the 1990s before relocating to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"). He released his debut EP _[Not the Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_the_Way "Not the Way") E.P._ (2002), debut album _[A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_(Cass_McCombs_album) "A (Cass McCombs album)")_ (2003), and follow-up _[PREfection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PREfection "PREfection")_ (2005) via Monitor Records and [4AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4AD "4AD").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-rcrda-6) In 2007, McCombs signed to [Domino Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino_Recording_Company "Domino Recording Company") and released _[Dropping the Writ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropping_the_Writ "Dropping the Writ")_ (2007). Domino Records released his following four records including _[Catacombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catacombs_(album) "Catacombs (album)")_ (2009), which was voted one of the "50 Top Albums on the Year" by _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Media "Pitchfork Media")_.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-pitchforkstaff-7) It was followed by _[Wit's End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wit's_End "Wit's End")_ (2011),[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-dominwithsend-8) _[Humor Risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humor_Risk_(album) "Humor Risk (album)")_ (2011),[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-dominotwo-9) and _Big Wheel and Others_ (2013).[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-centera-10) He toured with [John Cale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cale "John Cale") in 2012.

His single "Bradley Manning" premiered on the _[Democracy Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy_Now "Democracy Now") News Hour_ in 2012.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-centera-10) His songs have been featured in films including the surf film _The Present_ (2009),[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-jettygirl-11) and Ralph Arlyck documentary _[Following Sean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Following_Sean "Following Sean")_,[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-pbsfo-12) as well as notable skate videos featuring [Jason Dill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Dill "Jason Dill"),[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-dilljason-13) [Jerry Hsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Hsu "Jerry Hsu"),[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-hsu-14) Chima Ferguson and [Dylan Rieder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Rieder "Dylan Rieder").[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-duland-15)

In 2014, he did a co-headlining tour with the [Meat Puppets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat_Puppets "Meat Puppets").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-16) In June 2016, McCombs performed at the [Primavera Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primavera_Sound "Primavera Sound") and Field Day music festivals.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-17)

McCombs [ANTI- Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-_(record_label) "Anti- (record label)") debut, _Mangy Love_ was released on August 26, 2016,[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-18) which NME referred to as "McCombs' richest ever recording."[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-19) On February 8, 2019 he released _[Tip of the Sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tip_of_the_Sphere "Tip of the Sphere")_ on [ANTI- Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-_(record_label) "Anti- (record label)").[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-20) In 2020, Spurl Editions published McCombs' debut poetry collection _Toy Fabels_.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-21) His tenth studio album, _[Heartmind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heartmind_(album) "Heartmind (album)")_, was released on [ANTI- Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-_(record_label) "Anti- (record label)") on August 19, 2022.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-22)

In 2023, McCombs and Mr Greg, a [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco") school teacher, released the album _Mr Greg & Cass McCombs Sing and Play New Folk Songs for Children_ on [Smithsonian Folkways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithsonian_Folkways "Smithsonian Folkways").[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs#cite_note-23)
