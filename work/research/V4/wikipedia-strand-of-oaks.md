Musical artist

**Strand of Oaks** is the rock project by songwriter and producer **Timothy Showalter** (born July 20, 1982). Originally from [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana"), he currently resides in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"). His music has been classified as [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") and [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music"), as well as [folk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_rock "Folk rock").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2)

Showalter has released eight studio albums— _Leave Ruin_ (2009), _Pope Killdragon_ (2010), _Dark Shores_ (2012), _Heal_ (2014), _Hard Love_ (2017), _Eraserland_ (2019), _In Heaven_ (2021) and _Miracle Focus_ (2024).[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-pitchfork.com-3)

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Showalter was born in [Goshen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goshen,_Indiana "Goshen, Indiana"), [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana"). In middle school, he took an interest in [organized sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport "Sport") and wanted to become a [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball "Basketball") player. Showalter's athletic career ended early because he developed [juvenile rheumatoid arthritis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juvenile_idiopathic_arthritis "Juvenile idiopathic arthritis"); he took an interest in music and began to pursue a career in teaching instead.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4) After high school, Showalter moved from Indiana to Pennsylvania.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")\]

While Showalter was on tour, his wife had an affair. He moved back to [Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilkes-Barre,_Pennsylvania "Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania"), in 2003. Months later, he came home to find his house burned down.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2) Showalter spent his nights in hotels and on park benches with a borrowed guitar while working at an orthodox Jewish day school. During this time, he continued writing music that would later be released.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2)

### _Leave Ruin_ (2009)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Leave Ruin (2009)")\]

Showalter's first album _Leave Ruin_ was released in 2009 on the Pennsylvania label [La Société Expéditionnaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_Exp%C3%A9ditionnaire "La Société Expéditionnaire")[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2) while touring and collaborating with Lou Rogai ( [Lewis & Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_&_Clarke_(music) "Lewis & Clarke (music)")).[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-5)

### _Pope Killdragon_ (2010)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Pope Killdragon (2010)")\]

_Pope Killdragon_ is a self-released album by Showalter. The album was produced by audio engineer, Ben Vehorn. While holding onto Strand of Oaks' folky sound, Vehorn added in synthesizers and effects pedals to create an album with a sound that was unexplored territory for Showalter.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Oliphint-2) In between tours, Showalter wrote music for what would become _Dark Shores_. He recorded this album with [John Vanderslice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Vanderslice "John Vanderslice") who produced the songs.

_Pope Killdragon_ marked a turn in lyrical and musical style for Strand of Oaks. Its music featured strong [synthpop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthpop "Synthpop") influences, with Showalter explicitly citing [Kate Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Bush "Kate Bush") and [Tangerine Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangerine_Dream "Tangerine Dream") as influences on the sound of the album.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4) Lyrically, the album marked a turn to surrealism and fantasy with fewer [autobiographical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autobiography "Autobiography") elements than _Leave Ruin_.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Pitchfork_Killdragon-6)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4)

Despite receiving largely positive reviews, including an 8.1 from _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_,[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Pitchfork_Killdragon-6) and a B on _[Consequence of Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consequence_of_Sound "Consequence of Sound")_,[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-7) the album sold poorly and received little attention before the release of _Heal_.

### _Dark Shores_ (2012)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Dark Shores (2012)")\]

_Dark Shores_ is the second album self-released by Showalter. Stylistically the album represents a turn to a more straightforward [rock style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music"); it is the first Strand of Oaks album recorded entirely with a full band.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4) Showalter cited [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young "Neil Young") as an influence on the style of the album.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4) Lyrically, the album continues themes from _Pope Killdragon_, with some songs on _Dark Shores_ being direct sequels to songs on _Killdragon_.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-Am._Songwriter-4)

### _Heal_ (2014)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Heal (2014)")\]

Once Showalter returned from tour, he got to work recording about 30 demos in three weeks. He wrote and recorded songs in his home studio which led to the album, _Heal_. This was the first album Showalter released on Dead Oceans.

On Christmas Day in 2013, Showalter and his wife Sue were driving back to Philadelphia from Indiana when they hit a patch of ice and crashed into two semi-trucks. Showalter suffered a concussion and broke every rib on his right side. The near-death experience gave Showalter a boldness during mixing sessions while creating _Heal_ with [John Congleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Congleton "John Congleton"), just days after the crash.

_Heal_ received much acclaim, being named one of the best albums released in 2014. Showalter has toured extensively in support of the album and opened for [Jason Isbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Isbell "Jason Isbell"), Iron and Wine, and Ryan Adams. Strand of Oaks made their television debut in 2014 appearing on _Late Night with Seth Meyers_.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-8)

### _Hard Love_ (2017)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Hard Love (2017)")\]

On December 1, 2016, Timothy Showalter announced the fifth album from Strand of Oaks called _Hard Love_, which was released on February 17, 2017. He also released the first single off the album called "Radio Kids".[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-9) Strand of Oaks appeared on _Last Call with Carson Daly_, live at the Troubadour in L.A., first broadcast May 17, 2017.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-10)

_Hard Love_ received mixed to positive reviews from critics after its release. [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") assigned it a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean") of 65, indicating "generally favorable reviews".[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-11) In _Pitchfork_, [Stephen M. Deusner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Deusner "Stephen Deusner") wrote that the album "never really adds up to a particularly clear or bold statement," ultimately assigning it a score of 6.5 out of 10.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-12) In a more positive review for _[The Line of Best Fit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Line_of_Best_Fit "The Line of Best Fit")_, Erik Thompson gave _Hard Love_ a 9 out of 10 and praised its optimistic tone.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-13)

On January 19, 2018, Showalter released a follow-up to _Hard Love_, _Harder Love_, through [Dead Oceans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Oceans "Dead Oceans"). The project was announced in December 2017, and includes demos, B-sides, and alternative renditions of songs from _Hard Love_.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-14)

### _Eraserland_ (2019)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Eraserland (2019)")\]

On March 22, 2019, Strand of Oaks released their sixth album, _Eraserland_ via Dead Oceans. The lead single off the album, "Weird Ways", was released January 15, 2019.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-15)

On February 19, 2019, Strand of Oaks released a second single from the album. Called " _Ruby_", Showalter called it the "happiest song I've ever written."[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-16)

To promote the release of _Eraserland_, Showalter (with Jason Isbell, Amanda Shires, My Morning Jacket members Bo Koster and Tom Blankenship, and Will Johnson) appeared on _[The Late Show with Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Show_with_Stephen_Colbert "The Late Show with Stephen Colbert")_ and performed "Ruby".[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-17)

### _In Heaven_ (2021)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Strand_of_Oaks&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: In Heaven (2021)")\]

On October 1, 2021, Strand of Oaks released their eighth album _In Heaven_.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_of_Oaks#cite_note-pitchfork.com-3) It was preceded by the single "Galacticana". The record includes contributions from [the Smashing Pumpkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Smashing_Pumpkins "The Smashing Pumpkins")' [James Iha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Iha "James Iha") on the song "Easter".
