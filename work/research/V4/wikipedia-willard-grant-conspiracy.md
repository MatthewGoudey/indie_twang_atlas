**Willard Grant Conspiracy** ( **WGC**) was an [alt-country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt-country "Alt-country") band based originally in [Boston, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts "Boston, Massachusetts") and later near [Palmdale, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmdale,_California "Palmdale, California"), U.S. The band operated as a collective, with vocalist and songwriter Robert Fisher the only permanent member throughout its existence. Fisher's voice and [Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_(music) "Americana (music)") style have been compared to both [John Cale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cale "John Cale") and [Johnny Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Cash "Johnny Cash"), with most songs being [acoustic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_music "Acoustic music").

## History

The band was originally formed in Boston in 1995, by [North Hollywood, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hollywood,_California "North Hollywood, California")–raised Robert Fisher (born Robert Neil Fisher, September 9, 1957–February 12, 2017), and Paul Austin.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-clarkson-1) The two had previously collaborated since the early 1980s in the band Laughing Academy, first in Portland, Maine then in Boston, with various collaborators.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-3) The band was briefly renamed the Flower Tamers before dissolving. Fisher and Austin added guitarist Sean O'Brien to record their debut album, _3 AM Sunday at Fortune Otto's_, issued on Fisher's own Dahlia label. For their 1998 album, _[Flying Low](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flying_Low "Flying Low")_, released on [Rykodisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rykodisc "Rykodisc"), the trio added James Apt (guitar), David Michael Curry (viola), Erich Groat (mandolin) and Matt Griffin (bass).[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-4)

Over subsequent years, over 30 other musicians occasionally contributed to the band, both in the studio and during live performances.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-uncut-5) WGC was signed to the independent label [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music"), having previously released on Rykodisc, [Glitterhouse Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glitterhouse_Records "Glitterhouse Records") and Kimchee Records. Later albums included the live recording _Weevils in the Captain's Biscuit_, and _Mojave_. The band's 2003 release _[Regard The End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regard_The_End "Regard The End")_, featuring [Kristin Hersh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Hersh "Kristin Hersh") as well as Chris Eckman of [The Walkabouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walkabouts "The Walkabouts"), and recorded in [Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ljubljana "Ljubljana"), [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia "Slovenia"), received critical acclaim with _[Uncut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncut_(magazine) "Uncut (magazine)")_ magazine naming it Album of the Month. Their albums have been described as "experimenting with country and psychedelic sounds and the blues and punk, while... _Regard the End_, which many fans and critics see as their masterpiece, upgraded several traditional songs and merged them with a rock sound."[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-clarkson-1)

In 2002, Paul Austin started to play in [The Transmissionary Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Transmissionary_Six "The Transmissionary Six") with Terri Moeller of [The Walkabouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walkabouts "The Walkabouts"), and in 2011 he became a member of The Walkabouts. In 2003, Fisher returned to live in his home state of California.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-clarkson-1) Willard Grant Conspiracy toured extensively in 2005 and 2006, visiting 23 countries, including a showcase at the [South by Southwest music festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest "South by Southwest"). They continued to tour in the US and occasionally in Europe and the UK until 2016. Fisher and Curry also toured together as a duo,[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-6) and Fisher performed as a solo artist and with other musicians including Scottish singer-songwriter [Jackie Leven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Leven "Jackie Leven") and arranger and composer Malcolm Lindsay.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-clarkson-1)

Robert Fisher died of cancer on February 12, 2017, aged 59.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-uncut-5)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willard_Grant_Conspiracy#cite_note-7)
