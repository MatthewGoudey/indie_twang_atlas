_**I'm Wide Awake, It's Morning**_ is the sixth studio album by American band [Bright Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Eyes_(band) "Bright Eyes (band)"), released on January 25, 2005, by [Saddle Creek Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_Creek_Records "Saddle Creek Records") (the same day as their seventh album, _[Digital Ash in a Digital Urn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Ash_in_a_Digital_Urn "Digital Ash in a Digital Urn")_).

The album was reissued by [Dead Oceans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Oceans "Dead Oceans") alongside a six-track companion EP on November 11, 2022.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-5)

## Songs

The [music video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_video "Music video") for " [First Day of My Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Day_of_My_Life_(Bright_Eyes_song) "First Day of My Life (Bright Eyes song)")" was directed by [John Cameron Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cameron_Mitchell "John Cameron Mitchell").

This was the first Bright Eyes album to feature [Nate Walcott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nate_Walcott "Nate Walcott"), who is now a permanent member of the band.

"Road to Joy" contains an [interpolation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpolation_(popular_music) "Interpolation (popular music)") of [Beethoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven "Ludwig van Beethoven")'s " [Ode to Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ode_to_Joy "Ode to Joy")". The title of the album is taken from a lyric in this song.

Bright Eyes achieved success on the US charts when the singles "Lua" and " [Take It Easy (Love Nothing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_It_Easy_(Love_Nothing) "Take It Easy (Love Nothing)")" (the latter from _Digital Ash_) took the top two positions on the _Billboard_ Hot Singles Sales chart in 2004. In 2005, the band set off on a two-part world tour to promote the album along with _Digital Ash in a Digital Urn_, with the first half of the tour centring on the folk-influenced first album, and the latter half featuring the more electronic second album. Both records made it into the top 20 of the _Billboard_ albums charts, with _I'm Wide Awake, It's Morning_ peaking at number 10 on the [_Billboard_ 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") and at number 2 on the _Billboard_ [Independent Albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Albums "Independent Albums") chart.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-6) The tour was captured on the album _[Motion Sickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Sickness_(album) "Motion Sickness (album)")_, released later in the year.

Professional ratings

_I'm Wide Awake, It's Morning_ received widespread acclaim from music critics. At [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), the album received an average score of 85 out of 100, based on 33 reviews, which indicates "universal acclaim".[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-metacritic-7) _[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")_ describes it as "An album with the simmering glow of a masterpiece."[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-lat-11) _[Drowned in Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drowned_in_Sound "Drowned in Sound")_ critic Sean Adams called the album a "thing of awe", praising the lyrics and "calculated attention to detail".[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-dis-18) _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_'s Chris Dahlen gave the album 8.7 out of 10 and states " _I'm Wide Awake_ weaves the personal and the political more fluidly than most singers even care to try, and the consummate tunefulness just strengthens those moments where he pinches a nerve."[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-pitchfork-13)

In a less positive review, [Stephen Thomas Erlewine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Thomas_Erlewine "Stephen Thomas Erlewine") of [AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic") criticized Oberst's "heavy-handed pretension in the words and \[...\] affectedness in his delivery", calling the album proof that "instead of reaching musical maturity, he's wallowing in a perpetual adolescence."[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-allmusic-8)

Up to 2014, the album had sold 522,000 copies in US.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-19) In 2007 it was awarded a gold certification from the [Independent Music Companies Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Music_Companies_Association "Independent Music Companies Association"), which indicated sales of at least 100,000 copies throughout Europe.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Wide_Awake,_It's_Morning#cite_note-20)
