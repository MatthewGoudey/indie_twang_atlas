**Megafaun** is an American [psychedelic folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_folk "Psychedelic folk") band based in [Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham,_North_Carolina "Durham, North Carolina"), [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina "North Carolina").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-AAS-1)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Megafaun&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

Brothers Brad Cook (bass, guitar) and [Phil Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Cook_(musician) "Phil Cook (musician)") (keyboards) are from [Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chippewa_Falls,_Wisconsin "Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin"), and Joe Westerlund (percussion) is from [Eau Claire, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eau_Claire,_Wisconsin "Eau Claire, Wisconsin"). They first met at the [H.O.R.D.E.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.O.R.D.E. "H.O.R.D.E.") festival in 1997, and played together in various combinations, including in [DeYarmond Edison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeYarmond_Edison "DeYarmond Edison") with [Justin Vernon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Vernon "Justin Vernon").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Jarnow-2) After that band broke up in 2006, Vernon went on to record a solo project _[For Emma, Forever Ago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_Emma,_Forever_Ago "For Emma, Forever Ago")_ as [Bon Iver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Iver "Bon Iver"), and Westerlund and Brad and Phil Cook formed Megafaun.

Megafaun's first release was _Bury the Square_ in 2008, on which the band were described as "\[doing\] away with Edison's polite experimentation in favor of full-bore concrète music, harsh noise salvos, and wild free-jazz interventions".[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Howe-3) The group's second album, _Gather, Form & Fly_, was released in 2009 on the Hometapes label. It was described by [Pitchfork Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Media "Pitchfork Media") as "a headily absorbing, occasionally unsettling listen",[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Deusner-4) and by [Drowned in Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drowned_in_Sound "Drowned in Sound") as "a tapestry of sound collages and a freedom with form which creates a richly textured whole".[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Perry-5) A third album, _Heretofore_, was released in 2010.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Thurston-6) The self-titled _Megafaun_ was released in 2011.

Producer [Nick Sanborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Sanborn "Nick Sanborn") played bass with Megafaun.

Megafaun has toured with [The Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats "The Mountain Goats"), [Bowerbirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowerbirds_(band) "Bowerbirds (band)"),[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-Jarnow-2) [Akron/Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akron/Family "Akron/Family"), and in April 2012, The Drive-by Truckers, Damien Jurado (August 2012), among others.

The band released its first music video, produced by the art collective Sirocco Research Labs, to promote the single "Carolina Days" on [Pitchfork.tv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork.tv "Pitchfork.tv") during the spring of 2011.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-7)

They played with [Arnold Dreyblatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Dreyblatt "Arnold Dreyblatt") at the third annual [Hopscotch Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopscotch_Music_Festival "Hopscotch Music Festival") in [Raleigh, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raleigh,_North_Carolina "Raleigh, North Carolina"), which took place September 6–8, 2012.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-8)

In October 2012, Megafaun announced on their website that they were going on indefinite hiatus "to re-tool the shed, so to speak."[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafaun#cite_note-9)
