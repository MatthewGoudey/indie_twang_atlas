_**Feast of Wire**_ is the fourth studio album by American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band [Calexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calexico_(band) "Calexico (band)"). The album was released on February 18, 2003, through [Quarterstick Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterstick_Records "Quarterstick Records").

Professional ratings

Joe Tangari of _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_ called _Feast of Wire_ Calexico's "first genuinely masterful full-length, crammed with immediate songcraft, shifting moods and open-ended exploration," and "the album we always knew they had in them but feared they would never make."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_Wire#cite_note-pitchfork-10)

## Personnel

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Feast_of_Wire&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Personnel")\]

Credits adapted from [CD Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CD_Universe "CD Universe").[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_Wire#cite_note-14)

Calexico

- Joey Burns – guitar, upright bass, accordion, percussion, cuatro, cello, orchestra bells, pump organ, mandolin, bowed banjo, vibes, synthesizer, melodica, vocals
- John Convertino – drums, percussion, piano (track 9)
- Paul Niehaus – pedal steel
- Jacob Valenzuela – trumpet (tracks 2, 7, 10, 11, 15)
- Martin Wenk – accordion (tracks 4, 7), trumpet (tracks 7, 11), bowed vibes (track 16)
- Volker Zander – upright bass (tracks 4, 16)

Additional personnel

- Ed Kay – flute (track 15)
- Eddie Lopez – button accordion (track 11)
- Nick Luca – synthesizer (tracks 2, 5, 10), piano (tracks 4, 15), vibes (track 10), electric guitar (track 15)
- Jeff "Fruitpie" Marchant – trombone (track 15)
- [Craig Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Schumacher "Craig Schumacher") – synthesizer (tracks 2, 16), tympani (track 7), backup vocals (tracks 1, 2), trumpet (track 2)
- Fernando Valencia – violin (track 11)
- Joseph Valenzuela – trombone (track 2)
