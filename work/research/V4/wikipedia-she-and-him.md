**She & Him** is an American musical duo consisting of [Zooey Deschanel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zooey_Deschanel "Zooey Deschanel") (vocals, piano, ukulele[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-2)) and [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward") (guitar, production). It was formed in 2008 in [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-rollingstone-3) Their first album, _[Volume One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume_One_(She_&_Him_album) "Volume One (She & Him album)")_, was released on the independent label [Merge Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_Records "Merge Records") on March 18, 2008.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-pitchfork-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-billboard-5) They are commonly associated with the indie-folk resurgence of the mid-2000s, which music critic and professor Eric Harvey described in _[Stereogum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereogum "Stereogum")_ as "childlike, whimsical, earnest, acoustic [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks")-friendly music like [Feist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feist_(singer) "Feist (singer)"), [the Decemberists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Decemberists "The Decemberists"), [Regina Spektor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_Spektor "Regina Spektor"), and [Jose Gonzalez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Gonz%C3%A1lez_(singer) "José González (singer)")."[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-6)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

### Formation

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Formation")\]

Deschanel and Ward met on the set of the film _[The Go-Getter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Go-Getter_(2007_film) "The Go-Getter (2007 film)"),_ in which Deschanel had a starring role. [Martin Hynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Hynes "Martin Hynes"), the director, introduced them to each other and asked them to sing a duet for the film's end credits. They performed the song "When I Get to the Border" by [Richard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Thompson_(musician) "Richard Thompson (musician)") and [Linda Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Thompson_(singer) "Linda Thompson (singer)").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-7) The two bonded over a shared interest in albums produced by [George Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Martin "George Martin") and [Phil Spector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Spector "Phil Spector"), as well as certain [Ralph Peer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Peer "Ralph Peer") groups such as the [Carter Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Family "Carter Family").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-Costello_interview-8)

Ward heard Deschanel sing in the film _[Elf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_(film) "Elf (film)")_. He was surprised to learn that, although she wrote songs, she had not pursued a career in music. Deschanel commented in an interview with _[Venus Zine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_Zine "Venus Zine")_: "I always thought I would be able to sing and act; but, at a certain point, it became difficult for me to share the music part. So I was writing a ton of music, but not really doing anything with it. I didn't really know exactly who to collaborate with until I met Matt, and everything seemed to fall into place.”[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-9) Deschanel had done years' worth of home demos, but she was shy about becoming a big-name celebrity.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-NYT-10) On a whim, she sent them to Ward.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-Costello_interview-8) Ward called her a short time later telling her that he would like to record her songs properly. Deschanel said in an interview: "I always have loved music, ever since I was really little. I just loved to sing."[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-11)

### 2006–2008: Collaboration and _Volume One_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2006–2008: Collaboration and Volume One")\]

For _[Volume One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume_One_(She_&_Him_album) "Volume One (She & Him album)")_, the pair collaborated via e-mail, with Ward working in his studio in Portland, Oregon, and Deschanel in her home in Los Angeles, California. Los Angeles influenced Deschanel's songwriting, evoking long drives along the coast while listening to [Brian Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Wilson "Brian Wilson"), golden oldies, and jam music on 1970s AM radio.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-Costello_interview-8)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-NYT-10) The California music scene influenced Ward's production of the album and inspired his guitar work with [alt-country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt-country "Alt-country") singers like [Jenny Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Lewis "Jenny Lewis").

Ward and Deschanel recorded in Portland, Oregon, in December 2006 and February to March 2007. _Volume One_ was completed in three sessions. They also recorded in [Mike Coykendall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Coykendall "Mike Coykendall")'s and Adam Selzer's studios and had a mixing session with [Mike Mogis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Mogis "Mike Mogis") in his Omaha, Nebraska studio in either April or May 2007. Deschanel wrote all the original songs on the album except "Sweet Darlin'", which she co-wrote with [Jason Schwartzman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Schwartzman "Jason Schwartzman") (an actor known for his frequent collaborations with [Wes Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Anderson "Wes Anderson")). In addition to the ten original songs, the album features two [cover songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_(music) "Cover (music)"): "You Really Got a Hold on Me" (composed by [Smokey Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smokey_Robinson "Smokey Robinson")) and "I Should Have Known Better" (composed by [John Lennon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lennon "John Lennon") and [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney "Paul McCartney")).

They took a break after that, as Ward was on tour and Deschanel was filming a movie. _Volume One_ was released on March 18, 2008. Their first public appearance as a band was a year and two months earlier at the [Sundance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival") screening of _[The Go-Getter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Go-Getter_(2007_film) "The Go-Getter (2007 film)")_. They performed at other events and began their first tour in summer 2008. _[Paste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paste_(magazine) "Paste (magazine)")_ named She & Him's debut the No. 1 album of the year.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-12)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/29/She_%26_Him_at_NFF_2.jpg/250px-She_%26_Him_at_NFF_2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:She_&_Him_at_NFF_2.jpg) She & Him performing at the [Newport Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_Folk_Festival "Newport Folk Festival")(2 August 2008)

She & Him performed at the [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest#SXSW_2008 "South by Southwest") festival in March 2008[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-pitchfork2-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-14) and went on tour during July and August 2008. [Becky Stark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Becky_Stark "Becky Stark") of the band [Lavender Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavender_Diamond "Lavender Diamond") provided backup vocals.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-15)

### 2010: _Volume Two_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2010: Volume Two")\]

On March 17, 2010, She & Him released their second album, _[Volume Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume_Two_(She_&_Him_album) "Volume Two (She & Him album)")_.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-16) The album features eleven original songs written by Deschanel and covers of [NRBQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRBQ "NRBQ")'s 1977 single "Ridin' in My Car" and [Patience and Prudence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patience_and_Prudence "Patience and Prudence")'s "Gonna Get Along Without You Now", as well as a guest appearance from the indie pop group [Tilly and the Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilly_and_the_Wall "Tilly and the Wall") on the track "In the Sun".[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-17)

They performed concerts throughout the United States including the music festivals [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest#SXSW_2010 "South by Southwest"), [Coachella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Music_and_Arts_Festival "Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival"), [Bonnaroo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Bonnaroo_Music_Festival "2010 Bonnaroo Music Festival"), Savannah, [Sasquatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasquatch!_Music_Festival "Sasquatch! Music Festival"), [Nateva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nateva_Music_Festival "Nateva Music Festival"), [Verge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verge_music_festival "Verge music festival") and the inaugural [LouFest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LouFest "LouFest") in St. Louis, Missouri, headlining with the likes of Broken Social Scene, Jeff Tweedy and Built to Spill. They also toured Europe, visiting London, [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid "Madrid"), Barcelona, Paris, Berlin, Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo, and Amsterdam. They were chosen by [Matt Groening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Groening "Matt Groening") to perform at [All Tomorrow's Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Tomorrow's_Parties_(music_festival) "All Tomorrow's Parties (music festival)") music festival in England.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-18)

### 2011–2013: _A Very She & Him Christmas_ and _Volume 3_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: 2011–2013: A Very She & Him Christmas and Volume 3")\]

_[A Very She & Him Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Very_She_&_Him_Christmas "A Very She & Him Christmas")_ was announced on Pitchfork.com in September 2011. The twelve track Christmas album was released October 24, 2011, under Merge Records.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-19) [Amazon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com "Amazon.com") said on December 29, 2011, that the album was one of its top three best-selling MP3-albums over the Christmas period.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-20)

Deschanel confirmed on Twitter that a new album would be released in early 2013.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-21) _[Volume 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume_3_(She_&_Him_album) "Volume 3 (She & Him album)")_ was subsequently announced, with a release date of May 7, 2013 (May 13, 2013
, for Europe).[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-22) She & Him toured the US and Canada in support of _Volume 3_ in the summer of 2013.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-23) Supporting acts along the tour included [Tilly and the Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilly_and_the_Wall "Tilly and the Wall"), [Camera Obscura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_Obscura_(band) "Camera Obscura (band)"), [Emmylou Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmylou_Harris "Emmylou Harris"), [Rodney Crowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodney_Crowell "Rodney Crowell") and [The Chapin Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chapin_Sisters "The Chapin Sisters").

### 2014–present: Record label change and _Classics_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=She_%26_Him&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: 2014–present: Record label change and Classics")\]

On June 27, 2014, it was announced that She & Him had left independent label [Merge Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_Records "Merge Records") and had joined major label [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records"), with their fifth studio album, _[Classics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classics_(She_&_Him_album) "Classics (She & Him album)")_, scheduled to be released later in the year.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-24)[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-25) The next month, Deschanel and Ward were photographed with [Brian Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Wilson "Brian Wilson") in the studio recording his forthcoming album _[No Pier Pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Pier_Pressure "No Pier Pressure")_ which features Deschanel as a guest vocalist on the cha-cha-esque track "On the Island".[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-26) On September 15, 2016, She & Him announced the forthcoming release of their second holiday album, [_Christmas Party_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Party_(She_&_Him_album) "Christmas Party (She & Him album)"), in October of that year.[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-27)
On July 22, 2022, She & Him released [Melt Away: A Tribute to Brian Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melt_Away:_A_Tribute_to_Brian_Wilson "Melt Away: A Tribute to Brian Wilson").

The group's song " [I Thought I Saw Your Face Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Thought_I_Saw_Your_Face_Today "I Thought I Saw Your Face Today")" experienced a resurgence in popularity after going viral on the video-sharing app [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") in late 2025 and as a result became the duo's first song to enter the [_Billboard_ Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), debuting at number 99.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_%26_Him#cite_note-28)

In May 2026, the duo announced the "Pump Up The Volumes Tour" which they play songs from their albums Volume 1, 2 and 3 across the United States, including cities like New York and Los Angeles.
