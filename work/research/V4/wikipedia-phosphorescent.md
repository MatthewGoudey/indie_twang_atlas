Musical artist

**Phosphorescent** is the stage name of American singer-songwriter **Matthew Houck**.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-3) Originally from [Huntsville, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsville,_Alabama "Huntsville, Alabama"), Houck began recording and performing under this nickname in 2001 in [Athens, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia"). He is currently based in [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee").

## Overview

Before recording under the name Phosphorescent, Matthew Houck toured under the name Fillup Shack and self-released a limited pressing of the album _Hipolit_ in 2000.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-4) Houck later changed his stage name to Phosphorescent and released the full-length [LP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LP_record "LP record") _[A Hundred Times or More](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Hundred_Times_or_More "A Hundred Times or More")_ in 2003 through [Athens, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia")-based independent label [Warm Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warm_Records "Warm Records"). The following year, he released the [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") _[The Weight of Flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weight_of_Flight "The Weight of Flight")_. Phosphorescent rose to wider critical acclaim after releasing _[Aw Come Aw Wry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aw_Come_Aw_Wry "Aw Come Aw Wry")_ in August 2005 and _[Pride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride_(Phosphorescent_album) "Pride (Phosphorescent album)")_ in October 2007. The latter was named the 12th best album of 2007 by _[Stylus Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylus_Magazine "Stylus Magazine")_[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-5) and received an 8.0 rating from the online indie magazine _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-6) In 2009, inspired by [Willie Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Nelson "Willie Nelson")'s tribute album to [Lefty Frizzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lefty_Frizzell "Lefty Frizzell") _[To Lefty from Willie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Lefty_from_Willie "To Lefty from Willie")_, Houck crafted a tribute album to Nelson himself entitled _[To Willie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Willie "To Willie")_, released through [Dead Oceans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Oceans "Dead Oceans"). Phosphorescent released _[Here's to Taking It Easy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Here's_to_Taking_It_Easy "Here's to Taking It Easy")_ in 2010.

_[Muchacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muchacho_(album) "Muchacho (album)")_, Phosphorescent's sixth studio album, was released in 2013 to critical acclaim.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-7)[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-9) The full studio album _C'est La Vie_ followed in 2018.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-10)

In January 2024, Phosphorescent announced his new album _[Revelator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revelator_(Phosphorescent_album) "Revelator (Phosphorescent album)")_, to be released in April on [Verve Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verve_Records "Verve Records"). For the first time, it features a song written by someone other than Houck - his partner and collaborator, Jo Schornikow.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorescent_(musician)#cite_note-11)
