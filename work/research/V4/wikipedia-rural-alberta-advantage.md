**The Rural Alberta Advantage** is a Canadian [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band that formed in 2005 in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), Ontario. The band consists of Nils Edenloff on lead vocals and guitar; Amy Cole (Robin Hatch between September 2016 and January 2018) on keyboards, bass, and backing vocals; and Paul Banwatt on drums.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-1) They have released five albums and are signed to [Paper Bag Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Bag_Records "Paper Bag Records") in Canada and [Saddle Creek Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_Creek_Records "Saddle Creek Records") internationally. In 2011, they were nominated for a [Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polaris_Music_Prize "Polaris Music Prize").

## History

The Rural Alberta Advantage was formed after the band members met while organizing an open-mic event at The Winchester in [Cabbagetown.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabbagetown,_Toronto "Cabbagetown, Toronto")[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-2) The band's name was coined by Edenloff's brother, when he wrote in an e-mail to Edenloff that he was planning to explore "the rural [Alberta advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Advantage "Alberta Advantage")" by spending some time back on the family farm near [Donalda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donalda,_Alberta "Donalda, Alberta"), [Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta "Alberta") where they had spent part of their childhoods.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-prairiehome-3)

First formed in 2005, the band recorded a demo tape and released an [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") independently before completing their first full-length album, _Hometowns_, in early 2008. _Hometowns_ was recorded and produced from April 2007 to March 2008 by Roger Leavens at BoomBox Sound in Toronto, Ontario. The band toured extensively across Canada, including shows at the [Pop Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Montreal "Pop Montreal") and [Halifax Pop Explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Pop_Explosion "Halifax Pop Explosion") festivals, to support _Hometowns_.

The band was selected as [eMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMusic "EMusic")'s featured artist of the month for November 2008.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-4) The band subsequently signed to [Saddle Creek Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_Creek_Records "Saddle Creek Records") in 2009, and _Hometowns_ was re-released by the label in July.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-5) The band was also selected as the X3 Artist of the month by [Aux.tv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aux.tv "Aux.tv"), [CBC Radio 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Radio_3 "CBC Radio 3") and _[Exclaim!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclaim! "Exclaim!")_ for July 2009.

Their second LP, _Departing_, was released on March 1, 2011.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-6) The album was named as a longlisted nominee for the [2011 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Polaris_Music_Prize "2011 Polaris Music Prize"),[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-longlist-7) and [José Lourenço](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Louren%C3%A7o "José Lourenço")'s music video for "Stamp" received a [Juno Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award "Juno Award") nomination for [Video of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award_for_Video_of_the_Year "Juno Award for Video of the Year") at the [Juno Awards of 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Awards_of_2012 "Juno Awards of 2012").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-8)

Their third album, _Mended with Gold,_ was released on September 30, 2014. In the fall of 2014, _Mended with Gold_ was featured on [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR")'s _[All Songs Considered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Songs_Considered "All Songs Considered")_ list for the top albums of September.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-9)

On September 12, 2016, Amy Cole announced her departure from the group via the band's website. On September 18, the band announced a new tour with keyboard, bass pedal, and backup vocals being supplied by Robin Hatch. On November 7, the band announced that their fourth studio album was forthcoming.

Their fourth studio album, _The Wild,_ was released on October 13, 2017. Its lead track "Beacon Hill" was inspired by the 2016 [Fort McMurray wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_McMurray_wildfire "Fort McMurray wildfire").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-10)

In January 2018, the band announced that Cole had rejoined their lineup.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-11)

On March 31, 2022, their second EP _The Rise_ was released with six songs.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-12)

To build up anticipation for their 2023 tour, the single "Plague Dogs" was released on January 31, 2023.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rural_Alberta_Advantage#cite_note-13)
