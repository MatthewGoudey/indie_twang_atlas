**Shearwater** is an American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band from [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"), led by multi-instrumentalist and lead singer [Jonathan Meiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Meiburg "Jonathan Meiburg"),[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-SciAm-BirdmanBandman-2008-1) a singer-songwriter. The band's music is notable for its imagery based in nature, cerebral yet intimate melodic songs, as well as Meiburg's vocals.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-ChicagoTrib-ConcertReview-2010-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-PopMatters-AnimalJoy-2012-3)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shearwater_(band)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

### Early years

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shearwater_(band)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Early years")\]

In 1999, [Okkervil River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okkervil_River_(band) "Okkervil River (band)") band members [Jonathan Meiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Meiburg "Jonathan Meiburg") and [Will Sheff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Sheff "Will Sheff") founded Shearwater as an outlet for quieter songs on which they were collaborating. The band's name comes from the [shearwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater "Shearwater"), a tribe of seabirds related to petrels and albatrosses. Meiburg, who holds a master's degree in geography with a focus on ornithology, picked the name mostly for the sound of the word.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-10kBirds-Meiburg-2009-4)

Shearwater's debut, _[The Dissolving Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dissolving_Room "The Dissolving Room")_, introduced Kim Burke on upright bass; shortly after, drummer and vibraphonist Thor Harris joined the band. The addition of multi-instrumentalist Howard Draper plus tours and support dates with [The Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats "The Mountain Goats"), [Akron/Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akron/Family "Akron/Family") and [Blonde Redhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blonde_Redhead "Blonde Redhead") brought them exposure. Shearwater continued to produce music under this lineup in _[Everybody Makes Mistakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everybody_Makes_Mistakes_(Shearwater) "Everybody Makes Mistakes (Shearwater)")_ (2002) and _[Winged Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winged_Life "Winged Life")_ (2004), as well as the _[Thieves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thieves_(EP) "Thieves (EP)")_ [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (2005).

In May 2006, Shearwater released _[Palo Santo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Santo_(Shearwater_album) "Palo Santo (Shearwater album)")_, their fourth full-length album and last to be released on Misra Records, to much acclaim from critics and fans.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NYTimes-PaloSanto-Review-2006-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NYTimes-ShowReview-PaloSanto-2006-6) The song "Red Sea, Black Sea," was featured as an [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR") Song of the Day.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NPRMusic-SoTD-RedSea-2006-7) NPR's Stephen Thompson subsequently named _Palo Santo_ as the best album of 2006.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NPRMusic-SoTY-PaloSanto-2006-8) Although Sheff appeared as an instrumentalist for the recording of _Palo Santo_, the album's songs were composed and sung entirely by Meiburg, as the band felt that the songs that Meiburg had written were best suited for the project. Sheff then left the band to concentrate on other projects.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-AustinChronicle-PaloSanto-2006-9)

### Matador Records

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shearwater_(band)&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Matador Records")\]

Midway through the tour for _Palo Santo_, the band was notified by Misra Records that they would be unable to continue to distribute Shearwater's music. Meiburg personally emailed the owner of [Matador Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matador_Records "Matador Records"), Gerard Cosloy, who subsequently signed Shearwater to a multi-album deal. On April 10, 2007, Shearwater released a two-disc edition of _Palo Santo_ which featured five re-recorded tracks, remastered versions of the other six tracks, as well as a bonus disc of outtakes from the original sessions. Meiburg had felt that the original Palo Santo suffered from "murkiness", and could be improved upon in the studio.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-10)

Matador Records released the album _[Rook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rook_(album) "Rook (album)")_ on June 3, 2008.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-AuralStates-Rook-2008-11) On the tour that followed, Shearwater opened for Clinic and later [Coldplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldplay "Coldplay"). Additional touring accompanists were added at that time: Jordan Geiger of Hospital Ships and [Minus Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minus_Story "Minus Story") (on cornet, synthesizer, and percussion) and Kevin Schneider of Black Before Red (on keyboard, bass, and guitar) replaced Howard Draper.

In November, 2009, Shearwater was the just the ninth artist featured on NPR's [Tiny Desk Concerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiny_Desk_Concerts "Tiny Desk Concerts"), with NPR's Stephen Thompson calling it a "glorious four-song set that was at once pristine and ramshackle", referring to the featured a set of unusual instruments including "banjos and auto parts to go with Harris' homemade ' [waterphone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterphone "Waterphone")' and [hammered dulcimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammered_dulcimer "Hammered dulcimer")".[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-12)

On February 23, 2010, Shearwater released their sixth album, _[The Golden Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Golden_Archipelago "The Golden Archipelago"),_[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-AmSongwriter-GoldenArch-2010-13) which the band produced with John Congleton.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NYTimes-GoldenArchipelago-2010-14) The band toured extensively behind the record in 2010, with an international tour in the spring[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-BklynVegan-2010-15) (with [Wye Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wye_Oak_(band) "Wye Oak (band)") and Hospital Ships opening) and additional U.S. dates in the fall[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-BklynVegan-GoldArch-2010-16) (with special guest [Damien Jurado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado "Damien Jurado")). _The Golden Archipelago_ concluded the band's Island Arc trilogy, a project also encompassing _Palo Santo_ and _Rook_.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-RStone-AnimalJoy-2012-17)

On November 6, 2010, the band released an instrumental album, _Shearwater is Enron,_ via [Bandcamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandcamp "Bandcamp").[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-Bandcamp-Enron-2010-18) The album was recorded in the spring of 2010 and includes live material recorded at a performance under the pseudonym "Enron." It introduces some textures not traditionally associated with the band, such as electronic drum tracks and squalling guitar rock. Members of [Wye Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wye_Oak_(band) "Wye Oak (band)") and Hospital Ships assist on the live tracks.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-CokeMachineGlow-Enron-2010-19)

### Sub Pop Records

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shearwater_(band)&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Sub Pop Records")\]

In February 2012, a new full-length album called _[Animal Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Joy "Animal Joy")_ was released.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-NPR-FirstListen-AnimalJoy-2012-20) It was Shearwater's first record with [Sub Pop Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop_Records "Sub Pop Records").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-RStone-AnimalJoy-2012-17)[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-SubPop-100Shearwater-2013-21) The record signaled a new chapter for the band after the conclusion of the Island trilogy, as the album included more rock elements.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-Stereogum-AnimalJoy-2012-22) Following the release of the album, the band opened for the North American tour of [Sharon Van Etten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Van_Etten "Sharon Van Etten").[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-Pitchfork-EttenShear-Petty-2012-23)

In 2013, _Fellow Travelers_ was released on Sub Pop, a Shearwater album consisting entirely of cover songs by bands that Shearwater had toured with.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-WNYC-Soundcheck-FellowTrav-2014-24) Each band that was covered on the album was also invited to play on the album, under the condition that they could not play on their own song.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-UTR-FellowTravellers-2013-25)

The record _[Jet Plane and Oxbow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Plane_and_Oxbow "Jet Plane and Oxbow")_ was released on Sub Pop January 22, 2016.[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-KEXP-JetPlane-2014-26)[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-27) Frequent collaborator, producer [Danny Reisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Reisch?action=edit&redlink=1 "Danny Reisch (page does not exist)"), worked on the album, as did composer [Brian Reitzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Reitzell "Brian Reitzell"), [Wye Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wye_Oak_(band) "Wye Oak (band)")'s [Jenn Wasner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenn_Wasner "Jenn Wasner"), drummer Cully Symington, Howard Draper, and regular band members [Jesca Hoop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesca_Hoop "Jesca Hoop"), Lucas Oswald, and Abram Shook.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-Pitchfork-JetPlaneOxbowAnnounce-2015-28)[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-SubPop-JetPlaneOxBow-MichaelAzerrad-2015-29)

### Jet Plane and Oxbow tour and self-releases

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shearwater_(band)&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Jet Plane and Oxbow tour and self-releases")\]

The band toured the _Jet Plane and Oxbow_ album in 2016. The lineup for the tour was considerably different than the studio album, retaining Meiburg and Oswald but adding Emily Lee on keyboards, Sadie Powers on bass and Josh Halpern on drums. During the tour, it was customary for the band to play several covers from the [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie "David Bowie") album _[Lodger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodger_(album) "Lodger (album)")_ as an encore. On March 25, 2016, the entire album was covered at Chicago's Schuba's Tavern, followed by a subsequent recording for the [AV Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_A.V._Club "The A.V. Club") the next day.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-30) This was released as a limited edition vinyl and digital download and would eventually lead to a performance of the entire _Berlin Trilogy_ at Brookfield Place, New York for WNYC in October 2018.[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-31) The live recording was released as a limited digital download in 2019.

During the tour, Meiburg became impressed with openers Cross Record.[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearwater_(band)#cite_note-32) Meiburg and Cross Record's Emily Cross and Dan Duszynski went on to form a side-project, [Loma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loma_(band) "Loma (band)"), which has released three albums on Sub Pop: the self-titled _Loma_ (2018), _Don't Shy Away_ (2020) and _How Will I Live Without a Body?_ (2024).

In 2020, Shearwater began production of a crowdfunded studio album, but the recording was disrupted as a result of the [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_States "COVID-19 pandemic in the United States") lockdowns. In response, Meiburg enlisted Dan Duszynski to produce the ambient _Quarantine Music_ series, released digitally through Bandcamp. The new studio album, _The Great Awakening_, was released in June 2022.
