**The Felice Brothers** are an American [folk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_rock "Folk rock")/ [country rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rock "Country rock") band from New York.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Bumgardner-1)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

Originally from [Palenville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palenville,_New_York "Palenville, New York") in the [Catskill Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catskill_Mountains "Catskill Mountains"), the Felice Brothers started playing in the [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")'s [42nd Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/42nd_Street_(Manhattan) "42nd Street (Manhattan)") and [Union Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Square_(New_York_City) "Union Square (New York City)") subway stations while living in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Fleming-2)

Their 2006 self-released debut album _Through These Reins and Gone_[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Lisle-3) made Radio Woodstock [WDST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WDST "WDST")'s top 25 albums of the year. They released 2007's _[Tonight at the Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonight_at_the_Arizona "Tonight at the Arizona")_ on [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music")[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Fleming-2) then self-released _Adventures of the Felice Brothers Vol. 1._ James Felice said, "We put one record out on an English label \[Loose\], and we've released all the other stuff ourselves \[on their label New York Pro\]."[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Lisle-3) In 2007, the Felice Brothers played at [Levon Helm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levon_Helm "Levon Helm")'s Midnight Ramble in [Woodstock, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodstock,_New_York "Woodstock, New York") and toured with [Bright Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Eyes_(band) "Bright Eyes (band)").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-Barry-4)

### 2008

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: 2008")\]

On January 7, it was announced that The Felice Brothers had signed a record deal with Team Love Records in the US.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-6) They toured the United States extensively during the spring (February through mid-April) to promote their new album. The Brothers played at [Mountain Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Jam_(festival) "Mountain Jam (festival)") at [Hunter Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Mountain_(ski_area) "Hunter Mountain (ski area)") again that year. They also played at the 2008 [Clearwater Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clearwater_Festival "Clearwater Festival"), [Bonnaroo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnaroo "Bonnaroo") in [Manchester, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_Tennessee "Manchester, Tennessee"), the [Philadelphia Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Folk_Festival "Philadelphia Folk Festival") in [Schwenksville, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwenksville,_Pennsylvania "Schwenksville, Pennsylvania"), [All Points West Music and Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Points_West_Music_and_Arts_Festival "All Points West Music and Arts Festival") at [Liberty State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_State_Park "Liberty State Park") in [Jersey City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey "Jersey City, New Jersey"), and [Outside lands music and arts festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outside_lands_music_and_arts_festival "Outside lands music and arts festival") in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco"), the later two festivals being inaugural events.

In August, the band performed at the legendary [Newport Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_Folk_Festival "Newport Folk Festival"). Due to torrential rains, high winds, and lightning storms, power was cut to the entire [Newport, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Rhode_Island "Newport, Rhode Island") area. As the stage the band was performing on had no generators, the band played unplugged. They performed for over an hour with such songs as "Frankie's Gun!" and a sing-a-long to " [This Land Is Your Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Land_Is_Your_Land "This Land Is Your Land")".

### 2009

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2009")\]

The Felice Brothers joined [Old Crow Medicine Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Crow_Medicine_Show "Old Crow Medicine Show"), [Justin Townes Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Townes_Earle "Justin Townes Earle") and the [Dave Rawlings Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Rawlings_Machine "Dave Rawlings Machine") for a nine-city package tour called The Big Surprise Tour, after the lead track from the _[Yonder Is the Clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yonder_Is_the_Clock "Yonder Is the Clock")_ album. Kicking off on August 4, 2009 at the [Casino Ballroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampton_Beach_Casino_Ballroom "Hampton Beach Casino Ballroom") in [Hampton Beach, New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampton_Beach,_New_Hampshire "Hampton Beach, New Hampshire"), the tour travelled around the eastern United States before wrapping up at the World's Fair Park in [Knoxville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knoxville,_Tennessee "Knoxville, Tennessee"), on August 14, 2009.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-7)

### 2010

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2010")\]

The Felice Brothers opened for the [Dave Matthews Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band "Dave Matthews Band") at four concerts in Canada and the US, starting at the [Molson Amphitheatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molson_Amphitheatre "Molson Amphitheatre") in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto") on June 1, 2010. They also appeared at the Hardly Strictly Bluegrass Festival in San Francisco on October 3. In late September and early October the band reunited for four shows with Conor Oberst. All the shows were in California. They appeared at the [End of the Road Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_the_Road_Festival "End of the Road Festival"), Larmer Tree Gardens, Dorset, England in September.

### 2011

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: 2011")\]

The Felice Brothers released the single "Ponzi" on March 29. It was the first single from their newest album "Celebration, Florida" which released on May 10 on [Fat Possum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fat_Possum "Fat Possum") (USA) and May 23 on [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music") in Europe.

### 2012

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: 2012")\]

The Felice Brothers released a collection of songs on July 18 entitled "God Bless You, Amigo". It is a mixture of previously unreleased original songs and covers of classic folk songs. Shortly thereafter drummer Dave Turbeville was replaced by drummer David Estabrook.

### 2013

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: 2013")\]

On December 30, 2013, Dell launched their commercial called "Beginnings". It was made by Y&R, New York advertising agency, and features a cover of The Drifters' hit "This Magic Moment" performed by The Felice Brothers.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-8)

## Personnel

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Personnel")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/The_Felice_Brothers%2C_Hudson_NY.jpg/250px-The_Felice_Brothers%2C_Hudson_NY.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Felice_Brothers,_Hudson_NY.jpg)

The band has two main members, Ian and James Felice. Former members include their brother Simone Felice, their friend Josh "Christmas Clapton" Rawson, frequently described as a traveling dice player,[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-9) [fiddle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiddle "Fiddle") player Greg Farley, and drummer David Estabrook. At other times, they have featured a horn section in the band, composed of local [Hudson Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Valley "Hudson Valley") musicians. Ian is the main vocalist and plays the guitar and piano. James contributes vocals and plays the accordion, organ, and piano. Christmas plays the bass guitar. Dave Turbeville played the drums from 2009 to 2012, performing on Celebration, Florida, Poughkeepsie Princess, Mixtape, and God Bless You, Amigo. Simone Felice was the drummer as well as a vocalist and a guitarist. Simone is also an author, having released books entitled _Goodbye Amelia_, _Hail Mary, Full of Holes_ and _Black Jesus_. Simone Felice left the Felice Brothers in 2009. He now leads his own band - The Duke & the King (named after the duo of con-artists in Mark Twain's _[Adventures of Huckleberry Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventures_of_Huckleberry_Finn "Adventures of Huckleberry Finn")_) with Robert "Chicken" Burke. They released their debut album - Nothing Gold Can Stay on [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music") / Ramseur Records August 4, 2009, followed by Long Live the Duke & King in 2010. Simone released a self-titled album in 2012, followed that up with an album titled Strangers in 2014, and then released his third album titled The Projector in 2018.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-10)

## Cultural references

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Felice_Brothers&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Cultural references")\]

- The song "Whiskey In My Whiskey" is playing on the jukebox in Merlotte's bar in episode 7 of the first season of _[True Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Blood "True Blood")_ on HBO in 2008[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-12)
- The end of the song "Frankie's Gun!" is featured in the [British sitcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_sitcom "British sitcom") _[Outnumbered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outnumbered_(UK_TV_series) "Outnumbered (UK TV series)")'_ s 2009 Christmas Special, where all the family are seen singing along to it in the closing sequence.
- The song "Penn Station" was featured in the weather section on the [Welcome to Night Vale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_Night_Vale "Welcome to Night Vale") podcast in 2014
- The band is briefly featured playing their song "Cumberland Gap" in the film [Song One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_One "Song One"), 2014
- The song "Radio Song" plays on the jukebox in the bar where people are playing pool in the 2017 film [Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing,_Missouri "Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri")[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Felice_Brothers#cite_note-13)
- The song "Radio Song" also plays over the final scene in episode 15 _Best enemies_ of the second season of [Resident Alien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Alien_(TV_series) "Resident Alien (TV series)") on Syfy in 2022
- The song “Nail It on the First Try” plays over the final scene in the pilot episode of [St. Denis Medical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Denis_Medical "St. Denis Medical") on NBC in 2024
