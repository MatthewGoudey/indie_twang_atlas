_**Tallahassee**_ is the seventh studio album by [the Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats "The Mountain Goats"). It was the band's second new album to be released in 2002, and it marked quite a few changes. After releasing records (and cassettes) on small record labels such as Shrimper, Ajax, and [Emperor Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Jones "Emperor Jones"), _Tallahassee_ was the first Mountain Goats album to be released on a widely known independent label, the British [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock") label [4AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4AD "4AD"). It was also the first Mountain Goats album to have an official single released, for the song "See America Right."

_Tallahassee_ is the first record to feature what could be considered a full "band," with fuller instrumentation and a percussion section. Up to this point, most recordings under the Mountain Goats name had either been solo recordings by leader [John Darnielle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Darnielle "John Darnielle") or higher-quality recordings featuring [Rachel Ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Ware "Rachel Ware") on bass, and other supporting instrumentalists. On _Tallahassee_, Darnielle was joined by two past collaborators, multi-instrumentalists [Peter Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hughes_(musician) "Peter Hughes (musician)") and [Franklin Bruno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Bruno "Franklin Bruno"). It was co-produced, recorded, and mixed by producer [Tony Doogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Doogan "Tony Doogan") at Tarbox Studios in [Cassadaga, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassadaga,_New_York "Cassadaga, New York"), assisted by [Michael Ivins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Ivins "Michael Ivins") of [The Flaming Lips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flaming_Lips "The Flaming Lips"). The album was recorded in six days.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-1)

## Story

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tallahassee_(album)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Story")\]

_Tallahassee_ is completely devoted to two of Darnielle's recurring characters, a married couple constantly on the edge of divorce. As such, the lyrics tend to focus on the dysfunction in their relationship. The couple is known to fans as "the Alpha Couple," as many of the previous songs about them have titles beginning with the word "Alpha" (e.g. "Alpha Incipiens," "Alpha Desperation March"). The final song on the album is titled "Alpha Rats Nest" as a nod of sorts to the other songs.

In the songs on this album, the Alpha Couple move into a house on Southwood Plantation Road in [Tallahassee, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee,_Florida "Tallahassee, Florida"). The house is falling apart, a metaphor for their crumbling marriage. Sick of themselves and each other, yet unwilling to part, they begin [drinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholism "Alcoholism") themselves to death.

The album's liner notes, presumably written from the husband's point of view, add another dimension to the story, as does the album's (now defunct) promotional website, written by Darnielle and designed by his wife Lalitree.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-2)

Professional ratings

Veteran rock critic [Robert Christgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Christgau "Robert Christgau") of the _[Village Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Voice "Village Voice")_ gave the album an 'A' grade in his first review of a Mountain Goats album;[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-christgau2002-12) _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ and _[Mojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojo_(magazine) "Mojo (magazine)")_ also wrote glowing reviews. The album also received positive reviews from websites such as _[PopMatters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PopMatters "PopMatters")_[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-13) and _Dusted_.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-14) Although the [online music magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_magazine "Online magazine") _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_ gave _Tallahassee_ a mixed review upon release, stating that "Darnielle's apparent phobia for full-band arrangements prevents the music from keeping pace with the storylines",[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-Pitchfork-8) it later included the album at number 176 on their 2009 list of top 200 albums of the 2000s.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-15)

The songs "No Children" and "Old College Try" were featured in the _[Moral Orel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_Orel "Moral Orel")_ episodes "Numb" and "Help" respectively. The song "No Children" was also featured in the series finale of _[You're the Worst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_the_Worst "You're the Worst")_.

In 2021, "No Children" became a meme on TikTok where people performed a choreographed interpretive dance to the lyrics of the chorus.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee_(album)#cite_note-16)
