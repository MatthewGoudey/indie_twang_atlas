**Mount Moriah** is an American folk band consisting of lead vocalist [Heather "H.C." McEntire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.C._McEntire "H.C. McEntire"),[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-chicagoreader-1) guitarist Jenks Miller, and bassist Casey Toll.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-theavclub-2)

## Discography

- _Mount Moriah_ (2011, Holidays For Quince Records)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-pitchfork-3)
- _Miracle Temple_ (2013, [Merge Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_Records "Merge Records"))[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-spinmagazine-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-popmatters-5)
- _How to Dance_ (2016, Merge Records)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-pitchfork1-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Moriah_(band)#cite_note-popmattersq-7)
