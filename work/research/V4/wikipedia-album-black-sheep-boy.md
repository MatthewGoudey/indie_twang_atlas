_**Black Sheep Boy**_ is the third studio album by American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band [Okkervil River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okkervil_River "Okkervil River"), released on April 5, 2005. The title is inspired by the song "Black Sheep Boy" by 1960s [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music") singer [Tim Hardin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Hardin "Tim Hardin"). The album deals with Hardin's struggle with heroin addiction and Okkervil River lead singer [Will Sheff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Sheff "Will Sheff")'s failed relationships and heartbreaks. The [album cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album_cover "Album cover") features the work of [Providence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence,_Rhode_Island "Providence, Rhode Island") artist [William Schaff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Schaff "William Schaff").

## Background

Most of the songs for the record were written in February 2004 in [Bloomington, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana "Bloomington, Indiana") by Will Sheff. Sheff describes the album's theme in a 2005 interview:

"The Black Sheep Boy is a fantastical character that crops up here and there on the album. In the lyrics pretty much anything in quotes is the voice of the Black Sheep Boy. I think a lot of the dirtier, rougher, more obtuse songs are the product of the Black Sheep Boy."[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-popmatters-2)

The writing of the album took place during a difficult time for the band. The relative lack of commercial success of their previous albums meant that the band's future was in doubt and Sheff was rendered homeless in order to make ends meet and afford the costs related to the band's commitments.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-Anniversary-3)

Professional ratings

_Black Sheep Boy_ has received general acclaim. On the review aggregate site [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), the album has a score of 86 out of 100, indicating "universal acclaim".[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-Metacritic-4)

The album was ranked number 22 on [Amazon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com "Amazon.com")'s Top 50 Albums of 2005 list.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-15) The music [online magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_magazine "Online magazine") _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_ placed _Black Sheep Boy_ at number 174 on their list of top 200 albums of the 2000s.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-16)

In 2015, Okkervil River released an expanded three-disc re-issue of Black Sheep Boy to commemorate the album's 10th anniversary, including the [Black Sheep Boy Appendix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy_Appendix "Black Sheep Boy Appendix") EP in its entirety and a disc consisting of previously unreleased covers.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sheep_Boy#cite_note-pitchfork-anniversary-17)
