**Clem Snide** is an [alt-country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt-country "Alt-country") band featuring [Eef Barzelay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eef_Barzelay "Eef Barzelay") (guitar, vocals), Brendan Fitzpatrick (bass) and Ben Martin (drums).

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Clem_Snide&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

"Clem Snide" is a character in several novels by [William S. Burroughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_S._Burroughs "William S. Burroughs"), including _[Naked Lunch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naked_Lunch "Naked Lunch")_, _[The Ticket That Exploded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ticket_That_Exploded "The Ticket That Exploded")_ and _[Exterminator!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterminator! "Exterminator!")_. The band Clem Snide was started by songwriter and singer [Eef Barzelay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eef_Barzelay "Eef Barzelay"), Jason Glasser, William J. Grabek Jr., and drummer Eric Paull in Boston in 1991. A few years later, with the addition of bassist Jeff "SweetBread" Marshall, the band made its first record, _[You Were a Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Were_a_Diamond "You Were a Diamond")_, with producer [Adam Lasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Lasus "Adam Lasus"). After building up a local following they attracted the attention of Seymour Stein, who then signed them to the Sire label for whom they recorded _[Your Favorite Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Your_Favorite_Music "Your Favorite Music")_ in 1999.

A couple years later Clem Snide released their third album _[The Ghost of Fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ghost_of_Fashion "The Ghost of Fashion")_ on indie stalwart SpinArt Records. The record received some mainstream attention due to the song "Moment in the Sun", which was used as the theme song for the second season of the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") program _[Ed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_(television_program) "Ed (television program)")_.

After an ill-fated post-9/11 tour, the band began to unravel, but not before completing two more records: _[Soft Spot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_Spot_(album) "Soft Spot (album)")_ (2003) and _[End of Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_Love_(album) "End of Love (album)")_ (2005). After beginning work on another record, the strains of life in the music industry became too great and Clem Snide broke up. Barzelay went on to release two solo records, _[Bitter Honey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitter_Honey_(Eef_Barzelay_album) "Bitter Honey (Eef Barzelay album)")_ and _[Lose Big](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lose_Big "Lose Big")_.

In 2009, the band re-grouped and released their sixth record _[Hungry Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungry_Bird "Hungry Bird")_ on 429 Records. On February 23, 2010, Clem Snide released their seventh long-player, _[The Meat of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Meat_of_Life "The Meat of Life")_, also on 429 Records.

The band's music featured in the series _[Californication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Californication_(TV_series) "Californication (TV series)")_, when "Faithfully" was played during the end credits of "Waiting for a Miracle", the fourth episode of season 5 (originally broadcast January 2012).

In February 2013, Clem Snide released a new track, "The Woods", which was written for "Senses", the second episode of the [Professor Brian Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Cox_(physicist) "Brian Cox (physicist)")-presented [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") science and nature show _[Wonders of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonders_of_Life_(TV_series) "Wonders of Life (TV series)")_.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clem_Snide#cite_note-1)

"No One's More Happy Than You" from _[The Ghost of Fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ghost_of_Fashion "The Ghost of Fashion")_ was featured in the eleventh episode of [season 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Wife_(season_7) "The Good Wife (season 7)") of _[The Good Wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Wife "The Good Wife")_ that aired on January 10, 2016.

In 2017, Clem Snide's cover of " [Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_(Christina_Aguilera_song) "Beautiful (Christina Aguilera song)")" was used in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") show _[Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_(TV_series) "Love (TV series)")_, playing over the end credits of Season 2 Episode 4.
