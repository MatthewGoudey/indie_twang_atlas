**Delta Spirit** is an American rock band from [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), United States. The group consists of Jonathan Jameson (bass), Brandon Young (drums), [Matthew Vasquez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Logan_Vasquez "Matthew Logan Vasquez") (vocals and guitars), Kelly Winrich (multi-instrumentalist), and William McLaren (guitar).

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Delta_Spirit&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

Founded in San Diego in 2005, Jameson and Young, who had played with each other in many bands, decided to form a band with their new friend Matthew Logan Vasquez. Young first spotted Vasquez busking on a bench in San Diego at two in the morning, and the two exchanged information.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-1) Delta Spirit recorded their _I Think I've Found It_ EP at Winrich's home studio, and he joined the band within the first year. Winrich and Vasquez had been close friends since they were 18 years old. The EP was later released by Monarchy Music on CD and 12" Vinyl in 2006.

The band produced and recorded their debut album, _[Ode to Sunshine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ode_to_Sunshine "Ode to Sunshine")_, in a cabin in Julian, CA and released it independently in late 2007.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-2) _Ode to Sunshine_ was re-released with new artwork (featuring Winrich's great-uncle Dr. Thomas Payne on the cover) and a new version of the song "Streetwalker" on August 26, 2008, by [Rounder Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rounder_Records "Rounder Records"). The band has made numerous television appearances, including _[Late Night with Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Night_with_Conan_O'Brien "Late Night with Conan O'Brien")_ in late 2008 and _[Last Call with Carson Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Call_with_Carson_Daly "Last Call with Carson Daly")_ in March 2009.

Walker left the band in the fall of 2009. Their second album, _[History From Below](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_from_Below_(album) "History from Below (album)")_, was released on June 8, 2010. The album's title was inspired by the historian and author [Howard Zinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Zinn "Howard Zinn"). Many of the songs were composed on an acoustic guitar in hotel rooms as the band toured 293 dates in 2009. On November 15, 2010, Delta Spirit performed a live set for " [Guitar Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar_Center "Guitar Center") Sessions" on [DirecTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DirecTV "DirecTV"). The episode included an interview with program host, [Nic Harcourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Harcourt "Nic Harcourt").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-3) In 2010–2011, the band also performed on _[Jimmy Kimmel Live!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel_Live! "Jimmy Kimmel Live!")_, _[Last Call with Carson Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Call_with_Carson_Daly "Last Call with Carson Daly")_, and [_Jools Holland_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Later..._with_Jools_Holland "Later... with Jools Holland"). That same year Delta Spirit did 2 US tours and one European Tour. In 2011, William McLaren became a full member of Delta Spirit. McLaren formerly played in [The Willowz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Willowz "The Willowz") and [Cults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cults_(band) "Cults (band)"). In April 2011, Delta Spirit played in the [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Music_and_Arts_Festival "Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival") in [Indio, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indio,_California "Indio, California").
In the Summer of 2011, they rented a rehearsal studio in San Pedro, CA used by [Cold War Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War_Kids "Cold War Kids") and [Murder City Devils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_City_Devils "Murder City Devils") in order to write what would become their third record, "Delta Spirit". In July, they traveled to West Hurley, NY to refine and record that material at Dreamland with producer Chris Coady (Beach House, TV On the Radio).
In the fall of 2011, Delta Spirit opened for [My Morning Jacket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Morning_Jacket "My Morning Jacket"). On March 13, 2012 they released their third studio album, self-titled _Delta Spirit_. On August 4, 2012, the band performed at [Lollapalooza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza "Lollapalooza") in Chicago, IL.
In 2013 the band relocated to [Brooklyn, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn,_New_York "Brooklyn, New York").

The Delta Spirit song "Devil Knows You're Dead" was used in the final scene of the 5th and final season of _[Friday Night Lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_Night_Lights_(TV_series) "Friday Night Lights (TV series)")_. The song "People, Turn Around", was also used in the final scene of the Season 2 _[Sons of Anarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sons_of_Anarchy "Sons of Anarchy")_ episode "Potlatch". Their song "Salt in the Wound" from their 2008 album _[History from Below](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_from_Below "History from Below")_ appeared in the _[Grey's Anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey's_Anatomy "Grey's Anatomy")_ episode " [With You I'm Born Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/With_You_I'm_Born_Again_(Grey's_Anatomy) "With You I'm Born Again (Grey's Anatomy)")".[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-4) The song "9/11" was used in the final scene of the Season 2 _[My Life As Liz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Life_As_Liz "My Life As Liz")_ episode 5. The song "People C'mon" was used in _[The Big C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_C_(TV_series) "The Big C (TV series)")_ episode "Blue Eyed Iris" and in UFC Primetime: Velasquez vs. Dos Santos. The song "Running" is featured on " [The Walking Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series) "The Walking Dead (TV series)")" Original Soundtrack – Vol. 1[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-5)
The song "Yamaha" was used in the 2013 movie [_Warm Bodies_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warm_Bodies_(film) "Warm Bodies (film)").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-6) The song " [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_(Delta_Spirit_song) "California (Delta Spirit song)")" was used in _[American Dad!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Dad! "American Dad!")_ in season 10, episode 06.

In 2014, the band switched to [Dualtone Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dualtone_Records "Dualtone Records") and released the album _Into the Wide_ produced by Ben Allen (Animal Collective, Deerhunter, Belle and Sebastian). It is the group's highest-charting album in the United States to date. The band's publicity tour included a performance on _[Late Show with David Letterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Show_with_David_Letterman "Late Show with David Letterman")_.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Spirit#cite_note-broadwayworld1-7)

In 2019, the band announced a New Year's Eve Show in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"), and then later announced the Not Dead Yet tour for 2020.
