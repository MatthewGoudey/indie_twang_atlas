**Cotton Jones** (formerly **The Cotton Jones Basket Ride**) is an American [indie folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_folk "Indie folk") band, with elements of [psychedelic folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_folk "Psychedelic folk"), [dream pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_pop "Dream pop"), and [chamber pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_pop "Chamber pop"), based in [Cumberland, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumberland,_Maryland "Cumberland, Maryland") and currently signed to [Suicide Squeeze Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_Squeeze_Records "Suicide Squeeze Records").

Michael Nau (born October 31, 1984) is the lead singer-songwriter and plays guitar, Whitney McGraw (born July 20, 1986) is on keyboards, organ, and electronic autoharp, Todd Gowans (born February 4, 1986) also plays guitar, with Greg Bender on bass.

## Albums

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Albums")\]

### _The River Strumming_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: The River Strumming")\]

On September 23, 2008, the band released their first LP, _The River Strumming_, through the indie label St. Ives. It was a hand-made package limited to only 300 copies and was described as a "delightful slab of fuzzed-out dream folk."[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-1) The album was reissued by Suicide Squeeze Records during the summer of 2023 for its 15th anniversary, limited to only 1000 copies, 400 on 180-gram half coke bottle green and half bone, 600 on 180-gram black vinyl.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-2) Mark Lager, in a retrospective review on _[Aquarium Drunkard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquarium_Drunkard "Aquarium Drunkard")_, described _The River Strumming_ as "a summer night trip through the swamps, a car rambling down lonely backroads...macabre and magical psychedelia, southern gothic style."[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-3)

### _Paranoid Cocoon_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Paranoid Cocoon")\]

On January 27, 2009, the band released their second LP, their debut for Suicide Squeeze Records, _[Paranoid Cocoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paranoid_Cocoon "Paranoid Cocoon")_. It was described as a "weepy, reverb-drenched blend of Pacific Northwest, 1960s folk-pop, and heartland rock."[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-4)

### _Tall Hours in the Glowstream_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Tall Hours in the Glowstream")\]

On August 24, 2010, the band released their third LP, _[Tall Hours in the Glowstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tall_Hours_in_the_Glowstream "Tall Hours in the Glowstream")_. It was described as "classic country music filtered through a dream-pop haze."[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-5)

### _About the Game_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: About the Game")\]

On August 21, 2012, the band released _About the Game_, a 7" record limited to only 500 copies.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-6)

### _Mowing_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Mowing")\]

On November 18, 2015, Michael Nau announced his solo debut _Mowing_, introducing the first single "Winter Beat" via SoundCloud.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-7) The album's release date was February 19, 2016.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-8)

### _Some Twist_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Some Twist")\]

On June 16, 2017, Michael Nau released his second solo album _Some Twist_.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-9) [AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic") described it as a "high-humidity set for long summer days, present or imagined" and Red & Black described it as "outer space road trip music...songs that feel unreal in their otherworldliness."

### _Accompany_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cotton_Jones&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Accompany")\]

On December 8, 2023, Michael Nau released his fifth solo album _Accompany_.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Jones#cite_note-10) Glide Magazine called the album "Nau’s best solo effort to date."
