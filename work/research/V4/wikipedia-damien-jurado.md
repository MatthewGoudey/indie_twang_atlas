Musical artist

**Damien Troy Jurado** ([/hərˈɑːdoʊ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English");[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-2) born November 12, 1972) is an American singer-songwriter from [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington "Seattle, Washington"), United States. Over the years, he has released albums on [Sub Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop "Sub Pop"), [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian"),[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-3) [Loose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Music "Loose Music"),[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-4) and is currently on his own label, Maraqopa Records.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-5)

## Music career

Jurado's solo career began during the mid-1990s, releasing lo-fi folk based recordings on his own cassette-only label, Casa Recordings. Gaining a local cult following in Seattle, he was brought to the attention of Sub Pop Records by [Sunny Day Real Estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunny_Day_Real_Estate "Sunny Day Real Estate") singer [Jeremy Enigk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Enigk "Jeremy Enigk"). After two 7-inch releases ( _Motorbike_ and _Trampoline_) [Sub Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop "Sub Pop") issued his first full album, _[Waters Ave S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waters_Ave_S. "Waters Ave S.")_ in 1997. His second album, _[Rehearsals for Departure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehearsals_for_Departure "Rehearsals for Departure")_, was released in 1999, produced by [Ken Stringfellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Stringfellow "Ken Stringfellow") ( [The Posies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Posies "The Posies"), [Big Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Star_(band) "Big Star (band)"), [R.E.M.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.M. "R.E.M.")).

He often makes use of [found sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Found_sound "Found sound") and field recording techniques, and has experimented with different forms of tape recordings. In 2000 he released _Postcards and Audio Letters_, a collection of found audio letters and fragments that he had found from sources such as thrift store tape players and answering machines. Also released in 2000 was _[Ghost of David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_of_David "Ghost of David")_, Jurado's bleakest and most personal sounding record to date. _[I Break Chairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Break_Chairs "I Break Chairs")_ (2002) was produced by long-time friend, [Pedro the Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_the_Lion "Pedro the Lion")'s [David Bazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bazan "David Bazan"). It was his last album for Sub Pop, and was a much rockier, electric affair.

After signing for the Indiana-based label [Secretly Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian "Secretly Canadian"), Jurado reverted to his trademark folk ballad-based style, releasing four more albums: _[Where Shall You Take Me?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_Shall_You_Take_Me%3F "Where Shall You Take Me?")_ (2003), _[On My Way to Absence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_My_Way_to_Absence "On My Way to Absence")_, (2005) _[And Now That I'm in Your Shadow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And_Now_That_I'm_in_Your_Shadow "And Now That I'm in Your Shadow")_ (2006) and the rockier _[Caught in the Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caught_in_the_Trees "Caught in the Trees")_ (2008).[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-6)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/18/Damien_Jurado.jpg/500px-Damien_Jurado.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Damien_Jurado.jpg) Damien Jurado performing in [Denton, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Texas "Denton, Texas"), in 2009

In 2009, Jurado teamed with his brother Drake to issue an LP under the moniker _Hoquiam_, released on February 23, 2010. The album preceded Damien's next solo release, _[Saint Bartlett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Bartlett "Saint Bartlett")_, which was released May 25, 2010, and was produced by label mate [Richard Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Swift_(singer-songwriter) "Richard Swift (singer-songwriter)").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-7) After touring the album with [Kay Kay and His Weathered Underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kay_Kay_and_His_Weathered_Underground "Kay Kay and His Weathered Underground"), Jurado began work on his next album. On February 21, 2012, he released his 10th studio album, _[Maraqopa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maraqopa "Maraqopa")_, his sixth for Secretly Canadian.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-8) In January 2014, Jurado released _[Brothers and Sisters of the Eternal Son](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_and_Sisters_of_the_Eternal_Son "Brothers and Sisters of the Eternal Son")_,[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-9) an album which marked the third straight collaboration with producer [Richard Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Swift_(singer-songwriter) "Richard Swift (singer-songwriter)"). In March 2016, Jurado released _Visions of Us on the Land,_ and in December of the same year he and Swift released a collection of covers from 2010, _Other People's Songs, Volume 1._[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-10) In 2016, Damien Jurado released _The Horizon Just Laughed_, his first self produced album, recorded at L.A. studio [Sonikwire](https://soundbetter.com/profiles/134342-alex-bush--sonikwire-studio). In 2018, Jurado left Secretly Canadian to join Mama Bird Recording Co. and Loose Music, and subsequently released the albums _In The Shape Of A Storm_ (2019), and _What's New, Tomboy?_ (2020)

In 2021, Jurado established Maraqopa Records for all of his future releases.

On the 14th of May 2021, Jurado released his 17th full length studio album, _The Monster Who Hated Pennsylvania._

On the 24th of June 2022, Jurado released his 18th full length studio album, _Reggae Film Star._

On March 31, 2023, Jurado released his 19th studio album, _Sometimes You Hurt the Ones You Hate_. Prior to the release, Jurado announced that he would no longer be releasing music on Spotify, citing the company's low royalty rates for artists.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-11) Jurado eventually decided to release this album, and all future releases, on Spotify.

On October 12, 2023, Jurado released his 20th studio album, _Motorcycle Madness_. Physical copies of the album were limited to 200 vinyl records, and 100 compact discs.

On November 1, 2023, Jurado released his 21st studio album, _Passing the Giraffes_. The release marked Jurado's third studio album of 2023.

On January 8, 2024, Jurado released, _Roger's Audition: The "Reggae Film Star" Demos_. Physical copies of the album were limited to 100 compact discs.

Jurado released two non-album singles, "I've Never Known Alice" and "Call Me, Madame", on August 5, 2024.

Jurado released _Motorcycle Madness ('25)_ on February 14, 2025. In an online post, Jurado described the album as the "completed" version of the previous 2023 release. He elaborated, saying the 2023 release of _Motorcycle Madness_ was essentially a "rehearsal" for the upcoming release. Unlike the 2023 version, _Motorcycle Madness ('25)_ has a stereo mix.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-12)

On October 30, 2025, Jurado released his 22nd studio album, _Private Hospital_. Jurado made the decision to forego traditional formats, and released it as a book.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-13)

On May 1, 2026, Jurado released, _Did Something In Me Break?_, a collaborative full-length LP with folk musician, Lilly Miller. [\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-14)

### Collaborations and contributions

Jurado features on the song, "Almost Home," from [Moby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moby "Moby")'s album, _[Innocents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innocents_(Moby_album) "Innocents (Moby album)")_.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-15)

He is also featured in an episode of the [BYUtv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BYUtv "BYUtv") music documentary series _Audio-Files_.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-16)

Jurado's song, "Everything Trying," appeared in the NBC series, _The Blacklist_ (Season 1, episode 12), in the 2013 Best Foreign Film Oscar film _[The Great Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Beauty "The Great Beauty")_, in the Fox series _[House M.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_(TV_series) "House (TV series)")_ (Season 7, Episode 14), and in the 2011 film _[The Dilemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dilemma "The Dilemma")_.

The 2014 Joe Carnahan directed crime thriller, _[Stretch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stretch_(2014_film) "Stretch (2014 film)")_, features Jurado's song, "Let Us All In," during the end of the film.

He duets with [Lotte Kestner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotte_Kestner "Lotte Kestner") on her song, "Turn the Wolves," on the 2013 album, _The Bluebird of Happiness_.

The soundtrack for the 2015 romantic comedy, _[Tumbledown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumbledown_(2015_film) "Tumbledown (2015 film)"),_ includes several songs by Jurado.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Jurado#cite_note-17)

[Richard Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Swift_(singer-songwriter) "Richard Swift (singer-songwriter)") produced four of his albums.

Jurado's track "A.M. AM" from _[Visions of Us on the Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visions_of_Us_on_the_Land "Visions of Us on the Land")_ is prominently featured in Episode 3 as well as "Cloudy Shoes" from _[Saint Bartlett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Bartlett "Saint Bartlett")_ in Episode 6 of the 2018 [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") documentary series _[Wild Wild Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Wild_Country "Wild Wild Country")._

Jurado's song, "Silver Joy," is featured on the soundtrack to the 2023 Christmas comedy-drama, _[The Holdovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holdovers "The Holdovers")_.
