**Hiss Golden Messenger** is an American [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music") band originating from [Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham,_North_Carolina "Durham, North Carolina"), [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina "North Carolina"), formed in 2007 and led by singer and songwriter MC Taylor.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-:0-2)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hiss_Golden_Messenger&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The band was formed by Michael Carrington "M.C." Taylor and Scott Hirsch, following the dissolution of their previous band, the alternative country group [The Court and Spark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Court_and_Spark "The Court and Spark").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-3) Hiss Golden Messenger's early recordings were released through Taylor's own label, "Heaven & Earth Magic Recording Company."[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-american-4)

The band's debut album, _[Country Hai East Cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Hai_East_Cotton "Country Hai East Cotton")_, was released in 2009 via Taylor's label in a limited edition of 500.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-debut-5) This was followed by their international debut album, _Bad Debt_, in 2010, released via Black Maps.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-allmusic-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-DiS-7) The band's third album, _Poor Moon_, was released in 2012 via North Carolina–based "Paradise of Bachelors", and received positive reviews.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-p4k-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-CoS-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-huff-10) The band's fourth album, _Haw_, was released on April 2, 2013.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-11)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-haw-12)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-swampy-13)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/07/Hiss_Golden_Messenger_2018.jpg/250px-Hiss_Golden_Messenger_2018.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hiss_Golden_Messenger_2018.jpg) Hiss Golden Messenger performing live in December, 2018.

Hiss Golden Messenger was signed to [Merge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_Records "Merge Records") in April 2014 and released their fifth album and Merge label debut, _[Lateness of Dancers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateness_of_Dancers "Lateness of Dancers")_ on September 9, 2014.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-14)[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-15) They have gone on to release four additional full-length records via Merge, as well as remastered versions of _[Bad Debt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Debt_(album) "Bad Debt (album)"),_ [_Poor Moon_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poor_Moon_(Hiss_Golden_Messenger_album) "Poor Moon (Hiss Golden Messenger album)"), and _Haw_.

Their 2019 album _[Terms of Surrender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terms_of_Surrender "Terms of Surrender")_ received a nomination for [Best Americana Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Americana_Album "Grammy Award for Best Americana Album") at the [63rd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "63rd Annual Grammy Awards").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-grammy-16)

In July 2021, the band participated in the [Newport Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_Folk_Festival "Newport Folk Festival").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-17)

## Musical style

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hiss_Golden_Messenger&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Musical style")\]

The band's music contains elements from various musical genres, such as folk, [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), [dub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dub_music "Dub music"), [country soul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_soul "Southern soul"), [rhythm and blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_and_blues "Rhythm and blues"), [bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_music "Bluegrass music"), [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz"), [funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funk "Funk"), [swamp pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swamp_pop "Swamp pop"), [gospel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gospel_music "Gospel music"), [blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues "Blues"), and [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-p4k-8)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-debut-5)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-haw-12)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-swampy-13) The band's style was also described as " [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country")" and " [country rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rock "Country rock")."[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-debut-5)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-swampy-13)[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-18) The band's main influences include [the Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles"), [The Byrds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Byrds "The Byrds"), and [Buffalo Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_Springfield "Buffalo Springfield").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-allmusic-6) The band has been compared to [Will Oldham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Oldham "Will Oldham") and [Bill Callahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Callahan_(musician) "Bill Callahan (musician)").[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiss_Golden_Messenger#cite_note-19)
