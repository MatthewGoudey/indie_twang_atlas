**Vetiver** is an American [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music") band headed by songwriter [Andy Cabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Cabic "Andy Cabic").

## History

Vetiver was formed in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco") in 2002. The band released their self-titled debut album in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_in_music "2004 in music") on the small indie folk label [DiCristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DiCristina?action=edit&redlink=1 "DiCristina").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-1) Since the album's release, Vetiver has toured extensively, opening for and collaborating with [Devendra Banhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devendra_Banhart "Devendra Banhart") and [Joanna Newsom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Newsom "Joanna Newsom"). Vetiver released another album, _[To Find Me Gone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Find_Me_Gone "To Find Me Gone")_, on DiCristina in 2006.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-2) Banhart and Cabic also launched their own label, [Gnomonsong Recordings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnomonsong "Gnomonsong"), releasing [Jana Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jana_Hunter "Jana Hunter")'s _[Blank Unstaring Heirs of Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blank_Unstaring_Heirs_of_Doom "Blank Unstaring Heirs of Doom")_ in 2005 and _There's No Home_ in 2007.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-3) The label also released in 2008 Vetiver's _[Thing of the Past](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thing_of_the_Past "Thing of the Past")_, a collection of cover songs that have influenced Cabic's aesthetic.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-4) [Sub Pop Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop "Sub Pop") (US) and [Bella Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Union "Bella Union") (UK) released Vetiver's _[Tight Knit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tight_Knit "Tight Knit")_ (2009)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-6) _The Errant Charm_ (2011),[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-7)[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-8) and _Complete Strangers_ (2015).[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-9)

The band shared the bill with [Vashti Bunyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vashti_Bunyan "Vashti Bunyan") on her US tour in early 2007.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-10) Over the years, the band has toured with artists like [Fleet Foxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleet_Foxes "Fleet Foxes"),[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-11) [The Shins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shins "The Shins"), [Fruit Bats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Bats_(band) "Fruit Bats (band)")[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-12) and [Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco").[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-13)

Andy Cabic's music has also been featured in numerous TV commercials, including an original song for Birds Eye.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-14) Cabic also works as a composer, including the documentary _The Family Jams_[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-15) and the film _[Smashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smashed_(film) "Smashed (film)")_, which was shown at the [Sundance Film Festival in 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Sundance_Film_Festival "2012 Sundance Film Festival").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver_(band)#cite_note-16)
