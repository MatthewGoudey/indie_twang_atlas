Musical artist

**Howard “Howe” Gelb** (born October 22, 1956, in [Wilkes-Barre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilkes-Barre,_Pennsylvania "Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania"), Pennsylvania)[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-1) is an American singer-songwriter, musician and record producer based in [Tucson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona "Tucson, Arizona"), Arizona.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-4)

## Projects

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Howe_Gelb&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Projects")\]

Gelb's approach to music is collaborative and he has recorded with a number of side projects. In a 2004 interview with Gelb, _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ wrote "Gelb's way of dealing with it was to treat [Giant Sand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Sand "Giant Sand") (not to be confused with his 1970s electro-rock band Giant Sandworms) as a loose, uncompetitive, mutually supportive musical collective, a place for friends to hang out and play. 'I just liked the idea of having this kind of removed world, this brotherhood—the idea of a band being something more than a front person or dealing with the throes of fame.'"[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-guard-5)

In 2013, he worked with the Scottish singer/songwriter [KT Tunstall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KT_Tunstall "KT Tunstall") on her fourth studio album _[Invisible Empire // Crescent Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invisible_Empire_//_Crescent_Moon "Invisible Empire // Crescent Moon")_. He co-wrote and co-produced, and sang on several songs.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-6) His own solo album entitled _The Coincidentalist_ was released on New West Records in November 2013.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-7)

An impromptu performance at the wedding of a mutual friend lead to a long-distance collaboration under the name **Geckøs** with [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward") and McKowski of [The Lost Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_Brothers "The Lost Brothers"). The group released its first eponymous album in September 2025.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howe_Gelb#cite_note-8)
