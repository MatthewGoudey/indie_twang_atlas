Musical artist

**Daniel Tavis Romano** (born 1985) is a Canadian musician, poet and visual artist based out of his hometown of [Welland, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welland,_Ontario "Welland, Ontario").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-2) He is primarily known as a solo artist, though he is also a member of [Attack in Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_in_Black "Attack in Black") and has collaborated with [Julie Doiron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Doiron "Julie Doiron"), [Tamara Lindeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamara_Lindeman "Tamara Lindeman") and [Frederick Squire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Squire "Frederick Squire").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-kwrecord-3) He has also produced and performed with City and Colour, the recording project of [Dallas Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Green_(musician) "Dallas Green (musician)").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-4)

Romano is a partner in his own independent record label, [You've Changed Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You've_Changed_Records "You've Changed Records"), along with [Steve Lambke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Lambke "Steve Lambke"), of the [Constantines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantines "Constantines").[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-kite-5)

Some of his notable visual work includes designs for [M. Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Ward "M. Ward"), [Ben Kweller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Kweller "Ben Kweller"), [Ladyhawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladyhawk_(band) "Ladyhawk (band)") and [City and Colour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_and_Colour "City and Colour").

The Canadian music magazine _[Exclaim!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclaim! "Exclaim!")_ has identified five distinct styles in Romano's work: a gentle, rustic folk sound personified by projects such as _[Daniel, Fred & Julie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel,_Fred_&_Julie "Daniel, Fred & Julie")_ and _Workin' for the Music Man_, a retro [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") style on albums like _Sleep Beneath the Willow_ and _If I've Only One Time Askin'_, straight-ahead rock on _Nerveless_ and _How Ill Thy World Is Ordered_, psychedelic rock on works like _Modern Pressure_ and _Visions of the Higher Dream_, and punk rock in his works released under the band name Ancient Shapes.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-6)

Romano's 2011 album _Sleep Beneath the Willow_ was a longlisted nominee for the [2011 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Polaris_Music_Prize "2011 Polaris Music Prize"),[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-longlist-7) and his 2013 album _Come Cry With Me_ was longlisted for the [2013 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Polaris_Music_Prize "2013 Polaris Music Prize").

_If I've Only One Time Askin'_ was nominated for a 2016 [Juno Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award "Juno Award") for [Adult Alternative Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award_for_Adult_Alternative_Album_of_the_Year "Juno Award for Adult Alternative Album of the Year").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-8)

On January 4, 2018, Romano released two simultaneous surprise albums, _Human Touch_ and _Nerveless_.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-romano-9)

In 2020, during the [COVID-19 pandemic in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Canada "COVID-19 pandemic in Canada"), Romano released several new albums of both newly recorded and archival material in quick succession.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-10) This began with the surprise album _Visions of the Higher Dream_, which was made available on his [Bandcamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandcamp "Bandcamp") page for just $3.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-11) He followed up with the live album _Okay Wow_[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-12) and the studio album _Content to Point the Way_,[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-13) both recorded with his backing band The Outfit; and _Super Pollen_, a collaborative EP recorded with his brother Ian and Mike Haliechuk and Jonah Falco of [Fucked Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fucked_Up "Fucked Up").[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-14) In May, he followed up with _Forever Love's Fool_, a 22-minute [progressive rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_rock "Progressive rock") track recorded with [Danny Carey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Carey "Danny Carey") of the band [Tool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tool_(band) "Tool (band)").[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-15)

On June 18, 2021, Romano released a live album titled _Fully Plugged In._ Romano also announced another new album, _Cobra Poems_, set to release on September 10, 2021.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-16) He is also featured on Doiron's 2021 album _[I Thought of You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Thought_of_You_(album) "I Thought of You (album)")_.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-17)

In 2022 and 2023, Romano has appeared at some shows on [the Sadies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sadies "The Sadies")' concert tour to support _[Colder Streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colder_Streams "Colder Streams")_ as a guest guitarist.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-18) In 2025, he was announced as playing guitar in Pig Pen, a new band formed with celebrity chef [Matty Matheson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matty_Matheson "Matty Matheson") and [Wade MacNeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_MacNeil "Wade MacNeil") of [Alexisonfire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexisonfire "Alexisonfire").[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Romano#cite_note-19)
