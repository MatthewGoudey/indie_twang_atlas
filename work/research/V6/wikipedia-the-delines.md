Musical artist

**The Delines** are an American band from [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon"). Their original line-up was vocalist Amy Boone, Jenny Conlee ( [The Decemberists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Decemberists "The Decemberists")) on keyboards, Sean Oldham, Freddy Trujillo, [Willy Vlautin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Vlautin "Willy Vlautin") ( [Richmond Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine "Richmond Fontaine")) and pedal steel player Tucker Jackson ( [Minus 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minus_5 "Minus 5")). The band was formed in 2012 and is self-described as a "retro country soul band".[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-lastfm-1)

## History

The Delines were formed when singer Amy Boone was touring with [Richmond Fontaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine "Richmond Fontaine") and singing the female parts from that band's 2003 album, _Post to Wire_, which had been performed by her sister Deborah Kelly.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Uncut-2) Kelly and Boone had been in the Texas band the Damnations.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-No_Depression-3) Vlautin then formed the Delines, centred on Boone's evocative world-weary and vulnerable vocals. They gathered in Portland, Oregon with producer [John Askew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Askew "John Askew") to cut the band's debut album, _Colfax_. The songs on _Colfax_, which was released in June 2014, combined Vlautin's admired lyrics about characters from America's hinterland and their everyday struggles, with Boone's vocals, the emotion of which wrings out the pain of the characters. _Colfax_ was well received by the critics and scored 87 on [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") with seven positive reviews.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-metacritic-4)

Following the release of _Colfax_ the band embarked on a European tour including shows at the End Of The Road and Kilkenny Festivals.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Allmusic-5) They have since toured in North America and Australasia, and returned to Europe.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-The_Delines-6) A second album, _Scenic Sessions,_ was released as a limited edition CD for sale only on the 2015 European tour and from the band's website. This ten-song album had seven songs by Vlautin, a song written by Cory Gray and another by Amy Boone, as well as a cover of _Sunshine_ by [Sparklehorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparklehorse "Sparklehorse").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Bandcamp-7) Amy Boone was involved in a car accident in March 2016 and the band planned to record again when she had recovered.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Independent-8)

The band's second album proper, _The Imperial_, and a single from the album, "Eddie and Polly", were both released in January 2019.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Hunter-Tilney-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-bestfit-10) They also announced a European tour for the beginning of 2019 including a sold out show at the Union Chapel in London .[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-TBWS-11) _The Imperial_ debuted at number 1 for two weeks in the UK official Americana charts and was nearly as well received as their debut album, scoring 84 on [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") from six reviews.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-MT-12)

The band released their third album _The Sea Drift_ in January 2022, which topped year-end polls in many countries including _Heaven_ magazine in the Netherlands, _Americana UK_, number 11 in _[Uncut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncut_(magazine) "Uncut (magazine)")_, top 20 in _Rolling Stone France_, _El Pais_ and a host of others. They toured the UK three times over that year as well as dates across Europe and Australia including performances at Crossing Border, Kilkenny and Into the Great Wide Open Festivals.

## Members

The current members of The Delines are:[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines#cite_note-Bandcamp-7)

- Amy Boone – vocals, guitar
- Cory Gray – keyboards, trumpet
- Sean Oldham – percussion, vocals
- Freddy Trujillo – bass, vocals
- [Willy Vlautin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Vlautin "Willy Vlautin") – guitar, vocals
