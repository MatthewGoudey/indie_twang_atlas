**The Deslondes** are an [Americana musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_music "Americana music") group from [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans"). Their music blends influences from [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music"), [rock 'n' roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_'n'_roll "Rock 'n' roll"), [bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_music "Bluegrass music"), [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R&B "R&B"), [American roots music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_roots_music "American roots music"), [blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues "Blues"), [gospel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gospel_music "Gospel music"), [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), and [zydeco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zydeco "Zydeco").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-2) The group's members are Dan Cutler (vocals/stand-up bass), Sam Doores (vocals/guitar), Riley Downing (vocals/guitar), Howe Pearson (vocals/percussion), and John James Tourville (fiddle/pedal steel). All five members share in the songwriting process.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-3)

The band's first, self-titled album was released in 2015 on [New West Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_West_Records "New West Records").

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Deslondes&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The band formed in the gentrified section of the Holy Cross neighborhood in New Orleans' Lower Ninth Ward, and take their name from a street in that neighborhood.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-4) Sam Doores met Cameron Snyder while attending college. When Doores read [Woody Guthrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Guthrie "Woody Guthrie")'s autobiography, _Bound for Glory_, he quit school to head to New Orleans with Snyder where they formed the band The Broken Wing Routine, and attended the [Woody Guthrie Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Guthrie_Folk_Festival "Woody Guthrie Folk Festival") in [Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma "Oklahoma"), where they met [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri "Missouri") native Riley Downing. In New Orleans, Doores met Dan Cutler and formed The Tumbleweeds, while Snyder and John James Tourville met on tour with The Longtime Goners.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-5) Doores and Cutler also played in New Orleans band [Hurray for the Riff Raff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurray_for_the_Riff_Raff "Hurray for the Riff Raff"), for whom The Tumbleweeds often opened.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-6)

In 2013, The Tumbleweeds officially changed their name to The Deslondes and two years later released their first album, _The Deslondes_, to critical acclaim. Their first single, "Fought The Blues And Won," was premiered by [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-7) The music video for the song "The Real Deal" premiered on _[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")_.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-8)

A portion of their song "Low Down Soul" was played during a scene in season 6, episode 3 of _[Longmire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longmire_(TV_series) "Longmire (TV series)")_.

## Musical style

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Deslondes&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Musical style")\]

Doores describes the band's songwriting as a group process: "We're about as democratic as it gets. Five equal members who all write and contribute a whole lot to the project."[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-9) Their influences are vast, encompassing nearly every American music style. Pitchfork writes,

> "When the Deslondes open their self-titled debut with a walking piano line played way, way down the left side of the keyboard, they’re not just playing a rhythm that sounds distinctive in 2015 but also conveying an entire pop history that spans New Orleans rhythm and blues, early Memphis rock, Louisiana Hayride country, and every pick-up jazz band ever to busk on Royal Street."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Deslondes#cite_note-10)
