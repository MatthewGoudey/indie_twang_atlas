**Richmond Fontaine** was an American four-piece [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") and [alternative country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country "Alternative country") band, based in [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon"). They were active between 1994 and 2016 and recorded eleven studio albums, four live albums and two EPs.

Underpinned by lead singer and songwriter [Willy Vlautin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Vlautin "Willy Vlautin")'s lyrics, Richmond Fontaine songs often evoke [imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image "Image") of [Reno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno,_Nevada "Reno, Nevada"), [Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada "Nevada"), Portland, the [Western United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_United_States "Western United States"), and [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico "Mexico"), while telling stories in a style that critics have compared to [Raymond Carver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Carver "Raymond Carver").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-3) The group has cited influences such as [Gram Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram_Parsons "Gram Parsons"), [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(U.S._band) "X (U.S. band)"),[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-autogenerated1-4) Green on Red[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-5) and [Dave Alvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Alvin "Dave Alvin").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-6)

## History

Formed in 1994, Richmond Fontaine started touring the [Pacific Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest "Pacific Northwest") live circuit to support their first three albums released on [Cavity Search Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavity_Search_Records "Cavity Search Records"). Gradually gaining attention in the UK and Europe the band began regularly touring there. Most of the core musicians and producer [J. D. Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._D._Foster "J. D. Foster") have worked together for a decade, and produced several albums. The band is named after an American expat, "a burned out hippy", who helped bassist Dave Harding when his car was stuck in the desert in the [Baja California Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baja_California_Peninsula "Baja California Peninsula").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-7)

Richmond Fontaine first gained exposure outside the United States through a song that was included on a Vinyl Junkie Records 'Loose' compilation[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-autogenerated1-4) released in the UK. This was followed by the band's self-released fourth album, _Winnemucca_. The band signed with Decor Records in [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe "Europe") during 2003 with their next two releases proving pivotal to the band's success. Both were made "Albums of the Month" in influential magazine, _[Uncut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncut_(magazine) "Uncut (magazine)")_, which named both their fifth album _Post to Wire_ (2004) and sixth _The Fitzgerald_ (2005) "masterpieces".[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-autogenerated2-9) U.S. critics have been generally complimentary but have also cited the band's musical similarity to [Uncle Tupelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncle_Tupelo "Uncle Tupelo").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-11)

The band's 2007 album, _Thirteen Cities_, received positive reviews across Europe.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-autogenerated2-9) This was followed by _We Used to Think the Freeway Sounded Like a River_ in August 2009,[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-12) _The High Country_ in September 2011, and _You Can't Go Back If There's Nothing To Go Back To_ in March 2016.

## Other projects

Lead singer and songwriter Vlautin is also a novelist, with five books published since his debut, _[The Motel Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Motel_Life "The Motel Life")_, in 2006. He formed new group [The Delines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Delines "The Delines"), along with fellow Richmond Fontaine members Freddy Trujillo and Sean Oldham, in 2014.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Fontaine#cite_note-13)
