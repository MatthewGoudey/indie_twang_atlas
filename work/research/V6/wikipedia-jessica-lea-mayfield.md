Musical artist

**Jessica Lea Mayfield** (born August 27, 1989) is an American singer-songwriter from [Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent,_Ohio "Kent, Ohio"), [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio "Ohio"), United States. She is known for her ominous song writing, with a plaintive minimalist style that draws on both [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") and [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") music.

## Early years

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early years")\]

Mayfield first performed with her family [bluegrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_music "Bluegrass music") band One Way Rider at the age of 8.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-1) They began touring as a family band, boarding a 1956 tour bus (once belonging to [Bill Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Monroe "Bill Monroe"), [Kitty Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitty_Wells "Kitty Wells"), and [Ernest Tubb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Tubb "Ernest Tubb")) and headed south from Ohio to Tennessee. At age 11, Mayfield began playing guitar and writing songs.

## Studio work

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Studio work")\]

### _White Lies_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: White Lies")\]

At age 15, Mayfield recorded the 2005 EP _White Lies_ under the name Chittlin with her brother David Mayfield. The release was self-pressed in a single run of 100 copies, and [Dan Auerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Auerbach "Dan Auerbach") of [The Black Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Keys "The Black Keys") later discovered Mayfield through the EP.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-2) Auerbach and Mayfield subsequently began recording what became her debut album, _With Blasphemy So Heartfelt_.

### _With Blasphemy So Heartfelt_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: With Blasphemy So Heartfelt")\]

Mayfield's first LP was the September 16, 2008 release _With Blasphemy So Heartfelt_. The album was produced by Auerbach and recorded over a two-year span in his home studio in Akron. _With Blasphemy So Heartfelt_ features Mayfield on acoustic guitar and vocals, Auerbach on a variety of instrumentation, and Mayfield's brother David on upright bass. [Dr. Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Dog "Dr. Dog")’s Scott McMicken and Frank McElroy provide vocal harmonies on the track “I’m Not Lonely Anymore.” Says Auerbach of the recording experience, “I think she’s dark and moody in a mysterious way, not unlike [Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cave_and_the_Bad_Seeds "Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds").” He adds, “I’m just always really excited to make music with her.”

_With Blasphemy So Heartfelt_ debuted with quite a buzz around it. [Pitchfork Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Media "Pitchfork Media") gave the record a rating of 8.2 out of 10. Online magazine Blurt named _With Blasphemy_ the Best Album of 2008 and gave Mayfield the title of Best New Artist of 2008. The album's opening track "Kiss Me Again" was featured on [The CW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW "The CW")'s hit series _[Gossip Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gossip_Girl_(TV_series) "Gossip Girl (TV series)")_ on February 2, 2009, and "Bible Days" was featured on CBS's _[CSI: NY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_NY "CSI: NY")_. "For Today" is featured on the [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks") compilation _Have You Heard?: Winter '09_. Mayfield's cover of the [Buddy Holly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Holly "Buddy Holly") song " [Words of Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Words_of_Love "Words of Love")" is featured on the [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks") compilation _Sweetheart: Our Favorite Artists Sing Their Favorite Love Songs_.

### _Tell Me_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Tell Me")\]

Mayfield released her second LP _Tell Me_ on February 8, 2011; with her song “Our Hearts Are Wrong” being featured on the hit TV show, Pretty Little Liars.

### _Make My Head Sing_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Make My Head Sing")\]

Mayfield released her alternative rock album "Make My Head Sing" in 2014. Pitchfork said, "Jessica Lea Mayfield’s third album opens with the loud, percussive crunch and grind of an electric guitar, thudding loudly and precipitously— Co-producing with her bass player and husband Jesse Newport, Mayfield keeps the sound loose and roomy, preserving the effect of three musicians huddled in a cramped practice space. There’s something certainly compelling about this raw, minimalist sound."
The album is her first without Auerbach's influence.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-3)

### _Seth Avett and Jessica Lea Mayfield Sing Elliott Smith_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Seth Avett and Jessica Lea Mayfield Sing Elliott Smith")\]

Mayfield and Seth Avett of the Avett Brothers released an album of [Elliott Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Smith "Elliott Smith") cover songs in 2015.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-4)

### Collaborations

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Collaborations")\]

In 2007, Mayfield lent her voice to the track "Things Ain't Like They Used To Be" on [The Black Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Keys "The Black Keys")' album _[Attack & Release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_&_Release "Attack & Release")_ produced by [Danger Mouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danger_Mouse_(musician) "Danger Mouse (musician)"), making her the first guest vocalist to appear on any record released by The Black Keys.

Mayfield collaborated with Auerbach again on his 2009 debut solo release _[Keep It Hid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keep_It_Hid "Keep It Hid")_, lending her vocals to the track "When the Night Comes".

On June 3, 2026, Mayfield, with [Dolour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolour "Dolour"), released _[Pinkerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinkerton_(album) "Pinkerton (album)"),_ a cover of the album of the same name originally by [Weezer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weezer "Weezer").

### _Sorry is Gone_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Sorry is Gone")\]

On September 29, 2017, Mayfield's fourth studio LP _Sorry Is Gone_ was released by ATO Records: with the exception of the song "Offa My Hands", which was written and produced by [Patrick Damphier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Damphier "Patrick Damphier"), who has worked with [The Mynabirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mynabirds "The Mynabirds") and [The Arcs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Arcs "The Arcs"), the album was produced by [John Agnello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Agnello "John Agnello"), who has worked with [Dinosaur Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur_Jr "Dinosaur Jr") and [Sonic Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_Youth "Sonic Youth"), and includes appearances by Seth Avett and [Steve Shelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Shelley "Steve Shelley") of Sonic Youth.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-5) _Sorry is Gone_ was another nod to Mayfield's grunge-alternative influences, while incorporating some alt-country and pop elements as well.

The deeply personal album chronicles Mayfield's years-long journey as a survivor of domestic abuse, and separation from her abusive husband: "I feel like it’s almost my duty and my responsibility to advocate for this subject. Because if I really want things to change and I want women to be treated more fairly, then hiding my own experiences isn’t going to help anyone."[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lea_Mayfield#cite_note-6)

## Touring

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Lea_Mayfield&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Touring")\]

In addition to [opening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opening_act "Opening act") for many artists, including [The Black Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Keys "The Black Keys"), [The Avett Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Avett_Brothers "The Avett Brothers"), [Cake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cake_(band) "Cake (band)"), [Band of Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Horses "Band of Horses"), [Ryan Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Adams "Ryan Adams"), [Ray LaMontagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_LaMontagne "Ray LaMontagne"), and [Jay Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Farrar "Jay Farrar") ( [Son Volt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Volt "Son Volt"), [Uncle Tupelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncle_Tupelo "Uncle Tupelo")), Mayfield has frequently toured as a headliner, and performed at the [Blossom Music Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blossom_Music_Center "Blossom Music Center") in [Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuyahoga_Falls,_Ohio "Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio") as part of [A Prairie Home Companion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Prairie_Home_Companion "A Prairie Home Companion") in July 2010.
