**Kelsey Waldon** is an American country singer and songwriter. She has released four EPs and six full albums, the most recent being "Every Ghost" released on June 20, 2025.

## Early life

Waldon was born in [Ballard County, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballard_County,_Kentucky "Ballard County, Kentucky"), and raised in the rural Western Kentucky town [Monkey's Eyebrow, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkey's_Eyebrow,_Kentucky "Monkey's Eyebrow, Kentucky").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-1) Waldon's family roots in the [Bluegrass State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_State "Bluegrass State") date back over ten generations, from tobacco farmers to cattle raisers, and some of her first jobs were in farming and planting tobacco.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-2)

She picked up the guitar at age 13 to deal with her parents' divorce, and continued to use music as an outlet throughout her teenage years. After high school, instead of pursuing a college degree, Waldon moved to [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee") to see if she could make it in the music industry. She picked up any small gigs she could find, and continued to insert herself in the Nashville community. After two years she decided to enroll in [Belmont University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belmont_University "Belmont University") majoring in [songwriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songwriting "Songwriting") and music business so that she could learn more about the music industry.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-3)

## Career

Waldon self-released four EP's before releasing her debut LP _The Goldmine_. In 2007 she released _Dirty Feet, Dirty Hands_, followed by _Anchor In The Valley_ in 2010, _Anybody's Darlin_ in 2011, and _Fixin' It Up_ in 2012. Waldon's following began to grow when she released _The Goldmine_, produced by Michael Rinne, in 2014 which [The Fader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fader "The Fader") dubbed as "the brightest country debut of 2014".[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-4) The album was named one of Rolling Stone's "10 New Artists You Need To Know: Summer 2014" with journalist Marissa Moss calling Waldon, " [Tammy Wynette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammy_Wynette "Tammy Wynette") on a trip to Whiskeytown, as unafraid of heavy twang and spitfire pedal steel as coffeehouse confessionals."[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-5)

Waldon released another LP, titled _I've Got A Way_ in 2016. Her sophomore album ranked on two of [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR")'s most-acclaimed lists of the year; Fresh Air host [Ken Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Tucker "Ken Tucker")'s "Top 10 Favorite Albums of 2016" alongside [Beyoncé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9 "Beyoncé"), [Miranda Lambert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Lambert "Miranda Lambert"), and Stax legend [William Bell (singer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Bell_(singer) "William Bell (singer)")[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-6)

In May 2019, it was announced at the [Grand Ole Opry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Ole_Opry "Grand Ole Opry") that Waldon recently signed to [John Prine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Prine "John Prine")'s label [Oh Boy Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh_Boy_Records "Oh Boy Records"). Waldon is the first artist in fifteen years to be signed to Prine's label.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-7) Waldon released the album _White Noise/White Lines,_ her first release on Oh Boy Records, on October 4, 2019.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-8) The album was produced by Waldon and Dan Knobler. "Anyhow" was released as the lead single from the album.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-9) Writing for _Nashville Lifestyles Magazine_, music critic Luke Levenson commented, "The new LP pulls inspiration from both her Kentucky roots and the three intense years since her last release."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-10)

_They'll Never Keep Us Down_, an EP of politically charged [cover songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_song "Cover song"), was set for release on November 20, 2020. As well as the [Hazel Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazel_Dickens "Hazel Dickens") title track, she also tackled [Nina Simone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Simone "Nina Simone")'s " [Mississippi Goddam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Goddam "Mississippi Goddam")", [Kris Kristofferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Kristofferson "Kris Kristofferson")'s "The Law Is for Protection of the People", [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young "Neil Young")'s " [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_(Crosby,_Stills,_Nash_&_Young_song) "Ohio (Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young song)")", [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan")'s " [With God on Our Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/With_God_on_Our_Side_(song) "With God on Our Side (song)")", [Billy Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Taylor "Billy Taylor") and [Dick Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Dallas?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dick Dallas")' " [I Wish I Knew How It Would Feel to Be Free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Wish_I_Knew_How_It_Would_Feel_to_Be_Free "I Wish I Knew How It Would Feel to Be Free")" and Oh Boy Records founder John Prine's " [Sam Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Stone_(song) "Sam Stone (song)")". Guests include [Adia Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adia_Victoria "Adia Victoria"), [Kyshona Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyshona_Armstrong "Kyshona Armstrong"), and [Devon Gilfillian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Gilfillian "Devon Gilfillian").[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-11)

Kelsey has performed with artists of high notoriety within the country and Americana genres, such as John Prine, [Willie Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Nelson "Willie Nelson"), [Tanya Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Tucker "Tanya Tucker"), and Jamey Johnson. In 2019, John Prine invited Kelsey Waldon to join him on stage at [Bonnaroo Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnaroo_Music_Festival "Bonnaroo Music Festival") to sing Prine's duet "In Spite of Ourselves".[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-12) Waldon also joined guitarist and songwriter Jamey Johnson in performing Prine's Paradise, [Hank Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Williams "Hank Williams")' "I Saw the Light", and [Don Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Williams "Don Williams")' "Tulsa Time".[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-13) During Americana Fest 2019, Waldon stayed busy performing solo sets, as well as playing with [Tanya Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Tucker "Tanya Tucker") at 3rd and Lindsley. "That was definitely the most event-filled week I've ever done," Waldon told the _Nashville Scene_.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-14)

## Performances

Waldon made her debut performance on the [Grand Ole Opry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Ole_Opry "Grand Ole Opry") at the [Ryman Auditorium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryman_Auditorium "Ryman Auditorium") in 2016, then was invited to play the Opry House on April 23 of 2019. She performed at The Opry again on May 28, 2019, together with [John Prine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Prine "John Prine"), Sturgill Simpson, and others.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-15) She has also played the historic [Station Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Station_Inn "Station Inn"), the go-to spot of the 1970s, where intimate, post-Opry jam sessions were hosted by musicians like [Jimmy Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Martin "Jimmy Martin"), [Bobby Osborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Osborne "Bobby Osborne"), and [Bill Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Monroe "Bill Monroe").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-16)

In August, 2025, Waldon performed at the historic 3TEN ACL venue in Austin, Texas.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-17)[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Waldon#cite_note-18)
