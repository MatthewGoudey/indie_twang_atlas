Musical artist

**Caitlin Elisabeth Rose** (born June 23, 1987) is an American [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") singer and songwriter from [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-gittins-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-richman-2) She has released three albums, _Own Side Now_ (2010), _[The Stand-In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stand-In_(album) "The Stand-In (album)")_ (2013) and _[Cazimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cazimi "Cazimi")_ (2022). She also recorded two [Arctic Monkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Monkeys "Arctic Monkeys") songs for [Record Store Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_Store_Day "Record Store Day") in 2012.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-3)

## Career

Rose was previously the lead singer of Nashville indie band Save Macaulay,[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-4) on local label [Theory 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_8?action=edit&redlink=1 "Theory 8") until 2007.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-5) As a solo artist, she signed to [BMI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadcast_Music_Incorporated "Broadcast Music Incorporated") in 2008.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-6) The title track of her seven-track 2008 EP, _[Dead Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Flowers_(Catlin_Rose_EP)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dead Flowers (Catlin Rose EP)")_, is a cover of [a Rolling Stones song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Flowers_(The_Rolling_Stones_song) "Dead Flowers (The Rolling Stones song)").

Her first album, _[Own Side Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Own_Side_Now?action=edit&redlink=1 "Own Side Now")_, was released by [Names Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Names_Records?action=edit&redlink=1 "Names Records") in August 2010.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-richman-2) Her vocal performance and lyrics have led to comparisons to [Loretta Lynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loretta_Lynn "Loretta Lynn"), [Patsy Cline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patsy_Cline "Patsy Cline"),[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-gittins-1) and [Iris DeMent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_DeMent "Iris DeMent").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-7) Claire Suddath of _[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")_ magazine named _Own Side Now_ one of the Top 10 Albums of 2011, ranking it at No.7.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-8)

Her next studio album, _[The Stand-In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stand-In_(album) "The Stand-In (album)")_, was released on February 25, 2013.

After a nine-year gap, she released the album _[Cazimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cazimi "Cazimi")_ on November 18, 2022.

Also in 2022, Caitlin Rose appeared on local East Nashville indie label 3Sirens' debut compilation. The label is founded by [The Grahams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grahams "The Grahams").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-9) _"3Sirens Presents: With Love Part 1"_ is a collection of 80s and 90s cover songs, including Rose's take on Blondie's classic "One Way Or Another". Also featured on the release were local East Nashville favorites Derek Hoke, [The Grahams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grahams "The Grahams"), [Dylan LeBlanc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_LeBlanc "Dylan LeBlanc") and [Andrew Combs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs_(musician) "Andrew Combs (musician)").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-10)

## Personal life

Rose's mother is country songwriter [Liz Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Rose "Liz Rose") (who shares a Grammy nomination for writing with [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift")).[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-gittins-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-richman-2) She was born in Dallas, Texas, and her family moved to Nashville in 1994. She attended [community college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_college "Community college") in Nashville for a year before leaving to focus on music.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-richman-2) Rose is a big fan of astrology, and her 2022 album, _Cazimi_, is an astrological term for when the sun and another planet are perfectly conjoined.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caitlin_Rose#cite_note-11)
