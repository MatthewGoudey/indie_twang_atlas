Andrew Combs

**Andrew 'Buggs' Combs** (born December 31, 1977) is an American former professional [lacrosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacrosse "Lacrosse") player. He attended the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park "University of Maryland, College Park"), and played with the [Chesapeake Bayhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesapeake_Bayhawks "Chesapeake Bayhawks") of the [Major League Lacrosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Lacrosse "Major League Lacrosse").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs#cite_note-1)
Combs is currently a head lacrosse coach for the API Diamondbacks Lacrosse Club in Gambrills, Maryland for Athletic Performance Incorporated.
Combs was the Offensive Coordinator, under head coach Tony Seaman, for the [Towson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towson_University "Towson University") Men's Lacrosse team.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs#cite_note-2)

## Professional career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andrew_Combs&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Professional career")\]

**2007:** Began the season with the [Los Angeles Riptide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Riptide "Los Angeles Riptide") where he played in three games and recorded 13 goals and 1 assist. Traded to the [Washington Bayhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Bayhawks "Washington Bayhawks") and played in seven games at attack in which he scored 9 goals and 1 assist for 10 points. Finished the season with 22 total goals.

**2006:** Was traded to the Riptide from the [Rochester Rattlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester_Rattlers "Rochester Rattlers"). Was named the Offensive Player of the Week during weeks 9 and 11. Was named the Bud Light Game MVP on July 23. Played in 12 games and scored in 11, tallying 27 goals and 3 assists for 30 points while picking up 9 ground balls.

**2005:** Played in 6 games for the [Rochester Rattlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester_Rattlers "Rochester Rattlers"), scoring in all 6 games. Tallied 17 goals and had 3 assists for 20 points while recording 7 ground balls. Was named the [Major League Lacrosse Most Improved Player of the Year Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Lacrosse_Most_Improved_Player_of_the_Year_Award "Major League Lacrosse Most Improved Player of the Year Award").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs#cite_note-3) and named the Offensive Player of the Week in week 9.

**2004:** Played two games for the Baltimore Bayhawks, recording 4 ground balls and 4 shots.

**2003:** Played in 7 games as a rookie for the Baltimore Bayhawks. Tallied 8 goals and 1 assist for 9 points while picking up 9 ground balls.

Combs also plays lacrosse for the storied amateur [Mount Washington Lacrosse Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Washington_Lacrosse_Club "Mount Washington Lacrosse Club").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs#cite_note-4)

## College career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andrew_Combs&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: College career")\]

Combs played [college lacrosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_lacrosse "College lacrosse") at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park "University of Maryland, College Park") becoming the second Terrapin player ever to score 50 goals in a single season during his senior year. He earned National Player of the Week, first team All-ACC, and second team All-American honors during his senior season in which he scored 56 total points. He led the [ACC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Coast_Conference "Atlantic Coast Conference") in goals per game average with 3.13 per game. He’s currently ranked 15th all time on the Terrapins career goals list with 87.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Combs#cite_note-5)
