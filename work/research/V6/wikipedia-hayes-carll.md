Musical artist

**Joshua Hayes Carll** (born January 9, 1976),[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-3) known professionally as **Hayes Carll**, is an American singer-songwriter. A native of [The Woodlands, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Woodlands,_Texas "The Woodlands, Texas"),[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-4) his style of country, folk, and roots-oriented songwriting is noted for his plain-spoken poetry and sense of humor.

His songs have been covered by a wide range of artists, including [Kenny Chesney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Chesney "Kenny Chesney"),[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-5) [Hard Working Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_Working_Americans "Hard Working Americans"), Curtis Stigers, [Lee Ann Womack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Ann_Womack "Lee Ann Womack"), Corb Lund, Jack Ingram, Bruce Robison, [Kelly Willis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Willis "Kelly Willis"), and [Brothers Osborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_Osborne "Brothers Osborne"). He has appeared twice on the _[Austin City Limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_City_Limits "Austin City Limits")_[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-6) TV show, and has performed on _[Jools Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Later..._with_Jools_Holland "Later... with Jools Holland")_ and _[The Tonight Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show "The Tonight Show")_.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-7)[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-8)

Hayes Carll has supported several charitable organizations over the years, including the [Alzheimer’s Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzheimer%E2%80%99s_Association "Alzheimer’s Association"), Guitars for Vets, the Health Alliance for Austin Musicians (HAAM), and the Second Harvest Food Bank.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-11)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hayes_Carll&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Career")\]

### 2000–2009

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hayes_Carll&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: 2000–2009")\]

Carll spent the early years of his career performing cover songs for tips in the bars of [Crystal Beach, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Beach,_Texas "Crystal Beach, Texas"), located on the Bolivar Peninsula. In 2000, he discovered [The Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Quarter_Acoustic_Cafe "Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe") in Galveston—a small yet prestigious venue run by Wrecks Bell, the longtime bassist for [Townes Van Zandt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townes_Van_Zandt "Townes Van Zandt"). Carll began working as a bartender while also sharing his own songs, first at open mic nights and eventually as the opening act for touring artists like [Ray Wylie Hubbard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Wylie_Hubbard "Ray Wylie Hubbard") and the Sisters Morales.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-12)

In 2001, Carll independently recorded his debut album, _Flowers & Liquor_, at Willie Nelson's Pedernales Studio in Texas, with Lisa Morales serving as producer. After the album's release in 2002, Carll was voted Best New Act by the _Houston Press_ and drew comparisons with other notable Texas songwriters.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-14)

Carll recorded his second album, _Little Rock_, in Nashville with producer R.S. Field. Alongside his manager at the time, Mike Crowley, he founded HWY 87 Records to release the album, which made history as the first self-released record to reach number one on the Americana Radio Chart. The track "Down The Road Tonight" also became a regional hit.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-15) Carll signed with [Lost Highway Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_Highway_Records "Lost Highway Records") in 2006 and released his next album, _Trouble in Mind_, in 2008. It was ranked No. 60 of the year by _[The Village Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Village_Voice "The Village Voice")_.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-Pazz_+_Jop_2008_Albums-16) "She Left Me For Jesus," which appeared on _Trouble in Mind_, was the [Americana Music Association Song of the Year 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_Music_Association#Song_of_the_Year "Americana Music Association").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-Americana_Music_Honors_&_Awards-17)

In 2008, Carll founded the Stingaree Music Festival, a small music event held in [Crystal Beach, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Beach,_Texas "Crystal Beach, Texas"), named after a local restaurant where he used to wait tables. The festival ran for two years before being discontinued in 2010 following the devastation caused by [Hurricane Ike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Ike "Hurricane Ike").[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-18)

### 2010–2018

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hayes_Carll&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2010–2018")\]

Four songs by Carll appeared in the 2010 film _[Country Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Strong "Country Strong")_, three of which also appeared on the film's second soundtrack album, _[Country Strong: More Music from the Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Strong:_More_Music_from_the_Motion_Picture "Country Strong: More Music from the Motion Picture")_.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-19)

_KMAG YOYO & Other American Stories_ was released in 2011. KMAG YOYO is military slang for "Kiss my ass, guys, you're on your own."[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-sullivan-1) It received a nomination for Best Album by the [Americana Music Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_Music_Association "Americana Music Association") in 2011, and _[Spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_(magazine) "Spin (magazine)")_ voted it No. 3 in the category of "Best Country/Americana".[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-20) _[American Songwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Songwriter "American Songwriter")_ voted the song "Another Like You" the No. 1 song of 2011,[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-21) and the album No. 6.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-22) _[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")_ ranked "KMAG YOYO" at No. 46 of best singles of 2011,[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-23) and the album No. 47 on their list of "50 Country Albums Every Rock Fan Should Own."[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-24) After _KMAG_'s success, Carll left [Lost Highway Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Music_Group_Nashville "Universal Music Group Nashville").[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-varias-25) In 2015 he went into the studio with producer [Joe Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Henry "Joe Henry") to record _Lovers and Leavers_ and then released the album in April 2016 on his own Highway 87 Records to great critical acclaim.

Carll was nominated for a [Grammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award "Grammy Award") in 2016, [Best Country Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Country_Song "Best Country Song"), for "Chances Are," which was covered by [Lee Ann Womack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Ann_Womack "Lee Ann Womack").[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-:0-26)

### 2019–present

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hayes_Carll&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2019–present")\]

In 2019, he released his sixth album, [_What It Is_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_It_Is_(Hayes_Carll_album) "What It Is (Hayes Carll album)"), on [Dualtone Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dualtone_Records "Dualtone Records").[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-27)

In 2020, to stay connected during the Covid-19 pandemic, Carll launched a weekly livestream show, _Alone Together Tuesdays_. Over 68 episodes, he was often joined by [Allison Moorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allison_Moorer "Allison Moorer") and his son, Elijah. Through fan contributions, he raised money for various charities.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-28)

He re-recorded many of his songs, including duets with [Ray Wylie Hubbard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Wylie_Hubbard "Ray Wylie Hubbard") and Allison Moorer, for 2020's _[Alone Together Sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alone_Together_Sessions "Alone Together Sessions")_. The record was produced with [Darrell Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darrell_Scott "Darrell Scott"), and due to pandemic restrictions Carll recorded alone from his house while Scott recorded from his own.[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-29) His album, _[You Get It All](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Get_It_All "You Get It All")_, was released on October 29, 2021, on Dualtone Records.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-30)

In 2024, Carll and [Band of Heathens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Heathens "Band of Heathens") formed Hayes & The Heathens and released a self-titled record. The group also launched _Everybody's Somebody Fest_, a small annual music festival in [Luckenbach, TX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luckenbach,_TX "Luckenbach, TX").[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-31)

On April 23, 2025, Carll announced a new album _[We're Only Human](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We're_Only_Human_(album) "We're Only Human (album)")_ which was released on August 8, 2025, followed by a tour of the same name later in the year.[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-32)[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-33)

Carll appeared on [CBS Saturday Morning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Saturday_Morning "CBS Saturday Morning") on August 23, 2025, playing "Stay Here Awhile", "What I Will Be", and "We're Only Human".[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-34)[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayes_Carll#cite_note-35)
