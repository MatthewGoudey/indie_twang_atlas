## Scene/label facts
- Merge Records is Hiss Golden Messenger's home label since 2014 (Lateness of Dancers onward); his earlier Paradise of Bachelors/Blackmaps/self-released records (2010-2013) are already staged in lane_V4.
- Jonathan Wilson's Laurel Canyon (LA) home studio was a literal hub for the 2010s cosmic-country revival, hosting sessions with Wilco/Dawes members and later David Crosby/Graham Nash guest spots.
- Cory Hanson (Wand's frontman) records his solo albums for Drag City, distinct from Wand's In the Red/GOD? releases.
- Nashville's Echolalia/Shrunken Elvis/Rich Ruth circle (Spencer Cullum, Sean Thompson) forms a tight instrumentalist scene overlapping several C6 field-guide artists.
- Howlin Rain moved from American Recordings (Rick Rubin's label) to Ethan Miller's own Silver Current Records starting with Mansion Songs (2015).
- Garcia Peoples (NJ) are named directly for Jerry Garcia and are part of a dual-guitar-jam underground alongside Endless Boogie and the Woodsist/Woods scene.
