Ivy Tripp, third studio album by Waxahatchee, released April 7 2015 on Merge (US)/Wichita (intl). Tracks: Breathless, Under a Rock, Poison, La Loose, Stale by Noon, The Dirt, Blue, Air, <, Grey Hair, Summer of Love, Half Moon, Bonfire.
