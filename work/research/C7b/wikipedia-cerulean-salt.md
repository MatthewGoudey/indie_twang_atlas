Cerulean Salt, second studio album by Waxahatchee, released March 5 2013 on Don Giovanni Records. Tracks: Hollow Bedroom, Dixie Cups and Jars, Lips and Limbs, Blue Pt. II, Brother Bryan, Coast to Coast, Tangled Envisioning, Misery Over Dispute, Lively, Waiting, Swan Dive, Peace and Quiet, You're Damaged.
