Out in the Storm, fourth studio album by Waxahatchee, released July 14 2017 on Merge Records. Tracks: Never Been Wrong, 8 Ball, Silver, Recite Remorse, Sparks Fly, Brass Beam, Hear You, A Little More, No Question, Fade.
