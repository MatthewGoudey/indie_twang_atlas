Track listing: "Cover Me Up"; "Stockholm"; "Traveling Alone"; "Elephant"; "Flying Over Water"; "Different Days"; "Live Oak"; "Songs That She Sang in the Shower"; "New South Wales"; "Super 8"; "Yvette"; "Relatively Easy"

Artist credit: Jason Isbell
Release year: 2013
Record label: Southeastern Records
Musical style/sound: Alternative country, Americana. Recorded following a stint in rehab, with producer Dave Cobb encouraging Isbell to record live vocal takes.
Note: fourth studio album, released June 11, 2013 on Southeastern Records (Isbell's own label). Widely considered his artistic breakthrough after getting sober.
