Scene/label facts: Dear Life Records (Asheville) also releases Natalie Jane Hill, not just MJ Lenderman. Lame-O Records (Philadelphia) is now home to both Lily Seabird and Saintseneca. Burlington, VT scene (Lutalo, Lily Seabird, Fust, Charlie Hill) continues to be a hub for twangy indie folk. Ohtis (Normal, IL) is a country-rock band whose sound sits close to the C5/C2 boundary.
