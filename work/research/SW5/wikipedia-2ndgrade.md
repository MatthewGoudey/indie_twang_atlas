2nd Grade is a Philadelphia indie rock band, formed 2018 by Peter Gill; compared to Guided by Voices. Three full-length albums: Hit to Hit (2021), Easy Listening (2022), Scheduled Explosions (2024). (Label Double Double Whammy per band's Bandcamp /music listing.)
