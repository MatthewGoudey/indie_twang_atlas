Scene/genre fact: "countrygaze" is a fan-coined term (Taylor Grimes, Swim Into The Sound, Nov 2022) built around Wednesday's own Bandcamp tags ("rock, country-gaze, indie, lo-fi, shoegaze, Asheville" on Twin Plagues). Wednesday guitarist/bassist Xandy Chelmis has also used the term "countrygaze" in a Consequence interview. Bandcamp tag "country-gaze" surfaces Grave Saddles, Wednesday side projects (scoutmaster, Nash To Stoudemire), and not much else with real discographies. Grave Saddles guitarist Chris Broyles frames it as a subset of a broader "post-country" movement tracing to Acetone; Stereogum's "Intro to the New Wave of American Shoegaze" playlist (Eli Enis) grouped Grave Saddles, Greg Freeman, Hotline TNT, feeble little horse, and Wednesday together, though only some of those (Grave Saddles, Wednesday, Dutch Interior, Shallowater) actually carry country song structure rather than pure shoegaze.

Rejected/excluded from C4 (see reply for full reasoning):
- Wednesday (all albums) - anchor artist, explicitly out of scope per assignment.
- Hotline TNT (Cartwheel 2023, Raspberry Moon 2025, etc.) - pure shoegaze/dream-pop, no country-song structure or twang; better understood as part of the (excluded) 2020s non-twang shoegaze revival.
- Feeble Little Horse (Modern Tourism, Girl with Fish, Hayday, bitknot) - noise-pop, not twang/country; Julia's War Recordings (early label) is worth noting as a scene label but the band itself doesn't fit C4.
- Squirrel Flower - hazy indie folk/dream pop, not noise-wall-scale or country-structured; closer to C8 or Context.
- Wild Pink - heartland/dream-folk, not noise-wall scale even on "louder" records; referenced only as an influence-adjacent act in the countrygaze discourse.
