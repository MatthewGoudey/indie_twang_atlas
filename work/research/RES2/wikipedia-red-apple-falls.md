Red Apple Falls (Smog, 1997, Drag City). Tracklist: The Morning Paper, Blood Red Bird, Red Apples, I Was a Stranger, To Be of Use, Red Apple Falls, Ex-Con, Inspirational, Finer Days. "Cold Blooded Old Times" is NOT on this album (it is on Knock Knock, 1999). Row also had "Finer Points" which is not a real track; correct title is "Finer Days".
