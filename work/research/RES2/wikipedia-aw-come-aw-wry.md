Aw Come Aw Wry (Phosphorescent, 2005, Misra Records, released June 7 2005). Tracklist: Not a Heel; Aw Come Aw Wry #5; Joe Tex, These Taming Blues; Aw Come Aw Wry #6; I Am a Full Grown Man (I Will Lay in the Grass All Day); Dead Heart; Aw Come Aw Wry #3; South (Of America); Lost Name; Endless Pt.1; Endless Pt.2; Nowhere Road, Georgia, February 21, 2005. "My Dove, My Lamb" is NOT on this album (it is from Pride, 2007).
