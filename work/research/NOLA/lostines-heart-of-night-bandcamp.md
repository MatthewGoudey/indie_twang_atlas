Released December 2, 2022 on Gar Hole Records (Fayetteville, AR) and Mashed Potato Records. Split EP: The Lostines contribute 'A Tear' and 'Last Night'; Nick Shoulders & the Okay Crawdad contribute two tracks. Both acts came up in the New Orleans DIY country/Americana scene around Mashed Potato Studio (co-founded by Sam Doores).
