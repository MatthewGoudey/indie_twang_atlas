Released April 26, 2024 on Gar Hole Records. Tracks incl. 'A Tear', 'Full Moon Night', 'Neon Lights', 'After Party', 'Come Back To My Arms', 'Southwest Texas'. Debut full-length; features Sam Doores (The Deslondes), Ross Farbe (Videoage), Howe Pearson, K.C. Jones & Joel Savoy (Cajun fiddles). Recorded in New Orleans; blends 1960s girl-group harmonies, Cajun fiddle, pedal steel, baritone guitar.
