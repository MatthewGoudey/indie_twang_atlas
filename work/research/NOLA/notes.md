## The Deslondes members & pre-history
- Members (Wikipedia): Dan Cutler, Sam Doores, Riley Downing, Howe Pearson, John James Tourville. Past member: Cameron Snyder (drums/keys, left 2022 per Garden & Gun).
- Pre-Deslondes: Sam Doores + Riley Downing & The Tumbleweeds, "Holy Cross Blues" (2012, self-released) — confirmed via Facebook (Deslondes page), Discogs, Spotify, Louisiana Music Factory, Bandcamp (thedeslondes.bandcamp.com).
- Deslondes discography per Wikipedia: The Deslondes (2015), Hurry Home (2017), Ways & Means (2022) [all 3 already staged], Roll It Out (2024, New West) [NOT staged — added], Don't Let It Die, Vol. 1 (2026, New West) — a COVERS album (Swamp Dogg, Johnny Cash, Pat Reedy, Hurricane Smith songs per New West product page) — skipped as not an original-songs release.
- Sam Doores solo: "Sam Doores" LP, New West Records, released March 1 2020 (bandcamp.com samdooresmusic, matches New West product listing & Bluegrass Situation article).
- Riley Downing solo: "Start It Over" (2021, New West Records) — confirmed via New West product page, Spotify, Reddit discussion (release date May 14, 2021, produced by Andrija Tokic & John James Tourville).
- Dan Cutler: no verifiable solo LP found (Discogs artist page shows only a 2009 Los Po-Boy-Citos 7" and a Lostines EP credit, not a solo release) — SKIPPED.
