**Horse Feathers** is an American [indie folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_folk "Indie folk") band from [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon"), United States.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-gstraight-2)

## Biography

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Horse_Feathers_(band)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Biography")\]

After fronting several rock bands in his native Idaho, singer/songwriter Justin Ringle moved to Portland, Oregon in 2004 and began focusing on acoustic music, playing open mics regularly under the moniker Horse Feathers. In 2005, multi-instrumentalist [Peter Broderick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Broderick "Peter Broderick") heard a couple of Ringle's demos and offered to help flesh them out. In February 2006 the duo went into Skyler Norwood's Miracle Lake studios and recorded their debut album, _Words Are Dead_, which was released that September on Portland label, Lucky Madison. Later that year, Peter's sister [Heather Broderick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Broderick "Heather Broderick") joined the group on cello and by 2007, Horse Feathers began featuring a rotating cast of instrumentalists backing Ringle's guitar and vocals.

[![A color photograph of a four-person band performing on stage.](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/Horse_Feathers_band.jpg/250px-Horse_Feathers_band.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Horse_Feathers_band.jpg) Horse Feathers live in 2010.

Ringle and the Brodericks returned to Miracle Lake studios in the fall and winter of 2007 and recorded _House With No Home_, which was released on [Kill Rock Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Rock_Stars "Kill Rock Stars") in the spring of 2008. Around that time, Peter Broderick left the country to pursue music in Europe, and Nathan Crockett was enlisted to cover violin duties. Several months later, Heather Broderick also left the group to pursue other musical projects and cellist Catherine Odell came on board. The current line-up was rounded out at the beginning of 2009 when Sam Cooper joined as a multi-instrumentalist. As a four piece, the group has been touring extensively and released _Thistled Spring_ on Kill Rock Stars, on April 20, 2010.

The band's fourth album, _Cynic's New Year_, was released in 2012.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-gstraight-2) The album charted on the [Billboard charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts") even though it sold fewer copies than their previous record.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-Killrockstars.com-3) On October 12, 2014, [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR") debuted Horse Feathers' fifth album entitled _So It Is with Us_[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-4) which was released on October 21, 2014 on Kill Rock Stars on CD, vinyl, and digital download.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-5) The album was recorded in a rural Oregon barn following a hiatus from Ringle, and is unique in that it features both drums and bass.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-Killrockstars.com-3) On March 2, 2018, [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR") debuted Horse Feathers' new single "Without Applause", taken from the band's upcoming sixth album, 'Appreciation', which was released on May 4, 2018 via Kill Rock Stars and featured the new rhythm section of J. Tom Hnatow and Robby Cosenza.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Feathers_(band)#cite_note-6)
