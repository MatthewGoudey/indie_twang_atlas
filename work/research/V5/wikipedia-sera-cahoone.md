Musical artist

**Sera Cahoone** (born August 4, 1975) is an American singer-songwriter from [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "Seattle"), Washington. Cahoone's music combines elements of classic [country-western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country-western "Country-western") and modern [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") and [lo-fi music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi_music "Lo-fi music"). She is also a drummer, most notably having played drums for the bands [Carissa's Wierd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carissa's_Wierd "Carissa's Wierd") and [Band of Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Horses "Band of Horses").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NoDep-2008-1)

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Cahoone is the daughter of Bill Cahoone, a Rocky Mountain dynamite salesman, and Judy Cahoone (née Ahrendts), a business executive.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-CSMCareerDay-2009-2) Cahoone said that she was raised by a single mother.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NoDep-2008-1) She has a sister named Kal Cahoone[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-Westword-Kal-2010-3) and brother named Nathan Cahoone, both of whom are also musicians.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-DenverPost-OATDIL-2008-4)

Cahoone grew up in [Littleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littleton,_Colorado "Littleton, Colorado"), Colorado.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-Subpop-Cahoone-Bio-5) She attended and graduated from [Columbine High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbine_High_School "Columbine High School").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-DenverPost-OATDIL-2008-4)

Cahoone got her musical start on the drums at twelve years old. She had taken up drumming to combat anxiety, and found she had a talent and affinity for the instrument. She also played her brother's guitar and sang, but not in public at that time. Her first stage performance came in a North [Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver "Denver") bar called Ziggie's Saloon (near the Oriental Theater). She played drums behind a group of bluesmen at an open mic night.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-DenverPost-OATDIL-2008-4)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")\]

### Drumming career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Drumming career")\]

In 1998, Cahoone moved to [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "Seattle"), Washington, when the snowboarding shop where she worked in Colorado opened a retail store in Washington state.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-DenverPost-OATDIL-2008-4) She worked there for two years before quitting. She subsequently worked in a coffee shop and focused on playing music.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NoDep-2008-1)

Cahoone was the drummer in the band [Carissa's Wierd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carissa's_Wierd "Carissa's Wierd") until they broke up in 2003. After that, Cahoone played drums with various bands including Los Angeles singer-songwriter [Patrick Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Park "Patrick Park") and most notably, [Band of Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Horses "Band of Horses").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NPR-SelfTitled-2006-6) Cahoone has also played drums with Panda and Angel, and Betsy Olson. She recorded selected tracks of the critically acclaimed Band of Horses debut record, _[Everything All The Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_All_The_Time "Everything All The Time"),_ as a session player.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-DenverPost-OATDIL-2008-4)

### Solo recording career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Solo recording career")\]

#### _Sera Cahoone_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Sera Cahoone")\]

In 2006, Cahoone began to release solo work. Her first album was a self-released, self-titled record called _Sera Cahoone,_ which was praised by indie-rock radio station [KEXP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KEXP "KEXP") along with [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NPR-SelfTitled-2006-6) Seattle indie newspaper _[The Stranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stranger_(newspaper) "The Stranger (newspaper)")_ called Sera's debut "a breathtaking collection of sad and dusky songs that reveal an artist of remarkable depth as well as a truly stunning voice."[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-Stranger-SelfTitled-2006-7)

#### _Only as the Day Is Long_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Only as the Day Is Long")\]

In 2007, Cahoone was signed to the Seattle label [Sub Pop Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop_Records "Sub Pop Records")[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-KEXP-InStudio-2006-8) which was home to former Carissa's Wierd bandmates [Ben Bridwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Bridwell "Ben Bridwell") ( [Band of Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Horses "Band of Horses")) and later [Mat Brooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat_Brooke "Mat Brooke") ( [Grand Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Archives "Grand Archives")). Cahoone's second album, _Only As The Day Is Long_, was released in 2008.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-NPR-TinyDesk-2008-9)

#### _Deer Creek Canyon_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Deer Creek Canyon")\]

Sera and her band recorded her second Sub Pop record, _Deer Creek Canyon,_ in February 2012 at [Bear Creek Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bear_Creek_Studio "Bear Creek Studio") in [Woodinville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodinville,_Washington "Woodinville, Washington"), [Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_(state) "Washington (state)"), over a two-week period with Los Angeles producer [Thom Monahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_Monahan "Thom Monahan") ( [Devendra Banhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devendra_Banhart "Devendra Banhart"), [Vetiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vetiver "Vetiver")). Additional vocals and tracking were done in Los Angeles over several weeks in early March. The record was released September 25, 2012.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-Exclaim-DCC-2012-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-Pitchfork-DCC-2012-11)

#### _From Where I Started_

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: From Where I Started")\]

In 2017 it was announced that Sera's fourth album titled _From Where I Started_ will be self-released on March 24, 2017. Recorded at Flora Recording and Playback in Portland Oregon, _From Where I Started_ was co-produced by Sera Cahoone and John Morgan Askew ( [Neko Case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Case "Neko Case"), [Laura Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Gibson "Laura Gibson"), [Alela Diane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alela_Diane "Alela Diane")). Askew brought together key Portland musicians like [Rob Burger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Burger "Rob Burger") ( [Iron and Wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_and_Wine "Iron and Wine"), [Lucinda Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucinda_Williams "Lucinda Williams")), [Dave Depper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Depper "Dave Depper") ( [Death Cab For Cutie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Cab_For_Cutie "Death Cab For Cutie")) and [Annalisa Tornfelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annalisa_Tornfelt?action=edit&redlink=1 "Annalisa Tornfelt (page does not exist)") ( [Black Prairie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Prairie "Black Prairie")) with Cahoone's Seattle bandmates – [Jeff Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Fielder?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jeff Fielder (page does not exist)") ( [Mark Lanegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Lanegan "Mark Lanegan"), [Amy Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Ray "Amy Ray")) and Jason Kardong ( [Son Volt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Volt "Son Volt"), [Jay Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Farrar "Jay Farrar")).

### Touring

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Touring")\]

Cahoone has toured with [Son Volt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Volt "Son Volt"), [Matt Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Costa "Matt Costa"), [Band of Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_Horses "Band of Horses"), [Grand Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Archives "Grand Archives"), [Sea Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_Wolf_(band) "Sea Wolf (band)"), [Patrick Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Park "Patrick Park"), [Tift Merritt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tift_Merritt "Tift Merritt"), [Beachwood Sparks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beachwood_Sparks "Beachwood Sparks"), [Fruit Bats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Bats_(band) "Fruit Bats (band)"), [Ben Gibbard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Gibbard "Ben Gibbard"), [Jay Farrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Farrar "Jay Farrar"), [Gregory Alan Isakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Alan_Isakov "Gregory Alan Isakov"), [Blitzen Trapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blitzen_Trapper "Blitzen Trapper"), [Mason Jennings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_Jennings "Mason Jennings"), [Kathleen Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Edwards "Kathleen Edwards"), and [Joe Pug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Pug "Joe Pug"). She has opened for [Lucinda Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucinda_Williams "Lucinda Williams"), [Okkervil River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okkervil_River "Okkervil River"), [Mary Gauthier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Gauthier "Mary Gauthier"), and [The Indigo Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Indigo_Girls "The Indigo Girls").

Cahoone and her bandmate Jason Kardong were the first people to play music on the roof of Seattle's [Space Needle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Needle "Space Needle") as part of the [Sub Pop Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_Pop_Records "Sub Pop Records") Silver Jubilee Celebration on July 11, 2013. Their performance was broadcast live on radio station [KEXP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KEXP "KEXP") and streamed around the world.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-12)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sera_Cahoone&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

In August 2015, Cahoone became engaged to [Megan Rapinoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Rapinoe "Megan Rapinoe"), a midfielder/winger for the [United States Women's National Soccer Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Women's_National_Soccer_Team "United States Women's National Soccer Team") and the [Seattle Reign FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Reign_FC "Seattle Reign FC") of the [National Women's Soccer League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Women's_Soccer_League "National Women's Soccer League").[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-FoxSports-Rapinoe-2015-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-HuffPo-Engaged-2015-14) In January 2017, Rapinoe stated their wedding plans were on hold.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sera_Cahoone#cite_note-15)
