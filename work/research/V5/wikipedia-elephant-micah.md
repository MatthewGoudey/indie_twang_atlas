Musical artist

**Elephant Micah** is a musical band or stage name of southern [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana") songwriter and multi-instrumentalist **Joseph O'Connell**. Recording mostly at home, O’Connell has produced an eclectic body of work since beginning the project in 2000. Elephant Micah's inconsistently available music has become a [cult favorite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cult_favorite "Cult favorite") among some [lo-fi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi_music "Lo-fi music")/ [indie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_(music) "Indie (music)") and [folk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_rock "Folk rock") audiences.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Micah#cite_note-1)

O'Connell has released recordings with [BlueSanct Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlueSanct_Records "BlueSanct Records"), the experimental-leaning [Time-Lag Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-Lag_Records "Time-Lag Records"), and a number of other very small labels. In addition, he has issued several short-run CDs on his own [LRRC (Luddite Rural Recording Cooperative)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LRRC_(Luddite_Rural_Recording_Cooperative) "LRRC (Luddite Rural Recording Cooperative)") imprint,[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Micah#cite_note-2) also home to music by collaborator [Jason Henn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Henn?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jason Henn (page does not exist)").

In 2005, Elephant Micah shared bills with [Jason Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Molina "Jason Molina")'s Magnolia Electric Company during a string of shows in the United Kingdom.
