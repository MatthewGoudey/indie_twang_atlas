[Gillian Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillian_Welch "Gillian Welch") (born October 2, 1967) is a singer-songwriter from [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee"). Welch plays with her music partner [David Rawlings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rawlings "David Rawlings"), whom she met while they were both students at The [Berklee College of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berklee_College_of_Music "Berklee College of Music"). The first two Gillian Welch albums were released through the label [Almo Sounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almo_Sounds "Almo Sounds"). After [Universal Music Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Music_Group "Universal Music Group") purchased Almo, Welch started her own label, Acony Records.

In her career, Welch has collaborated with several artists, appearing as a musician, songwriter, and vocalist.

The artist in all recordings is listed as "Gillian Welch" except where noted.
