Musical artist

**Anaïs Mitchell** ([/əˈneɪ.ɪs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English"); born March 26, 1981) is an American singer-songwriter, musician, and playwright.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-1) She has released eight studio albums throughout her career, including _[Hadestown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadestown_(album) "Hadestown (album)")_ (2010), _[Young Man in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Man_in_America "Young Man in America")_ (2012),[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-queen-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-3) _[Child Ballads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_Ballads_(album) "Child Ballads (album)")_ (2013), and _[Anaïs Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell_(album) "Anaïs Mitchell (album)")_ (2022).[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-4)

With theatre director [Rachel Chavkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Chavkin "Rachel Chavkin"), Mitchell developed _Hadestown_ into a [stage musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadestown "Hadestown"). It premiered [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") in 2016,[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-5) and made its [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre") debut three years later to critical acclaim.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-7) _Hadestown_ won eight awards at the [73rd Annual Tony Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/73rd_Tony_Awards "73rd Tony Awards"), including [Best Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Musical "Tony Award for Best Musical"). For her work, Mitchell won the [Tony Award for Best Original Score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Original_Score "Tony Award for Best Original Score") and was nominated for [Best Book of a Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Book_of_a_Musical "Tony Award for Best Book of a Musical"). She additionally won the [Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Musical_Theater_Album "Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album"). In 2020, Mitchell published her first book, _Working on a Song: The Lyrics of Hadestown_, and was named by _[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")_ magazine as one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-8)

Mitchell is a member of the band [Bonny Light Horseman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonny_Light_Horseman_(band) "Bonny Light Horseman (band)"), whose [self-titled debut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonny_Light_Horseman_(album) "Bonny Light Horseman (album)") was released in 2020. The band's second album, _Golden Rolling Holy_, was released in 2022,[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-9) followed by _[Keep Me on Your Mind/See You Free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keep_Me_on_Your_Mind/See_You_Free "Keep Me on Your Mind/See You Free")_ in 2024.

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Mitchell's father, a novelist and college professor, named her after author [Anaïs Nin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Nin "Anaïs Nin").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-wears_the_trousers-10) She grew up on Treleven farm[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-11) in [Addison County, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addison_County,_Vermont "Addison County, Vermont") and graduated from [Mount Abraham Union High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Abraham_Union_High_School "Mount Abraham Union High School"). She was raised [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaker "Quaker").[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-12) Her mother was Deputy Secretary of Vermont's Agency of Human Services.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-13) After traveling to the Middle East, Europe and Latin America as a child, she attended [Middlebury College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlebury_College "Middlebury College"), where she majored in [political science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science "Political science") and graduated in 2004.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-14)[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-:0-15)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")\]

She began writing her first songs at the age of 17, around 1998. Just five years later Mitchell won the New Folk award in 2003, when she was 22, at the [Kerrville Folk Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerrville_Folk_Festival "Kerrville Folk Festival").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-16) Her album _[Hymns for the Exiled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymns_for_the_Exiled "Hymns for the Exiled")_ was released on Chicago's [Waterbug Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterbug_Records "Waterbug Records") label in 2004.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-17) This recording attracted the attention of singer-songwriter [Ani DiFranco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ani_DiFranco "Ani DiFranco"), who signed her to the [Righteous Babe Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Righteous_Babe_Records "Righteous Babe Records") label.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-:0-15)

In 2006, Mitchell debuted a draft of her "folk opera" _Hadestown_, which she wrote in collaboration with arranger [Michael Chorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chorney "Michael Chorney") and director Ben T. Matchstick.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-18) A revised version of _Hadestown_ was staged in 2007. Her third album, _[The Brightness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Brightness "The Brightness"),_ was released that same year on [Righteous Babe Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Righteous_Babe_Records "Righteous Babe Records").[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-19)

Her album _[Hadestown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadestown_(album) "Hadestown (album)"),_ produced by Todd Sickafoose, was released in spring 2010 to favorable reviews.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-20)[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-21) Described as "the story of Orpheus and Eurydice set in post-apocalyptic Depression-era America,[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-22) the album includes guest appearances by [Ani DiFranco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ani_DiFranco "Ani DiFranco"), [Greg Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Brown_(folk_musician) "Greg Brown (folk musician)"), [Justin Vernon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Vernon "Justin Vernon") of [Bon Iver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Iver "Bon Iver"), Ben Knox Miller of [The Low Anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Low_Anthem "The Low Anthem"), and The Haden Triplets ( [Petra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Haden "Petra Haden"), [Rachel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Haden "Rachel Haden"), and [Tanya Haden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Haden "Tanya Haden")).[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-23)

Mitchell continued quietly working on a stage version of _Hadestown_ while also writing and recording new material. In early 2012, she released _[Young Man in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Man_in_America "Young Man in America")_ on Wilderland Records.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-24)[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-25) Mitchell opened the [North American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American "North American") leg of [Bon Iver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Iver "Bon Iver")'s autumn 2012 tour, which included two sold-out shows at [Radio City Music Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_City_Music_Hall "Radio City Music Hall").[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-26) The album was largely praised by critics as "genre-defying" and her "second consecutive masterpiece."[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-27)

In late 2012, Mitchell completed recording seven songs from the collection of [Child Ballads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_Ballads "Child Ballads"), compiled by [Francis James Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_James_Child "Francis James Child"), with fellow musician Jefferson Hamer.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-28) The [album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_Ballads_(album) "Child Ballads (album)"), produced by [Gary Paczosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Paczosa "Gary Paczosa"), was released in February 2013,[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-new_album-29) winning a BBC Radio 2 Folk Award for Best Traditional Song.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-30) This was followed in 2014 by _[xoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xoa "Xoa"),_ for which Mitchell re-recorded a number of her older songs using only guitar and vocals. This stripped back album included some songs from _[Hadestown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadestown_(album) "Hadestown (album)")_ which were recorded for the first time in Mitchell's own voice, as well as three brand new songs.

In summer 2016, the newly expanded theatrical version of _[Hadestown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadestown_(musical) "Hadestown (musical)")_ opened at [New York Theatre Workshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Theatre_Workshop "New York Theatre Workshop")[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-31) with Vogue magazine predicting that " _Hadestown_ will be your next musical theatre obsession".[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-32) The following year, it received its Canadian premiere at The Citadel Theatre, Edmonton, and in April 2018, London's [National Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_National_Theatre "Royal National Theatre") announced that it would present a three-month run during the winter ahead of the show's Broadway transfer.[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-33) _Hadestown_ opened on Broadway at the [Walter Kerr Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Kerr_Theatre "Walter Kerr Theatre") on April 17, 2019.

In 2019, Mitchell was appearing as part of a three-piece "supergroup" called [Bonny Light Horseman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonny_Light_Horseman_(band) "Bonny Light Horseman (band)"), consisting of herself, [Eric D. Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_D._Johnson "Eric D. Johnson") of [Fruit Bats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Bats_(band) "Fruit Bats (band)") and guitarist [Josh Kaufman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Kaufman_(musician) "Josh Kaufman (musician)").[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-34) The group's self-titled debut album was released on January 24, 2020.

In June 2021, American supergroup [Big Red Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Red_Machine_(band) "Big Red Machine (band)") announced their second studio album, _[How Long Do You Think It's Gonna Last?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_Long_Do_You_Think_It's_Gonna_Last%3F "How Long Do You Think It's Gonna Last?")_, which features Mitchell's guest vocals in three of its tracks: "Latter Days", "Phoenix", and "New Auburn".[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-:3-35)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

While at Middlebury College, Mitchell met Noah Hahn, whom she married in 2006.[\[36\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-36) They have two daughters, Ramona and Rosetta.[\[37\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-37)

## Reception

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Reception")\]

Mitchell has received favorable reviews on her musical style, sound and performance. An article in _[Acoustic Guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_Guitar_(magazine) "Acoustic Guitar (magazine)")_ magazine calls Mitchell "fearlessly emotive" and compares her to [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan"), [Leonard Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Cohen "Leonard Cohen"), and [Gillian Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillian_Welch "Gillian Welch").[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-38) The UK's [_Independent_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent") newspaper called her "the most engaging, and in some ways, most original artist currently working in the field of new American folk music"[\[39\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-39) _The New York Times_ noted that "Ms Mitchell's songs address contemporary angst with uncanny vision" and called her "a formidable songwriting talent".[\[40\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana%C3%AFs_Mitchell#cite_note-40)
