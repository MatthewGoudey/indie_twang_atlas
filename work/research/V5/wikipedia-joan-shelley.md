Musical artist

**Joan Shelley** (born 1985) is an American [indie folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_folk "Indie folk") musician from [Louisville, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky "Louisville, Kentucky"), United States.

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Joan_Shelley&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Career")\]

Shelley has released nine solo studio recordings. Her second album, _Ginko_, and third album, _Farthest Field_ (with [Daniel Martin Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Martin_Moore "Daniel Martin Moore")), were released in 2012 on Ol Kentuck.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-woub-1) In 2014, Shelley released her fourth album, _Electric Ursa_, on No Quarter Records.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-pitchfork-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-rollingstone-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-stereogum-4) In 2015 she released her fifth album, _Over and Even_, also on No Quarter.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-pitchfork1-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-stereogum1-6) In 2017, Shelley released her eponymous sixth album.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-rollingstonemagazine-7)[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-theindependent-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-OfficialSite_recordings-9) Her seventh album, _[Like the River Loves the Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Like_the_River_Loves_the_Sea "Like the River Loves the Sea")_, was released in 2019.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-10)

No Quarter released Shelley's eighth album, _The Spur_, on 24 June 2022. Upon its release, the album received critical acclaim.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-11)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-12)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Joan_Shelley&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

Shelley attended the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia "University of Georgia"). She decided to go to the university due to [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia")'s strong music culture.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-Aroesti-13)

Shelley frequently works with her husband, guitarist and musicologist Nathan Salsburg.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-WTF-14) Shelley and Salsburg had a daughter in 2021.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Shelley#cite_note-Aroesti-13)
