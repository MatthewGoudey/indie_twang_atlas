_**Woodland**_ is the seventh studio album by [Gillian Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillian_Welch "Gillian Welch"), the third studio album by [David Rawlings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rawlings "David Rawlings") (his fifth including his studio work with Dave Rawlings Machine), and the second studio album to be credited to the pair as a duo. Released by Acony Records on August 23, 2024, it is the first album of original material from Welch since 2011's _[The Harrow & the Harvest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Harrow_&_the_Harvest "The Harrow & the Harvest")_, and from Rawlings since 2017's _Poor David's Almanack_. Their 2020 album, _[All the Good Times (Are Past & Gone)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_the_Good_Times_(Are_Past_&_Gone) "All the Good Times (Are Past & Gone)")_, consisted entirely of [covers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_version "Cover version") of preexisting songs.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_(album)#cite_note-NDWoodland-2)

The album is named after Welch and Rawlings' [Woodland Sound Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_Sound_Studios "Woodland Sound Studios") in [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee"), which was destroyed by [a tornado in 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Nashville_tornado_outbreak "2020 Nashville tornado outbreak"). The artists stated in a press release that the songs on _Woodland_ are "a swirl of contradictions, emptiness, fullness, joy, grief, destruction, \[and\] permanence."[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_(album)#cite_note-PitchforkWoodland-3)

The album won the 2025 [Grammy Award for Best Folk Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Folk_Album "Grammy Award for Best Folk Album").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_(album)#cite_note-4)

## Singles

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Woodland_(album)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Singles")\]

The first single from the album, "Empty Trainload of Sky", was released on July 19, 2024.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_(album)#cite_note-JambandsWoodland-5) It was followed by "Hashtag" on August 9.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_(album)#cite_note-6)
