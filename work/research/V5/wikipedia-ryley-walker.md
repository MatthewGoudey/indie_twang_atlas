Musical artist

**Ryley Walker** (born July 21, 1989) is an American singer-songwriter and guitarist from [Rockford, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockford,_Illinois "Rockford, Illinois").[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-Hopper2014-5)

## Biography

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ryley_Walker&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Biography")\]

Walker grew up in a working-class family in Rockford.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-Hopper2014-5)

Walker and critics have cited the band [Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_(band) "Genesis (band)") and singer-songwriter [Nick Drake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Drake "Nick Drake") as early influences.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-RyleyWalker_Guardian2021-04-05-6)

Walker began his career in Chicago's independent music scene after moving there in the early 2010s, releasing several cassette [EPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") and a vinyl EP.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-7) In 2014, he released his debut album for the [Tompkins Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tompkins_Square_Records "Tompkins Square Records") label, followed by _[Primrose Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primrose_Green "Primrose Green"),_ released on Dead Oceans in 2015.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-8) Backing musicians Walker employed on _Primrose Green_ include several noted Chicago jazz and experimental musicians, such as [Fred Lonberg-Holm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Lonberg-Holm "Fred Lonberg-Holm").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-9)

Also in 2015, Walker released an instrumental album, recorded in collaboration with fellow Chicago musician Bill MacKay, entitled _Land of Plenty_. It was recorded live during a January 2015 residency at The Whistler nightclub in Chicago. The songs on the album were taken from the last two shows of the residency, on January 25 and 30. MacKay's guitar was recorded on the left channel, and Walker's guitar was recorded on the right channel. Their instruments converge in the center. MacKay played 6-string guitar, [requinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requinto_guitar "Requinto guitar"), and glass slide, while Walker played 6 & 12-string guitars. The album's sound was described as fingerstyle ballads, psychedelic waltzes, and raga-inspired blues.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-whistlerchicago.com-10) The duo's second album, _Spiderbeetlebee_, was released by Drag City in 2017.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-11) MacKay was a recurring member of Walker's live band between 2017 and 2021.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-12)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-14) He also played electric guitar on Walker's 2018 album _[Deafman Glance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafman_Glance "Deafman Glance")_.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-15)

On August 19, 2016, Walker released his fourth solo album, _Golden Sings That Have Been Sung_, which was yet another change in his evolving sound.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-16) His fifth is _Deafman Glance_, released in May 2018. Walker released _The Lillywhite Sessions_, an album of covers of the aforementioned Dave Matthews Band album. In 2021, Walker released _[Deep Fried Grandeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Fried_Grandeur "Deep Fried Grandeur")_, a live album with the [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo "Tokyo") band [Kikagaku Moyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kikagaku_Moyo "Kikagaku Moyo"), and _Course in Fable_, his sixth solo album of original music, both on his own Husky Pants Records label.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-17)[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-RyleyWalker_SevenDays2021-06-30-18)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-RyleyWalker_Guardian2021-04-05-6)

Walker has, as of 2021, released two albums with Chicago-based jazz drummer [Charles Rumback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Rumback?action=edit&redlink=1 "Charles Rumback (page does not exist)")\[ [de](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles%20Rumback "de:Charles Rumback")\]. The first, _Cannots_, was released by Dead Oceans in 2016. In November 2019, the Thrill Jockey released _Little Common Twist._

As of 2021, Walker had gotten sober and had moved from New York City to Vermont.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-RyleyWalker_SevenDays2021-06-30-18)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-RyleyWalker_Guardian2021-04-05-6)
As of 2022, Walker had moved back to New York.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker#cite_note-19)
