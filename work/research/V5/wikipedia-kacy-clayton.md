**Kacy & Clayton** is a Canadian folk/roots duo originating from [Wood Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_Mountain,_Saskatchewan "Wood Mountain, Saskatchewan"), [Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatchewan "Saskatchewan").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-1) The duo consists of second cousins Clayton Linthicum on guitar and Kacy Anderson on vocals.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-2)

They released their second album _Strange Country_ in 2015,[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-3) In 2016, Kacy & Clayton signed to [New West Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_West_Records "New West Records"), which rereleased _Strange Country_ that year.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-4)

The duo released their follow-up album, _The Siren’s Song_, in August 2017 via New West Records.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-5) After having toured with [Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco") for their previous album, _The Siren’s Song_ was produced and recorded by [Jeff Tweedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Tweedy "Jeff Tweedy") in his studio The Loft in Chicago.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-6) The album was supported with a tour as opening act for [The Decemberists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Decemberists "The Decemberists").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-7)

## Awards

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kacy_%26_Clayton&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Awards")\]

The band received two [Canadian Folk Music Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Folk_Music_Award "Canadian Folk Music Award") nominations at the [10th Canadian Folk Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Canadian_Folk_Music_Awards "10th Canadian Folk Music Awards") in 2014, for New/Emerging Artist of the Year and Young Performer of the Year. They won the Young Performer award.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-9)

In 2017, the band's album _The Siren's Song_ was a shortlisted [Juno Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award "Juno Award") finalist for [Juno Award for Contemporary Roots Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award_for_Contemporary_Roots_Album_of_the_Year "Juno Award for Contemporary Roots Album of the Year").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-10)

In 2018, _The Siren's Song_ was a longlisted nominee for the [2018 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Polaris_Music_Prize "2018 Polaris Music Prize").[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacy_%26_Clayton#cite_note-11)
