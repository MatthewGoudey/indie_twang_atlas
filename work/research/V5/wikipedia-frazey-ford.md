Musical artist

**Frazey Obadiah Ford** (born February 11, 1973) is a Canadian singer-songwriter and actress. She joined the [Be Good Tanyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be_Good_Tanyas "Be Good Tanyas") in their first year. Her solo debut _[Obadiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obadiah_(album) "Obadiah (album)")_ was released on [Nettwerk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nettwerk "Nettwerk") on July 20, 2010.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-province_15jun2010-1)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dd/Frazey_Ford_2011.jpg/250px-Frazey_Ford_2011.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Frazey_Ford_2011.jpg) Frazey Ford performing live in Germany, 2011

Jenny Charlesworth of _[The Georgia Straight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Georgia_Straight "The Georgia Straight")_ wrote that a few of the songs "swing with a mellow neo-soul beat enlivened by buttery vocals", and noted influences from [rhythm and blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_and_blues "Rhythm and blues") singers [Ann Peebles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Peebles "Ann Peebles"), [Roberta Flack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Flack "Roberta Flack"), and [Donny Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donny_Hathaway "Donny Hathaway").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-straight30July2010-2) Ford also credits her free-spirited parents (her father was an American [conscientious objector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscientious_objector "Conscientious objector") who moved to Canada), and being a mother has had a strong influence on her songwriting.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-ott_cit19July2010-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-vcJuly282010-4)

_Obadiah_ takes its name from Ford's middle name, Obadiah. When she was born, her parents asked her brothers to choose her middle name; they decided to name her after their pet cat Obadiah that had recently run away.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-bbcRadioScotlandOctober082010-5)

Ford was raised in [Castlegar, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlegar,_British_Columbia "Castlegar, British Columbia").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-vcJuly282010-4) She currently resides in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver "Vancouver").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazey_Ford#cite_note-ott_cit19July2010-3)
