Musical artist

**Barbara Josephine Bulat** (born April 13, 1984),[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-1) known professionally as **Basia Bulat** ([/ˈbɒʃəbuːˈlɒt/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English")),[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-CBCAll-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-LFPHeart-3) is a Canadian [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music") singer-songwriter. She is known for performing with an [autoharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoharp "Autoharp").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-JamAutoharp-4)

## Early life and education

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life and education")\]

Bulat grew up in [Etobicoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etobicoke "Etobicoke"), Ontario,[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-LFPHeart-3) where her mother was a music teacher[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-JamHeart-5) who taught piano and guitar. She is of Polish origin and a member of the [Canadian Polonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish-Canadians "Polish-Canadians").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-SOCAN-6) She has said the radio at home was permanently tuned to an [oldies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldies "Oldies") station.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Straight-7) "I don't think I realised the radio had more than one station until I was 11 or 12," she says.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-8)

She attended the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario "University of Western Ontario") in the city of [London, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London,_Ontario "London, Ontario") where she received a degree in English;[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Western-9) she also took some classes with Olenka Krakus of the band [Olenka and the Autumn Lovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olenka_and_the_Autumn_Lovers "Olenka and the Autumn Lovers").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-10) Bulat began an MA in English at Western in 2006 but moved to Montreal to record "an audible memory" of her time there which resulted in her debut album.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-11)

Although Bulat no longer lives in London, Ontario, she has recognized the importance of London in establishing her career: "London has always been so supportive of me. I feel like the city really adopted me," she says. "It wasn't part of a larger commercial music industry but a small, close-knit scene, which was kind of nice."[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-postcity.com-12)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")\]

While a student at the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Ontario "University of Western Ontario"), Bulat had a radio program on Western's community and campus radio station [CHRW-FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHRW-FM "CHRW-FM") titled "Happy Tuesday" with co-host Holly Coish.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-13) Bulat first appeared on stage after she was coaxed by friends into opening for [Julie Doiron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Doiron "Julie Doiron")[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Hour-14)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-postcity.com-12) and soon gained a local following.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-LFPHeart-3)

### _Oh, My Darling_ (2007)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Oh, My Darling (2007)")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4a/Bulat%2C_Basia_%282008%29_1.jpg/250px-Bulat%2C_Basia_%282008%29_1.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bulat,_Basia_(2008)_1.jpg) Bulat performing with her [autoharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoharp "Autoharp") in 2008

Bulat released an independent [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") in 2005 and was subsequently signed to [Rough Trade Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Trade_Records "Rough Trade Records"), which released her full-length debut album, _[Oh, My Darling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh,_My_Darling "Oh, My Darling")_ in April 2007, produced by [Howard Bilerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Bilerman "Howard Bilerman"). The album was later released in Canada by [Hayden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_(musician) "Hayden (musician)")'s Hardwood Records.

Her singles "Snakes and Ladders", "Little One" and "I Was a Daughter" have been playlisted on [CBC Radio 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Radio_3 "CBC Radio 3") while various tracks have received airplay on college radio in Canada and the United States. Her album _Oh, My Darling_ made the shortlist for the [2008 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Polaris_Music_Prize "2008 Polaris Music Prize").[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-CBCPolaris-15)

Bulat has toured across Canada, the US and Europe; in June 2008, she ventured to Australia. She played the 2008 [Dawson City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawson_City "Dawson City") Music Festival in the [Yukon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukon "Yukon"). Bulat cited the week she spent in the quiet and remote location as an inspiration for her songwriting.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-spinnerYukon-16)

### _Heart of My Own_ (2010)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Heart of My Own (2010)")\]

Her second album, _[Heart of My Own](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_My_Own "Heart of My Own")_, also produced by [Howard Bilerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Bilerman "Howard Bilerman"), was released by [Rough Trade Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Trade_Records "Rough Trade Records") in the US and in Canada via Secret City Records on January 26, 2010.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-17) It peaked at number 13 on the [Billboard charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts") on February 13, 2010.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Basia_Bulat_Chart_History-18)

Bulat appeared on _[Hockey Night in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Night_in_Canada "Hockey Night in Canada")_ on December 10, 2011, during which she sang the [Canadian national anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_Canada "O Canada") at an [Ottawa Senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa_Senators "Ottawa Senators") game at Scotiabank Place (now the [Canadian Tire Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Tire_Centre "Canadian Tire Centre")).[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-sens-19)

### _Tall Tall Shadow_ (2013)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Tall Tall Shadow (2013)")\]

On September 30, 2013, Bulat released her third album _[Tall Tall Shadow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tall_Tall_Shadow "Tall Tall Shadow")_ worldwide via Secret City Records. It was produced by [Tim Kingsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kingsbury "Tim Kingsbury") and Mark Lawson. The album was nominated for a 2014 [Juno Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Award "Juno Award") in the Adult Alternative Album of the Year category,[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-JunoAwards2014-20) and was a shortlisted nominee for the [2014 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Polaris_Music_Prize "2014 Polaris Music Prize").[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-shortlist-21) The album has been well received in Canada and the United States.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-22) It peaked at number 25 on the [Billboard charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts") on October 19, 2013.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Basia_Bulat_Chart_History-18)

The song "Tall Tall Shadow" was featured in the opening video montage on _[Hockey Night in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Night_in_Canada "Hockey Night in Canada")_ prior to the games between the Chicago Blackhawks and Toronto Maple Leafs, and the Montreal Canadiens and New York Islanders on December 14, 2013.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-23)

### _Good Advice_ (2016)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Good Advice (2016)")\]

Her fourth album, _Good Advice_, was produced by [My Morning Jacket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Morning_Jacket "My Morning Jacket") frontman [Jim James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_James "Jim James") and was released on February 12, 2016. It became a shortlisted nominee for the [2016 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Polaris_Music_Prize "2016 Polaris Music Prize").[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-24) The title of the album refers to female friends whom she relied on during a recent breakup.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-25) It peaked at number 22 on the [Billboard charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts") on March 5, 2016.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-Basia_Bulat_Chart_History-18)

### _Basia's Palace_ (2025)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Basia_Bulat&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Basia's Palace (2025)")\]

Bulat's fifth album, which she released on February 21, 2025, was longlisted for the [2025 Polaris Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polaris_Music_Prize "2025 Polaris Music Prize").[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basia_Bulat#cite_note-26)
