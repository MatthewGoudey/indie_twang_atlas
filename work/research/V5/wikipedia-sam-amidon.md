Musical artist

**Samuel Tear Amidon** (born June 3, 1981) is an American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist.

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sam_Amidon&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Amidon's parents are folk artists Peter Amidon and Mary Alice Amidon.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-1) They were members of [Bread and Puppet Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread_and_Puppet_Theater "Bread and Puppet Theater") in the 1970s, touring with the group in [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe "Europe") and living on the Bread and Puppet Farm in Glover, [Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont "Vermont"). They appeared on the 1977 Nonesuch recording _Rivers of Delight with the Word of Mouth Chorus_, a classic recording of shape-note/ [Sacred Harp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Harp "Sacred Harp") folk hymns by early [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") [composers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composers "Composers").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-2) His younger brother, Stefan Amidon, is a professional drummer who performs with The Sweetback Sisters[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-3) among other groups.

Amidon grew up in [Brattleboro, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brattleboro,_Vermont "Brattleboro, Vermont"), where he sang in the family band with his parents and played [fiddle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiddle "Fiddle") from the age of 3. By 13 he was playing professionally with pianist [Thomas Bartlett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doveman "Doveman"), a childhood friend, and with his brother Stefan, in the contradance band Popcorn Behavior. Inspired by groups such as Wild Asparagus and Nightingale, as well as the traditional Irish fiddlers [Martin Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Hayes_(musician) "Martin Hayes (musician)") and [Tommy Peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Peoples "Tommy Peoples"), Popcorn Behavior released their first [album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album "Album") in 1994 when Amidon and Bartlett were 14 and 13, respectively, and Stefan was 10. Keith Murphy joined the group for their third album and the band toured throughout the country, released five albums, and were interviewed on NPR's "All Things Considered", all while the band members were still in high school.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-5) Amidon attended [The Putney School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Putney_School "The Putney School") in [Putney, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putney,_Vermont "Putney, Vermont") for one year.

As a teenager, Amidon discovered music by artists such as [Tony Conrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Conrad "Tony Conrad"), [Albert Ayler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Ayler "Albert Ayler"), [Yo La Tengo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yo_La_Tengo "Yo La Tengo"), [Miles Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Davis "Miles Davis") and [Don Cherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Cherry_(singer) "Don Cherry (singer)"), as well as reissues of field recordings of the _Old Weird America_ such as the [Harry Smith Anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_of_American_Folk_Music "Anthology of American Folk Music"), the [Alan Lomax Southern Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Journey "Southern Journey") series, and the songs of [Dock Boggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dock_Boggs "Dock Boggs").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-6) In the early 2000s, he lived in New York City and played in the experimental indie rock bands [Doveman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doveman "Doveman") and [Stars Like Fleas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stars_Like_Fleas "Stars Like Fleas").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-7)

## Music career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sam_Amidon&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Music career")\]

In 2001, Amidon self-released _Solo Fiddle_, an album of traditional Irish fiddle instrumentals.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-8)

Amidon's first album of songs, _[But This Chicken Proved False Hearted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/But_This_Chicken_Proved_False_Hearted "But This Chicken Proved False Hearted")_ (2007), was made with longtime collaborator [Thomas Bartlett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doveman "Doveman") and was initially released on the [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles")-based electronic label [Plug Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plug_Research "Plug Research").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-9) It was reissued on CD and LP in 2015 by [Omnivore Recordings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnivore_Recordings "Omnivore Recordings").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-10)

His second album, _[All Is Well](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Is_Well_(Sam_Amidon_album) "All Is Well (Sam Amidon album)")_ (2008), was produced, recorded and mixed by [Valgeir Sigurðsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valgeir_Sigur%C3%B0sson "Valgeir Sigurðsson") at [Greenhouse Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse_Studios "Greenhouse Studios") in [Iceland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceland "Iceland") and featured orchestral arrangements by [Nico Muhly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Muhly "Nico Muhly"). His third album, _[I See the Sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_See_the_Sign "I See the Sign")_ (2010), was also produced by Sigurðsson and featured multi-instrumentalist [Shahzad Ismaily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahzad_Ismaily "Shahzad Ismaily") and orchestral arrangements by Muhly, with guest vocals by [Beth Orton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_Orton "Beth Orton"). Both albums were released on the Icelandic label/collective [Bedroom Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedroom_Community "Bedroom Community")[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-11) and met with critical acclaim[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-12) from sources such as [Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Media "Pitchfork Media"), [Stylus Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylus_Magazine "Stylus Magazine") and the [New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times "New York Times"), which chose _I See the Sign_ as a top-ten album of 2010.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-13)

Amidon's fourth album, _[Bright Sunny South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Sunny_South "Bright Sunny South")_, produced by Bartlett and Jerry Boys, was released 14 May 2013, his first on [Nonesuch Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonesuch_Records "Nonesuch Records"). His next album, _[Lily-O](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily-O "Lily-O")_, recorded with a band consisting of Ismaily, [Bill Frisell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Frisell "Bill Frisell"), and Chris Vatalaro, was released on the same label on 30 September 2014. "A hauntingly beautiful new album" according to the [New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times "New York Times"), [PopMatters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PopMatters "PopMatters") called it "a thing of beauty and wonder, bold and strong".

Amidon's next album, _[The Following Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Following_Mountain "The Following Mountain")_, was released in May 2017, again on Nonesuch Records. It was his first album of original compositions, contrary to his previous work, which mostly reworked existing music.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-14)

Amidon has appeared as a guest artist on albums by [Tune-Yards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tune-Yards "Tune-Yards"), [Glen Hansard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Hansard "Glen Hansard"), [Jacob Collier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Collier "Jacob Collier"), Mx Justin Vivian Bond, Olof Arnalds, [The Blind Boys of Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Boys_of_Alabama "The Blind Boys of Alabama"), [The National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_National_(band) "The National (band)")'s Grateful Dead Tribute Day Of The Dead, and others.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-15) He has also self-published a book, _Notes On The Twitterographer_.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-16)

## Live performances

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sam_Amidon&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Live performances")\]

Amidon tours extensively throughout the United States and Canada, Europe, Australia, and Japan, and has appeared at festivals such as [End of the Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_the_Road_Festival "End of the Road Festival"), [Green Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Man_Festival "Green Man Festival"), [Roskilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roskilde_Festival "Roskilde Festival"), [Lowlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowlands_(festival) "Lowlands (festival)"), Pickathon, Solid Sound, Big Ears, [Celebrate Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebrate_Brooklyn "Celebrate Brooklyn"), The Sydney Festival, the [Jaipur Literature Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Literature_Festival "Jaipur Literature Festival"), and others. He has become known for freewheeling concert performances,[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-17) often with Vatalaro and Ismaily.

In 2007 he premiered Muhly's _The Only Tune_ at [Carnegie Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Hall "Carnegie Hall") as part of John Adams's In Your Ear festival.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-18) He has performed the piece at London's [Roundhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundhouse_(venue) "Roundhouse (venue)") and [Barbican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbican "Barbican"), as well as at Cork's Safe Harbour festival in collaboration with the [Crash Ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_Ensemble "Crash Ensemble"). Writing about the recorded version of _The Only Tune_, which appeared on Muhly's 2008 album _Mothertongue_, Greil Marcus said, "It's incalculably spooky, the way the action comes out of nowhere, the way Amidon has prepared you to expect nothing."

Between 2002 and 2006, Amidon devised a multimedia program called "Home Alone Inside My Head," consisting of "self-inflicted field recordings," drawn [comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comics "Comics"), short videos, [storytelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storytelling "Storytelling") and [improvisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improvisation "Improvisation"). He excerpted the project online and performed it in full at experimental music and art spaces around [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") and [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn") and beyond, including NYC's [The Kitchen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kitchen_(art_institution) "The Kitchen (art institution)") and MAD Museum, Monkeytown in Brooklyn, the AVA gallery on NYC's [Lower East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_East_Side "Lower East Side"), [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati "Cincinnati")'s CAC, the Kuhturm Gallery in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig "Leipzig"), and MASS MoCA in North Adams, MA.[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-19)[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-20)

Amidon has performed as part of the [Bedroom Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedroom_Community "Bedroom Community") Whale Watching Tour[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-21) at The [Barbican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbican "Barbican") and [Reykjavík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk "Reykjavík")'s Harpa concert hall. He has also appeared in 2015 and 2016 at the [Bon Iver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Iver "Bon Iver")-curated Eaux Claires festival in [Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin "Wisconsin"),[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-22) performing his own music and appearing onstage as guest vocalist with [Bon Iver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Iver "Bon Iver"), Richard Reed Parry, and parading the grounds of the festival leading a pop-up "Guitarkestra." In September 2016, Amidon hosted a multi-artist tribute to Pete Seeger at [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland "Ireland")'s National Concert Hall in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin"),[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-23) featuring Irish and American artists such as [Tommy Sands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Sands_(Irish_folk_singer) "Tommy Sands (Irish folk singer)"), [Mairead Ni Mhaonaigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mairead_Ni_Mhaonaigh "Mairead Ni Mhaonaigh"), [The Voice Squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_Squad "The Voice Squad"), Bell X1 and others. Amidon has done collaborative concerts and mini-tours with artists such as [Bill Frisell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Frisell "Bill Frisell"), [Nels Cline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nels_Cline "Nels Cline"), [Jason Moran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Moran_(musician) "Jason Moran (musician)"), and [Marc Ribot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Ribot "Marc Ribot").[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-24) In February 2017 he toured as soloist with the [Australian Chamber Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Chamber_Orchestra "Australian Chamber Orchestra")[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-25) under the direction of [Pekka Kuusisto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekka_Kuusisto "Pekka Kuusisto"), including performances at the [Sydney Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Opera_House "Sydney Opera House"), [Sydney Recital Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Recital_Hall "Sydney Recital Hall"), and concerts throughout the country. In 2020 he played at the first edition of the [Monheim Triennale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monheim_Triennale "Monheim Triennale").[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-26)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sam_Amidon&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

Amidon is married to musician [Beth Orton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beth_Orton "Beth Orton"). They have a son, Arthur, born in 2011.[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-Sugaring-27) Based in London,[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-Sugaring-27) the musicians lived in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") for several years before returning to the UK.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Amidon#cite_note-RedBull-28)
