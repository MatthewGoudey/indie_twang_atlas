Musical artist

**Angel Olsen** (born **Angelina Marie Carroll**; January 22, 1987) is an American singer-songwriter from [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis "St. Louis"), [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri "Missouri")[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-9) who lives in [Asheville, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asheville,_North_Carolina "Asheville, North Carolina").[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-spin.com-10)

To date, Olsen has released six studio albums: _Half Way Home_ (2012), _[Burn Your Fire for No Witness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_Your_Fire_for_No_Witness "Burn Your Fire for No Witness")_ (2014), _[My Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Woman_(album) "My Woman (album)")_ (2016), _[All Mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Mirrors "All Mirrors")_ (2019), _[Whole New Mess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_New_Mess "Whole New Mess")_ (2020), and _[Big Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Time_(Angel_Olsen_album) "Big Time (Angel Olsen album)")_ (2022).

## Early life and education

Angel Olsen was born on January 22, 1987, in [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis "St. Louis"), [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri "Missouri"). At age three, Olsen was adopted by a [foster family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_care "Foster care") that had cared for her since shortly after her birth. The difference in years between her and her parents left an impression. "Because there are so many decades of difference between us, I became more interested in what their childhood was like," she says of her parents, both of whom died in 2021.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Petrusich-11) "I fantasized about what it was like to be young in the '30s and '50s, more so than other kids my age."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-spin.com-10) Olsen explained that "my mother just has this capacity for children."[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-spin.com-10)

Despite early adolescent aspirations to be a "pop star", her interests later shifted in high school. Olsen became more introverted, regularly attending [punk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk_rock "Punk rock") and [noise music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noise_music "Noise music") shows at the [Lemp Neighborhood Arts Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemp_Neighborhood_Arts_Center "Lemp Neighborhood Arts Center") and the Creepy Crawl as well as [Christian rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_rock "Christian rock") shows throughout the city.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Appelstein-12) She began learning the piano and guitar and writing her own music.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-spin.com-10)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-guardian14-13) At the age of 16, she joined a local band called Good Fight, self-described as "a meeting of early [No Doubt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Doubt "No Doubt") and punk rock."[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-PearShaped-14) Two years after graduating from [Tower Grove Christian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_Grove_Christian_Academy "Tower Grove Christian Academy"), Olsen moved to [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago").[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Appelstein-12)

She befriended [country rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rock "Country rock") singer-songwriter [Bonnie "Prince" Billy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Oldham "Will Oldham"), who invited her to go on tour as his backing vocalist.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-:0-15)

## Career

### 2011–2014: _Strange Cacti_ and _Half Way Home_

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Angel_Olsen_%40_The_Sinclair_%28Cambridge%2C_MA%29_%2829733888992%29.jpg/500px-Angel_Olsen_%40_The_Sinclair_%28Cambridge%2C_MA%29_%2829733888992%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Angel_Olsen_@_The_Sinclair_(Cambridge,_MA)_(29733888992).jpg) Olsen performing live at the Sinclair in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts "Cambridge, Massachusetts"), 2016

Olsen released her debut [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play"), _[Strange Cacti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strange_Cacti "Strange Cacti")_ in 2011,[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Bathetic_Records-16) and her debut [studio album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album#Studio "Album"), _Half Way Home_, in 2012,[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-17) on Bathetic Records. In addition to her work with Bonnie "Prince" Billy and the [Cairo Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cairo_Gang_(band) "The Cairo Gang (band)"), Olsen has collaborated with a number of other notable figures of American indie rock, including [Tim Kinsella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kinsella "Tim Kinsella") of [Cap'n Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap'n_Jazz "Cap'n Jazz"), [LeRoy Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leroy_Bach "Leroy Bach") of [Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco") and [Cass McCombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_McCombs "Cass McCombs").[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Joyful_Noise_Recordings-18) Her collaboration with Kinsella and Bach, as well as with Chicago poet Marvin Tate, resulted in the album _[Tim Kinsella Sings the Songs of Marvin Tate by Leroy Bach Featuring Angel Olsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kinsella_Sings_the_Songs_of_Marvin_Tate_by_Leroy_Bach_Featuring_Angel_Olsen "Tim Kinsella Sings the Songs of Marvin Tate by Leroy Bach Featuring Angel Olsen")_ which the group released on [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis "Indianapolis") label [Joyful Noise Recordings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyful_Noise_Recordings "Joyful Noise Recordings") on December 3, 2013.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Joyful_Noise_Recordings-18)

### 2014–2017: _Burn Your Fire for No Witness_ and _My Woman_

Olsen signed a [recording contract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_contract "Recording contract") with [Jagjaguwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagjaguwar "Jagjaguwar"),[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-19) ahead of her first full-band record, _[Burn Your Fire for No Witness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_Your_Fire_for_No_Witness "Burn Your Fire for No Witness")_,[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-20) which was released on February 17, 2014.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-21)[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-22) The closing track of the album, "Windows", was featured in the final episode in the first season of the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") original series _[13 Reasons Why](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_Reasons_Why "13 Reasons Why")_ in 2017.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4a/P4K_-_Saturday_%2835120920564%29_%28cropped%29.jpg/500px-P4K_-_Saturday_%2835120920564%29_%28cropped%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:P4K_-_Saturday_(35120920564)_(cropped).jpg) Olsen performing live at [Pitchfork Music Festival 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_Music_Festival_2017 "Pitchfork Music Festival 2017") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois "Illinois"), 2017

Olsen's third studio album, _[My Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Woman_(album) "My Woman (album)")_, was released on September 2, 2016.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-23)[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-24) In a review for _[Consequence of Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consequence_(publication) "Consequence (publication)")_, critic Ciara Dolan described the album as a "startling record of unimpeachable strength and honesty",[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-25) while _[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_'s Jenn Pelly described it as "her best record yet".[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-26)

On November 10, 2017, Olsen released the compilation album _[Phases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phases_(Angel_Olsen_album) "Phases (Angel Olsen album)")_, including b-sides and bonus tracks. The album was preceded by the lead single "Fly on Your Wall".

### 2019–2021: _All Mirrors_, _Whole New Mess_, _Songs of the Lark and Other Far Memories_ and _Aisles_

Olsen's fourth studio album, _[All Mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Mirrors "All Mirrors")_, was released on October 4, 2019, to critical acclaim. Laura Snapes of _Pitchfork_ described the album as "breathtaking", and a "strong wind" that blows in and "leaves you undone",[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-27) while Alexis Petridis of _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ described it as "challenging and intriguing", and Luke Saunders of _Happy Mag_ described it as a change of "theatric transcendency",[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-28) when compared to her previous releases. The same year, British producer [Mark Ronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ronson "Mark Ronson") featured Olsen on his fifth studio album _[Late Night Feelings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Night_Feelings "Late Night Feelings")_, on the track "True Blue".[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-:0-15)

In 2020, Olsen released several remixes of popular releases. On April 9, Olsen released a remix of "All Mirrors" produced by [Chromatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatics_(band) "Chromatics (band)")' [Johnny Jewel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Jewel "Johnny Jewel").[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-29) On June 3, Olsen released a remix of "New Love Cassette" produced by Mark Ronson.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-30) On August 28, Olsen released her fifth studio album _[Whole New Mess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_New_Mess "Whole New Mess")_ through Jagjaguwar.[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-31) The album features tracks from _All Mirrors_ arranged in a more intimate style and would earn her the Best Folk/Bluegrass Record at the 2021 [Libera Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libera_Awards "Libera Awards") by the [American Association of Independent Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_of_Independent_Music "American Association of Independent Music").[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-32) The same year, Olsen worked on a [cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_version "Cover version") of " [Mr. Lonely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Lonely_(Bobby_Vinton_song) "Mr. Lonely (Bobby Vinton song)")", originally by [Bobby Vinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Vinton "Bobby Vinton"), for the film _[Kajillionaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajillionaire "Kajillionaire")_ directed by [Miranda July](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_July "Miranda July"). She also collaborated with film composer [Emile Mosseri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emile_Mosseri "Emile Mosseri") on the cover which was released on September 16, and was included in the soundtrack.[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-33)

On March 30, 2021, Olsen announced a special edition [box set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_set "Box set") titled _Songs of the Lark and Other Far Memories_, which contains her previous two studio albums _[All Mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Mirrors "All Mirrors")_ and _[Whole New Mess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_New_Mess "Whole New Mess")_ alongside [demos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demo_(music) "Demo (music)"), re-workings, remixes and covers to close this chapter of her career, released via Jagjaguwar. It was announced with the [lead single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_single "Lead single") "It's Every Season \[Whole New Mess\]" and was released on May 7.[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-34) On May 20, Olsen released a single with [Sharon Van Etten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Van_Etten "Sharon Van Etten"), " [Like I Used To](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Like_I_Used_To_(Sharon_Van_Etten_and_Angel_Olsen_song) "Like I Used To (Sharon Van Etten and Angel Olsen song)")", which was produced by [John Congleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Congleton "John Congleton"). Olsen and Van Etten appear in the [music video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_video "Music video") with their hair styled in similar [shag haircuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shag_(haircut) "Shag (haircut)").[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-35) On August 20, Olsen released her fourth extended play, _Aisles_, consisting of five cover versions of popular songs from the 1980s.[\[36\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-36) _Pitchfork_ writer Evan Rytlewski described it as "an unusual departure for a songwriter who's always staked everything on her conviction".[\[37\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-37)

### 2022–present: _Big Time_ and _Forever Means_

Olsen's sixth studio album, _[Big Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Time_(Angel_Olsen_album) "Big Time (Angel Olsen album)")_, was released on June 3, 2022.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-Petrusich-11) The album was preceded by the lead single "All the Good Times", and followed by a second single, the title track "Big Time". On April 14, 2023, Olsen released her fifth EP, _[Forever Means](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever_Means "Forever Means")_, containing four previously unreleased tracks originally recorded for _Big Time_.

## Style

_[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")_ has likened her album _All Mirrors_ to acts such as [the Cure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cure "The Cure"), [Cocteau Twins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocteau_Twins "Cocteau Twins") and [Siouxsie and the Banshees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siouxsie_and_the_Banshees "Siouxsie and the Banshees"), saying that she has created a dark [dream-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_pop "Dream pop") dealing with anxiety.[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-38) Olsen plays a vintage [Gibson S-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibson_S-1 "Gibson S-1") guitar from 1979.[\[39\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-39)

## Personal life

On April 16, 2021, Olsen stated she was [gay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesbian "Lesbian").[\[40\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-40) In 2022, Olsen revealed that her relationship with partner Beau Thibodeaux had ended, and that she had dated long-time friend and musician [Meg Duffy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Duffy "Meg Duffy") for a few months in 2020.[\[41\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-41)[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-theguardian.com-42) In a 2022 interview, Olsen stated she now identifies as [pansexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pansexuality "Pansexuality"),[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-theguardian.com-42) and in August 2023 celebrated one year with boyfriend and singer-songwriter [Maxim Ludwig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Ludwig "Maxim Ludwig"). Ludwig co-directed the lyric video for Olsen's song "Nothing's Free",[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-43) additionally serving as a support act on her 2023 North American tour.[\[44\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-44) In 2024, Olsen and Ludwig married.[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Olsen#cite_note-45)
