Musical artist

**Meg Baird** is an American musician based in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco"), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), United States, who, in addition to her solo career, is known as a founding member, lead vocalist, and drummer for [Heron Oblivion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron_Oblivion "Heron Oblivion"), along with members from [Assemble Head in Sunburst Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assemble_Head_in_Sunburst_Sound "Assemble Head in Sunburst Sound") and [Comets on Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comets_on_Fire "Comets on Fire"). She was also a guitarist and the lead female vocalist in Philadelphia [psychedelic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_music "Psychedelic music") folk rock band [Espers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espers_(band) "Espers (band)"), and played drums in Philadelphia punk band [Watery Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watery_Love?action=edit&redlink=1 "Watery Love (page does not exist)"). Baird frequently collaborates with the [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles")-based harpist [Mary Lattimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Lattimore "Mary Lattimore").

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Meg_Baird&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Originally from [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey "New Jersey"), United States, Baird's family history is based in the folk tradition: Baird is the great-great niece of Isaac Garfield "I.G." Greer, a historian and Appalachian folk singer born in 1881.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-1) His inclusion on one of the earliest albums issued by the [Archive of Folk Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive_of_Folk_Culture "Archive of Folk Culture") in the [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress "Library of Congress") helped expose Baird to folk music at a young age, while she was taking piano lessons, teaching herself guitar, and listening to [Smithsonian Folkways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folkways_Records "Folkways Records") albums.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-2)

## Music

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Meg_Baird&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Music")\]

While a member of Espers In 2007, Baird released her first solo album, _[Dear Companion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_Companion "Dear Companion")_, on the [Drag City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_City_(record_label) "Drag City (record label)") record label. Baird's second solo album, _Seasons on Earth_, was released in 2011 and received favorable reviews from _[The Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire_(magazine) "The Wire (magazine)")_, [Allmusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allmusic "Allmusic"), _[Spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_(magazine) "Spin (magazine)")_ and [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-5)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-6) This was followed by _[Don't Weigh Down the Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don't_Weigh_Down_the_Light "Don't Weigh Down the Light")_ in 2015, described as "post-folk" by _[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")_.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-7)

## Bands and collaborations

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Meg_Baird&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Bands and collaborations")\]

Baird was a founding member of the Philadelphia psychedelic folk group Espers, whose first album was released by [Time-Lag Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-Lag_Records "Time-Lag Records") in 2003.

In 2014, Baird joined [Heron Oblivion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron_Oblivion "Heron Oblivion") as lead vocalist and drummer. The [supergroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergroup_(music) "Supergroup (music)") includes Charles Saufley, Noel Van Harmonson ( [Comets on Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comets_on_Fire "Comets on Fire")), and Ethan Miller ( [Howlin Rain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howlin_Rain "Howlin Rain"), [Comets on Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comets_on_Fire "Comets on Fire")).[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-10)

She also plays with sister Laura in [The Baird Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Baird_Sisters?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Baird Sisters (page does not exist)") and have released three albums between 2003 and 2011. In 2013, the Baird Sisters joined Glenn Jones on _My Garden State_.

Between 2009 and 2012, Baird played drums for Philadelphia punk band Watery Love.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-11)

In 2018, Baird and [Mary Lattimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Lattimore "Mary Lattimore") released their album _Ghost Forests_ to critical acclaim,[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-12)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-13) reaching the number three position on [Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts")'s [new age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New-age_music "New-age music") chart.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-14)

## Touring

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Meg_Baird&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Touring")\]

As a solo artist, she has toured with many noted fingerstyle guitarists, including [Bert Jansch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bert_Jansch "Bert Jansch"),[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-15) [James Blackshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blackshaw "James Blackshaw"), [Micah Blue Smaldone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micah_Blue_Smaldone "Micah Blue Smaldone"), [Michael Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chapman_(singer) "Michael Chapman (singer)"), [Glenn Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Jones_(guitarist) "Glenn Jones (guitarist)"), [Michael Hurley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Hurley_(musician) "Michael Hurley (musician)"), and [Jack Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Rose_(guitarist) "Jack Rose (guitarist)"). In February 2016 Baird served as the support act to [Ryley Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryley_Walker "Ryley Walker") and [Danny Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Thompson "Danny Thompson") on an extensive tour of the United Kingdom.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-16) In 2019, Baird and Mary Lattimore toured extensively in support of their album _Ghost Forests_.

## Style and influences

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Meg_Baird&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Style and influences")\]

Evoking Appalachian and Anglo-American folk ballads, Baird's vocal style has been compared to members of the UK folk-rock scene, including Celia Humpris of [Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trees_(band) "Trees (band)"), [Fairport Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairport_Convention "Fairport Convention")'s [Sandy Denny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Denny "Sandy Denny"), and [Pentangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentangle_(band) "Pentangle (band)")'s [Jacqui McShee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqui_McShee "Jacqui McShee").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-17)[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-18)[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Baird#cite_note-19)
