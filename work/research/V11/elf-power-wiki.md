**Elf Power** is an American [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band that originated in [Athens, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia"), United States. The line-up consists of guitarist/vocalist [Andrew Rieger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Rieger "Andrew Rieger"), keyboardist [Laura Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Carter_(musician) "Laura Carter (musician)"), guitarist Dave Wrathgabar, bassist Bryan Poole, and drummer Peter Alvanos. They are part of the [Elephant Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Six "Elephant Six") Collective.

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Elf_Power&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The band began as a four-track recording project of Rieger, Carter, Raleigh Hatfield and Dave Wrathgabar, later of [Fablefactory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fablefactory "Fablefactory"). These recording sessions eventually resulted in the first incarnation of their debut album _[Vainly Clutching at Phantom Limbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vainly_Clutching_at_Phantom_Limbs "Vainly Clutching at Phantom Limbs")_. The band's line-up expanded when Rieger and Carter recruited [Elephant Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Six "Elephant Six") musician Bryan Poole, also known as [The Late B.P. Helium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_B.P._Helium "The Late B.P. Helium"). While the band occasionally played live shows and continued to record, Poole chiefly stayed in [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia") to continue work with [Of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Of_Montreal "Of Montreal") while Rieger and Carter moved to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City").

### Mid–late 1990s

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Elf_Power&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Mid–late 1990s")\]

After residing in [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") for some time, the band released _[The Winter Hawk EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Winter_Hawk_EP "The Winter Hawk EP")_ in 1996.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-ww-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-tp-2) Eventually, Rieger and Carter were convinced to move back to [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia"), where the band's second album _[When the Red King Comes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_the_Red_King_Comes "When the Red King Comes")_ was ultimately recorded and released[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-ww-1) as a joint venture between the [Arena Rock Recording Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Rock_Recording_Co. "Arena Rock Recording Co.") and the [Elephant Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Six "Elephant Six") label. The album featured contributions by members of [The Olivia Tremor Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Olivia_Tremor_Control "The Olivia Tremor Control"), [Kevin Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Barnes "Kevin Barnes") of [Of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Of_Montreal "Of Montreal"), and [Jeff Mangum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Mangum "Jeff Mangum") of [Neutral Milk Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_Milk_Hotel "Neutral Milk Hotel").

Shortly following the release of _When the Red King Comes_, Elf Power collaborated with [Dave Fridmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Fridmann "Dave Fridmann"), best known for his work with [The Flaming Lips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flaming_Lips "The Flaming Lips"), to record the album _[A Dream in Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Dream_in_Sound "A Dream in Sound")_.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-tp-2) The album was again recorded with the help of Mangum and Barnes, as well as [Scott Spillane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Spillane "Scott Spillane") of [Neutral Milk Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_Milk_Hotel "Neutral Milk Hotel") and [The Gerbils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gerbils "The Gerbils").

During the 1999 supporting tour for _[A Dream in Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Dream_in_Sound "A Dream in Sound")_, Adrian Finch joined the band to play [violin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violin "Violin"). Following the addition, the band recorded an [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") focused chiefly on cover material entitled _Come On_. Around this time, _Vainly Clutching at Phantom Limbs_ was reissued with material from early sessions.

### Early–mid-2000s

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Elf_Power&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Early–mid-2000s")\]

The band regrouped in late 2000 to record _[The Winter Is Coming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Winter_Is_Coming "The Winter Is Coming")_, again with help from members of other Elephant Six bands. Notably, the band spent this time as a supporting act for fellow [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia "Athens, Georgia") band [R.E.M.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.M. "R.E.M."), as well as [Wilco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilco "Wilco"). [Bryan Poole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Poole "Bryan Poole") would exit the band for good following the European tour for the album to focus on solo work.

2002 saw the release of two albums from the band: _[Creatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creatures_(Elf_Power_album) "Creatures (Elf Power album)")_, an effort released by [spinART](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpinART "SpinART"), and _[Nothing's Going to Happen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing's_Going_to_Happen "Nothing's Going to Happen")_, an album released on [Orange Twin Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Twin_Records "Orange Twin Records") that contained various cover songs and the [Come On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_On_(EP) "Come On (EP)") EP. During this time, Finch left the lineup and Ballard Lesemann stepped in to play [bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_(instrument) "Bass (instrument)").

In 2004, Elf Power released their seventh studio album _[Walking with the Beggar Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walking_with_the_Beggar_Boys "Walking with the Beggar Boys")_. The album was released to notable media and critical praise and featured contributions from [Vic Chesnutt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Chesnutt "Vic Chesnutt").

The band released _[Back to the Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_the_Web "Back to the Web")_ on [Rykodisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rykodisc "Rykodisc") in April 2006, an album which featured bassist Derek Almstead. A tour of the United States and Europe followed. They were also chosen to play in the United Kingdom 2006 [All Tomorrow's Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Tomorrow's_Parties_(music_festival) "All Tomorrow's Parties (music festival)") music festival curated by [The Shins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shins "The Shins").

### 2008–present

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Elf_Power&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2008–present")\]

The band's ninth album _[In a Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_a_Cave "In a Cave")_ was released in March 2008 on [Rykodisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rykodisc "Rykodisc"). Their [MySpace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MySpace "MySpace") page first described the project as "a lushly orchestrated mix of eclectic songwriting that is sure to conjure up memories of 1997's _When the Red King Comes_" and stressed its "more mature approach to arrangements, lyrics, and instrumentation."

Elf Power collaborated with [Vic Chesnutt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Chesnutt "Vic Chesnutt") on _[Dark Developments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Developments_(album)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dark Developments (album) (page does not exist)")_, released on [Orange Twin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Twin "Orange Twin") in 2008.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-p-3) It was supported by U.S. and European tours.

Aaron Wegelin, who drummed on the first five Elf Power albums, died on February 2, 2021.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-4)

In 2022, Elf Power released their twelfth album _Artificial Countrysides_ on [Yep Roc Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yep_Roc_Records "Yep Roc Records").[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_Power#cite_note-pm-5)
