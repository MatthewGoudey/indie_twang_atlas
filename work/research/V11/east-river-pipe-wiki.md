Musical artist

**F.M. Cornog** is an American songwriter, singer, self-taught musician, and home-recordist who records under the name **East River Pipe**. _[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")_ described Cornog as "the Brian Wilson of home recording."[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_Pipe#cite_note-1)

Cornog was born in [Norfolk, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk,_Virginia "Norfolk, Virginia"), and raised in [Summit, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit,_New_Jersey "Summit, New Jersey").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_Pipe#cite_note-2) After high school, Cornog worked a series of menial jobs before succumbing to alcoholism,
drug abuse, mental illness, and eventual homelessness, ending up in the [Hoboken train station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoboken_Terminal "Hoboken Terminal").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_Pipe#cite_note-3)

During this time he met [Astoria, Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astoria,_Queens "Astoria, Queens")-resident Barbara Powers, and with Powers' support and label (Hell Gate), Cornog released some home-recorded cassettes and 7" singles under the name East River Pipe, which he chose after observing a sewage pipe spewing out raw waste into the [East River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River "East River").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_Pipe#cite_note-4) These initial 7" singles attracted the attention of [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK "UK")-based [Sarah Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Records "Sarah Records") who released his records from 1993 to 1996, making Cornog one of the few American artists ever signed to the label.

In the U.S., Cornog released his first LP, _Shining Hours In A Can_, on the Chicago-based micro-indie Ajax Records in 1994. A year later, he found a more permanent home on Merge Records, the Chapel Hill-based indie run by [Mac McCaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_McCaughan "Mac McCaughan") and [Laura Ballance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Ballance "Laura Ballance"). Merge released _Poor Fricky_ (1995), _Mel_ (1996), _The Gasoline Age_ (1999), _Shining Hours In A Can_ (2002;reissue), _Garbageheads On Endless Stun_ (2003), _What Are You On?_ (2006), and _We Live In Rented Rooms_ (2011).

Artists who have covered East River Pipe songs include [David Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Byrne "David Byrne"), [Lambchop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambchop_(band) "Lambchop (band)"), [The Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats "The Mountain Goats"), [Waxahatchee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waxahatchee "Waxahatchee"), [Okkervil River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okkervil_River "Okkervil River"), [The Pains of Being Pure at Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pains_of_Being_Pure_at_Heart "The Pains of Being Pure at Heart"), [For Against](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_Against "For Against"), [Mary Lou Lord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Lou_Lord "Mary Lou Lord"), and others. _[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")_ called Cornog "the most gifted of the new loners."[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_Pipe#cite_note-5)
