**The Moldy Peaches** are an American [indie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_music "Independent music") founded by [Adam Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Green_(musician) "Adam Green (musician)") and [Kimya Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimya_Dawson "Kimya Dawson"). Leading proponents of the [anti-folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-folk "Anti-folk") scene, the band had been on [hiatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recess_(break) "Recess (break)") since 2004, but in 2023 announced on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter") that they would be reuniting. The appearance of their song " [Anyone Else but You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_Else_but_You "Anyone Else but You")" in the film _[Juno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_(film) "Juno (film)")_ significantly raised their profile; Dawson and Green made a handful of reunion appearances together in December 2007.

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Moldy_Peaches&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2b/Aaron_Wilkinson.jpg/250px-Aaron_Wilkinson.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Aaron_Wilkinson.jpg) Aaron Wilkinson

Green and Dawson met at Exile on Main Street Records in [Mount Kisco, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kisco,_New_York "Mount Kisco, New York"), and began working together.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-1) Green put out a 7-inch EP called _X-Ray Vision_ under the name the Moldy Peaches, featuring recordings he made from 1994 to 1996 with Dawson and various other friends, notably Jules Sheridan, a songwriter based in Scotland. Green and Dawson recorded a CDR album in 1998 under the name Moldy Peaches 2000 called _FER THE KIDS_ before Dawson moved to [Port Townsend, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Townsend,_Washington "Port Townsend, Washington").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-crud-2)

In early 1999, Green joined her there, and more home and live recordings transpired. The band returned to New York City as a four piece later in the year, including Jest Commons, guitar, and Justin Campbell, drums. They became active on the New York City [anti-folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-folk "Anti-folk") scene, playing at the [SideWalk Cafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SideWalk_Cafe "SideWalk Cafe") before the band broke up.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-crud-2) Dawson and Green both recorded solo albums. The band reformed in August 2000 with [Chris Barron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Barron "Chris Barron") of the [Spin Doctors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_Doctors "Spin Doctors") on lead guitar, Brian Piltin on bass guitar, and Strictly Beats (Brent Cole) on drums.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-crud-2) (The "2000" was dropped from their name around this time). A new 11 song album was recorded, which led to a deal with [Rough Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Trade_Records "Rough Trade Records") in the UK.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-crud-2) They gained recognition after their initial 7" 'Who's Got the Crack" was named 'Single of the Week' in _[NME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NME "NME")_. Rough Trade released the album _[The Moldy Peaches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches_(album) "The Moldy Peaches (album)")_ in 2001. Released in the U.S. on September 11, 2001, the same day as the [September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks"), it contained the song "NYC Is Like a Graveyard." The band expanded to a six-piece, with guitarists [Jack Dishel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Dishel "Jack Dishel") and Aaron Wilkinson, bass player Steven Mertens, and drummer Strictly Beats, augmenting the original duo of Dawson and Green. They toured internationally with [The Strokes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Strokes "The Strokes") with whom they shared record label and management.

Aaron Wilkinson left the band and was replaced by [Toby Goodshank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Goodshank "Toby Goodshank"). Wilkinson died from an [overdose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overdose "Overdose") in July 2003.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-3) The Strokes dedicated their album _[Room on Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Room_on_Fire "Room on Fire")_ to his memory.

In 2003, a second album _Moldy Peaches 2000_ was released, a double-CD compilation of various scraps and live recordings.

After an extensive U.S. headlining tour in the winter of 2003, the band went into hiatus in early 2004. However, the band reunited in late 2004 for a one-off show to benefit Accidental CDs, Records and Tapes, a hole-in-the-wall record store on Ave A in NYC. That store was an early supporter of the band and helped hook them up with the gig that ultimately got them their record deal. Both central members embarked on solo careers.

On December 2, 2007, Dawson and Green played an impromptu set together at The Smell in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") to end a show where Dawson was headlining. They changed the lyrics of the song "Who's Got The Crack" to "Who's Got The Blues".[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-4) On December 3, 2007, the Moldy Peaches played at the _[Juno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_(film) "Juno (film)")_ film premiere.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-5)

The band was booked to appear on _[Late Night with Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Night_with_Conan_O'Brien "Late Night with Conan O'Brien")_ on January 14, 2008, but they canceled because of the [writer's strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%932008_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike "2007–2008 Writers Guild of America strike").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-6) Dawson has said that she is not keen to reform the band at present.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-7) However, Dawson and Green did appear together on the NPR radio show _[Bryant Park Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryant_Park_Project "Bryant Park Project")_ on January 16, 2008.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-8) and appeared on television show _[The View](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_View_(talk_show) "The View (talk show)")_ on January 21, 2008.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-9)

Subsequent to the success of the _Juno_ soundtrack, which hit No.1 on the _Billboard_ 200 on its third week of physical release, the song "Anyone Else but You" was released as a UK single on February 25, 2008.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-10)

On November 13, 2011, the Moldy Peaches played a short set at the [Knitting Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting_Factory "Knitting Factory") in New York.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-11)

The Moldy Peaches played three European sets in 2023, in London, Barcelona and Madrid.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-12)

On August 10 and 11, 2023, The Moldy Peaches played two sets at Brooklyn Steel in Brooklyn, NY.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-13)

On October 13, 2024, the Moldy Peaches released an alternate version of 'Anyone Else but You'.

In 2026, [Adventure Time: Side Quests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Time:_Side_Quests "Adventure Time: Side Quests") used covers of the [Adventure Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Time "Adventure Time") theme song and " [Island Song (Come Along With Me)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_Song "Island Song")" by The Moldy Peaches for the opening and closing theme songs.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-14)

## Solo projects

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Moldy_Peaches&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Solo projects")\]

Green released his seventh solo album and accompanying film, _Aladdin_, in 2016 via Fat Possum. His initial band included a 3-piece string section.

Dawson has continued to tour playing small clubs and house parties. Her album _Remember That I Love You_ on [K Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Records "K Records") has been well received. Dawson collaborated with [Aesop Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aesop_Rock "Aesop Rock") on the album _[Hokey Fright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokey_Fright "Hokey Fright")_ under the name [The Uncluded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Uncluded "The Uncluded").

Dishel's former outfit Stipplicon having broken up, he formed a new band, Only Son, who have toured with [Regina Spektor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_Spektor "Regina Spektor"). Dishel also plays in Spektor's band.

Mertens has his own group SpaceCamp and plays in Green's backing band.

Cole is in the boyband Candy Boys and has performed with numerous acts including [Dufus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dufus_(band) "Dufus (band)"), [Jeffrey Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Lewis "Jeffrey Lewis"), Only Son, [Sandra Bernhard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bernhard "Sandra Bernhard"), and Toby Goodshank.

Goodshank plays solo shows and is also in a duo, Double Deuce, with his sister Angela.

## Notable appearances

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Moldy_Peaches&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Notable appearances")\]

- The song " [Anyone Else but You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_Else_but_You "Anyone Else but You")" was used in the [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards")-nominated documentary film _[Murderball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murderball_(film) "Murderball (film)")_.
- The song "Anyone Else but You" was used in a mobile phone TV-ad in France during [World Cup 2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup "2006 FIFA World Cup"), featuring French star [Zinedine Zidane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinedine_Zidane "Zinedine Zidane").
- The song "Anyone Else but You" is central to the 2007 Academy Award-winning film _[Juno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_(film) "Juno (film)")_ and a version is also performed by the two main characters ( [Elliot Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Page "Elliot Page")[\[a\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-Elliot-15) and [Michael Cera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Cera "Michael Cera")).
- A re-written version of "Anyone Else but You" was featured in a commercial for Atlantis Resorts in the Bahamas.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-16)
- The song "Jorge Regula" was used in a [Pepsodent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepsodent "Pepsodent") commercial in Hispanic America, without the group's permission.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moldy_Peaches#cite_note-17)
- A version of the song "Jorge Regula" also appears in the 2006 indie film _[The Guatemalan Handshake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guatemalan_Handshake "The Guatemalan Handshake")_.
