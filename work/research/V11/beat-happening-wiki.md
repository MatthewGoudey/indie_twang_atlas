**Beat Happening** was an American [indie pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_pop "Indie pop") band formed in [Olympia, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympia,_Washington "Olympia, Washington") in 1982. [Calvin Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Johnson_(musician) "Calvin Johnson (musician)"), [Heather Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Lewis_(musician) "Heather Lewis (musician)"), and [Bret Lunsford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bret_Lunsford "Bret Lunsford") were the band's continual members. Beat Happening were early leaders in the American indie pop and [slacker rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slacker_rock "Slacker rock") movements as well as pioneers of [twee pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twee_pop "Twee pop"), noted for their playful disregard for the technical aspects of musicianship that contributed to [lo-fi music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi_music "Lo-fi music"), and songwriting with carefree and coy themes.

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Beat_Happening&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

### Formation

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Beat_Happening&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Formation")\]

Beat Happening met while attending the [Evergreen State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evergreen_State_College "Evergreen State College") and began recording in 1983. The band took its name from a student art film, _Beatnik Happening_, made by Lunsford's girlfriend.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock-1) The year prior, Johnson had founded the label [K Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Records "K Records"). The band's basic line-up was drums, guitar, and vocals, though when they formed, their only instruments were a pair of maracas and a Sears [Silvertone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvertone_(brand) "Silvertone (brand)") guitar purchased at a thrift shop. Lewis once joked in an interview that she believed the history of the band could be readily told through a list of people whose drums they had borrowed. She and Johnson had been members of a previous band and approached Lunsford, who had no musical experience at the time.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock-1)

Early recordings made use of an [Echoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echoplex "Echoplex") machine to add heavy reverb to Lunsford's guitar, in an attempt to mask his unkempt performance.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock-1)

The band traveled to Tokyo in 1984 with intentions of touring. Their first show in Japan was at the high school of a former exchange student Calvin knew.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock-1) The band members recorded _Three Tea Breakfast_, a five-song EP that marked the band's first release.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock2-2)

### Studio albums

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Beat_Happening&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Studio albums")\]

_[Beat Happening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening_(album) "Beat Happening (album)")_ (1985), their full-length debut, was critically acclaimed. This acclaim also followed with _[Jamboree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamboree_(Beat_Happening_album) "Jamboree (Beat Happening album)")_ (1988), which became their most iconic record. By the release of _[Dreamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreamy_(Beat_Happening_album) "Dreamy (Beat Happening album)")_ in 1991, Beat Happening was one of the most popular bands in the indie rock community, leading to their pivotal role in the [International Pop Underground Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Pop_Underground_Convention "International Pop Underground Convention"), which brought [anti-corporate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-corporatism "Anti-corporatism") rock its earliest mainstream acceptance. Their last full-length album was 1992's _[You Turn Me On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Turn_Me_On "You Turn Me On")_, which showed the band breaking many of their established conventions from earlier albums to further explore the influence of [the Velvet Underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Velvet_Underground "The Velvet Underground"); this was most audible on repetitive [post-rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-rock "Post-rock")-tinged songs such as "Tiger Trap" and "Godsend", the latter of which runs 9 minutes. The album was described by AllMusic as a "masterpiece." Though never announcing a break-up and claiming at one point to still practice once a month, the members of Beat Happening have moved on to various other projects. Johnson in particular pursued fleshing out the roster of his label [K Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Records "K Records"), in addition to producing music in several projects, including [Dub Narcotic Sound System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dub_Narcotic_Sound_System "Dub Narcotic Sound System") which Johnson formed to experiment with the [recording studio as an instrument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_studio_as_an_instrument "Recording studio as an instrument").

In 2000, the band released the "Angel Gone" single, which had been their first new release in eight years. This was followed in 2002 by _[Crashing Through](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crashing_Through_(album) "Crashing Through (album)")_, which collects all of the band's officially released music except for two of their tracks from a live cassette split with [The Vaselines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vaselines "The Vaselines"). The box set included a booklet containing a lengthy essay on the history and impact of the band by [Lois Maffeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lois_Maffeo "Lois Maffeo"), as well as rare photos of Calvin, Heather, and Bret.

In September 2015, the band announced the release of a new compilation spanning their entire career entitled _[Look Around](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Around_(Beat_Happening_album) "Look Around (Beat Happening album)")_, which was released by [Domino Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino_Recording_Company "Domino Recording Company") on 20 November 2015.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-3)

With the full cooperation of the band and the K label, Domino released a vinyl box set of the entire Beat Happening discography, _We Are Beat Happening_, in November 2019.

### Legacy

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Beat_Happening&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Legacy")\]

Beat Happening's live performances stood out for Calvin's dancing moves (pogo, hula, and shimmy), which he had seen on news reports about UK punks. His onstage behavior did not ingratiate the band to hardcore audiences when they toured with Fugazi in the late 1980s, and audiences were openly hostile, even throwing debris like ashtrays at the band.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-LoveRock3-4) Nonetheless, rock critic Michael Azzerrad suggests that Beat Happening "was a major force in widening the idea of a punk rocker from a mohawked guy in a motorcycle jacket to a nerdy girl in a cardigan."[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-Azzerrad-5) The presence of Heather Lewis on drums and Calvin's non-threatening stage presence presented a wider variety of behaviors and identities than other acts in the hardcore scene of the time, which was predominantly male and focused on aggression or hyper-masculinity.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-Azzerrad-5) Beat Happening has been cited as an influence on early [riot grrl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riot_grrl "Riot grrl") acts such as [Bratmobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratmobile "Bratmobile")[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-Azzerrad-5) and [Kathi Wilcox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathi_Wilcox "Kathi Wilcox") of [Bikini Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikini_Kill "Bikini Kill").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-Rookie-6)

[Kurt Cobain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Cobain "Kurt Cobain") had the K Records logo tattooed on his forearm, saying it was to "try and remind me to stay a child."[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-7)[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-8) The song "Lounge Act" on _[Nevermind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevermind "Nevermind")_ references his logo tattoo in the line "I'll arrest myself and wear a shield." Cobain also played guitar on a K Records release, "Bikini Twilight," with Johnson, released as The Go Team.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_Happening#cite_note-9)

[Calvin Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Johnson_(musician) "Calvin Johnson (musician)") was one of the founders of indie-rock label [K Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Records "K Records"), and contemporarily comprised one-half of the indie pop band [The Go Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Go_Team "The Go Team") along with [Tobi Vail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobi_Vail "Tobi Vail"); they had an approach and aesthetic similar to that of Beat Happening.
