**The Folk Implosion** is an American band founded in the early 1990s by [Lou Barlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Barlow "Lou Barlow") and John Davis.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-Larkin90-2) It was initially a side-project started by Barlow to explore different territory than that which was being canvassed with his primary band at the time, [Sebadoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebadoh "Sebadoh").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-Larkin90-2) The name is a play on the [Jon Spencer Blues Explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Spencer_Blues_Explosion "Jon Spencer Blues Explosion"). The band was on hiatus from 2004 to 2020.

## History

Following the release of Sebadoh's _Bubble and Scrape_ in 1993, Barlow received a tape from Davis, songwriter and librarian, that kicked off their collaboration as a songwriting team and studio recording project.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-:0-3) The Folk Implosion released _[Walk Through This World with the Folk Implosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walk_Through_This_World_with_the_Folk_Implosion "Walk Through This World with the Folk Implosion")_ on cassette in 1993 on the Communion Label and as a 7-inch EP in 1994 on Drunken Fish.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-4)

The band's status was relatively obscure before [Larry Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Clark "Larry Clark")'s film _[Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kids_(film) "Kids (film)")_, and its soundtrack, most of which contained original compositions by Barlow and Davis. Both Sebadoh and Folk Implosion contributed to the soundtrack, with the track "Daddy Never Understood" attributed to Deluxx Folk Implosion. Deluxx Folk Implosion included Barlow, Davis, and members of the band Deluxx, Bob Fay and Mark Peretta.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-:0-3)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-5) The introduction of Fay and Peretta to the band allowed the Folk Implosion to realize its songs live.

" [Natural One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_One "Natural One")", one of the Folk Implosion songs from the _Kids_ soundtrack, became a hit single.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-Larkin90-2) It peaked at no.29 on the [_Billboard_ Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), no.4 on the [Billboard Modern Rock Charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Rock_Tracks "Modern Rock Tracks") and no.20 on the [Mainstream Rock Charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainstream_Rock_Tracks "Mainstream Rock Tracks").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-6) The track peaked at no.45 in the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-British_Hit_Singles_&_Albums-7) The video for "Natural One" was included in [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV")'s [Buzz Bin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buzz_Bin "Buzz Bin").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-8)

In 1995, the band contributed the song "Indierockinstrumental" to _[Red Hot + Bothered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hot_+_Bothered "Red Hot + Bothered")_, a benefit album for people with [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS "AIDS"), produced by the [Red Hot Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hot_Organization "Red Hot Organization").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-9)

In 1996, the band covered the song " [I'm Just a Bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Just_a_Bill "I'm Just a Bill")" from _[Schoolhouse Rock!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoolhouse_Rock! "Schoolhouse Rock!")_ for the tribute album _[Schoolhouse Rock! Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoolhouse_Rock!_Rocks "Schoolhouse Rock! Rocks")_ as "Deluxx Folk Implosion".

Though the band attempted to duplicate the success of "Natural One", their subsequent releases were not as successful.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-Larkin90-2) _[Dare to Be Surprised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dare_to_Be_Surprised "Dare to Be Surprised")_ was released by Communion in 1997 and received 6/10 when reviewed in SPIN Magazine.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-10) The group had started work on the album in 1995 before "Natural One" was released.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-11) After the success of the [Kids Soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kids_soundtrack "Kids soundtrack"), Folk Implosion signed to [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records "Interscope Records") and recorded the 1999 studio album _[One Part Lullaby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Part_Lullaby "One Part Lullaby")._ The lead single "Free to Go" peaked moderately in both the US and UK. While the album was well reviewed, Barlow and Davis chose not to tour for the album and the project in its original incarnation slowed to an end.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-:0-3)[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-12)

After Davis left the band in 2000, Barlow recruited [Russ Pollard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Pollard "Russell Pollard") and [Imaad Wasif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaad_Wasif "Imaad Wasif") from the band [Alaska!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska! "Alaska!") to record _[The New Folk Implosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Folk_Implosion "The New Folk Implosion")_. In 2002, the band appeared as the backing musicians for the main character played by [Alessandro Nivola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Nivola "Alessandro Nivola") in the film _[Laurel Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurel_Canyon_(film) "Laurel Canyon (film)")_.

John Davis returned to teaching and began to release music again as a solo artist in 2013.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-14)

In 2021, the band begun recording new material, and played a small acoustic set live.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-15) They also released an expanded edition of the _Walk Through This World_ EP on Bandcamp on September 11, 2021.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-16) The following year, the band released the "Feel It If You Feel It" single, followed by the _It Just Goes With_ EP in March 2023, and it was their first new material in nearly 20 years. A few months later, Folk Implosion released _Music for Kids_. It consisted of the band's contributions to the _Kids_ soundtrack in addition to other songs recorded during the same era.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Folk_Implosion#cite_note-17)
