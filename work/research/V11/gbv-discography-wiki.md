The **[Guided by Voices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_by_Voices "Guided by Voices") discography** includes dozens of releases, both official and unofficial.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_by_Voices_discography#cite_note-3) [Robert Pollard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pollard "Robert Pollard"), the main creative force behind the band, is a prolific songwriter, having written or co-written more than 1,600 songs, with over 500 of them released under the Guided by Voices moniker.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_by_Voices_discography#cite_note-Pollard-4)

In addition to official Guided by Voices albums, there have been numerous official singles, extended plays, split releases and compilations, as well as four box-sets of previously unreleased material known as "Suitcases" that each contain four CDs with twenty-five tracks each, for a total of 100 songs per release.
