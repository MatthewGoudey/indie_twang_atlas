American songwriter

**Bill Fox** is an American musician from [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland,_Ohio "Cleveland, Ohio") musician who fronted the [garage pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garage_rock "Garage rock") band [The Mice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mice_(band) "The Mice (band)") and released two solo albums before abruptly leaving the music industry in 1998. After a cult following built over many years, music royalties enticed Fox to semi-reluctantly return to music in 2007. He released a third solo album in 2012.

## Early career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bill_Fox_(musician)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early career")\]

Bill Fox was the lead singer and [guitarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar "Guitar") of the three-piece Cleveland, Ohio garage pop band The Mice from 1984 to 1988. The band released one [single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_(music) "Single (music)"), one [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") and one [LP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LP_record "LP record") before Fox unexpectedly and suddenly exited the band, much to the dismay of his brother and bandmate Tommy Fox.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-1)

The band was one of the most successful indie bands on the burgeoning Ohio [power pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_pop "Power pop") scene at the time, and has been cited as an influence by the [Dayton, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio "Dayton, Ohio") band [Guided by Voices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_by_Voices "Guided by Voices").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-CMJ_New_Music_Monthly_Sep_1998-2)

## Solo career and exit from music

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bill_Fox_(musician)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Solo career and exit from music")\]

In the 1990s, Fox released two solo folk albums. The [Lo-fi recordings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi_music "Lo-fi music") on these albums were often made in his apartment using a [4-track cassette recorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-track_(multitrack) "4-track (multitrack)") using a microphone box or his thigh as percussion instruments.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-CMJ_New_Music_Monthly_Sep_1998-2)

After a brief West Coast tour for his second album, Fox promptly quit the music business and remained intentionally disconnected from all media contacts regarding his music for nearly a decade.

## Return to music

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bill_Fox_(musician)&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Return to music")\]

Fox was the subject of a lengthy article by Joe Hagan in the June/July 2007 issue of _[The Believer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Believer_(magazine) "The Believer (magazine)")_, in which he is referred to as "one of America’s greatest contemporary songwriters."[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-3) Hagan set out to make contact with Fox and get an answer to the question "Why Did Bill Fox Stop Playing Music?" Though he did not meet Fox, he did get his answer. Hagan's article made a large impact on past and present fans of Fox to rediscover his music. Reportedly the article prompted some iTunes royalties that have inspired Fox to both allow the reissue of his solo albums and begin performing again.

On April 3, 2008, [Scat Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scat_Records "Scat Records") announced Fox had agreed to allow them to re-release both of his albums on vinyl and CD. Robert Griffin, owner of Scat Records, told cleveland.com what Fox thinks of the reissues: "He just thinks they're music, and God, if some of these weird people still want to buy 'em, OK, let's print some more up."[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-4) The reissue of _Shelter from the Smoke_ was released late 2009, adding three tracks from Fox's short-lived early 1990s group The Radio Flyers, as well as both songs from the 1995 _Bird of the World_ 7" single. A reissue of _Transit Byzantium_ has yet to appear.

In 2009, Fox began to perform again in his native Cleveland and surrounding areas. In May 2010, he headlined at the 16th anniversary concerts of [Kelp Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelp_Records "Kelp Records"), based in [Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa "Ottawa"), Ontario, Canada.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-Ottawaperf-5) Fox headlined anniversary performances in Toronto,[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-6) Montreal[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-7) and Ottawa.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-Ottawaperf-5)

On June 8, 2010, [alternative rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock") band [Nada Surf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nada_Surf "Nada Surf") released a cover of Fox's song "Electrocution" on an album titled _[if i had a hifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_I_Had_A_Hi-Fi_(Nada_Surf_album) "If I Had A Hi-Fi (Nada Surf album)")_.

On October 20, 2011, a video entitled "An Anthem For Occupy Wall Street" was uploaded to [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube"). It contains images of the [Occupy Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street "Occupy Wall Street") events of that year along with a previously unreleased song by Bill Fox, entitled "Men Who Are Guilty of Crimes." Bill Fox approved the song's use in the video, discussing it in a November 2011 interview.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-8)

In 2012, Fox released his first album of new music in 14 years titled _One Thought Revealed_. He continues to play occasional shows.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-9)

In 2016, Fox released his first single since 1995, a split seven inch with Forgotten Souls of Antiquity.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Fox_(musician)#cite_note-ReferenceA-10)

In 2017 a re-issue of his cassette-only album 'Before I Went to Harvard' was pressed onto 12" vinyl through Eleventh Hour Recording Company and a previously unreleased song entitled "Mole in the Ground was included on Sound Asleep Records' " [Hit the Hay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hit_the_Hay "Hit the Hay") Vol. 9" compilation.

On April 15, 2025 Fox released a new album entitled _Resonance_.
