Musical artist

**Daniel Dale Johnston** (January 22, 1961 – c. September 11, 2019) was an American singer, musician and artist regarded as a significant figure in [outsider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outsider_music "Outsider music"), [lo-fi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi_music "Lo-fi music"), and [alternative music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_rock "Alternative rock") scenes.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-allmusic-bio-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-dazed-123-3) Most of his work consisted of cassettes recorded alone in his home,[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-AV2013-6) and his music was frequently cited for its "pure" and "childlike" qualities.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-7)

Johnston spent extended periods in [psychiatric institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatric_institution "Psychiatric institution")[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-AV2013-6) and was diagnosed with [bipolar disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_disorder "Bipolar disorder").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-8)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-allmusic-bio-2) He garnered a local following in the 1980s by passing out tapes of his music while working at a [McDonald's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's "McDonald's") in [Dobie Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobie_Center "Dobie Center") in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-:0-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-10) His cult status was propelled when [Nirvana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nirvana_(band) "Nirvana (band)")'s [Kurt Cobain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Cobain "Kurt Cobain") was seen wearing a [T-shirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-shirt "T-shirt") that featured the artpiece _"Jeremiah the Innocent"_[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-11) from Johnston's 1983 cassette album _[Hi, How Are You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hi,_How_Are_You "Hi, How Are You")_.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-AV2013-6)

Johnston also created visual art, and his illustrations were exhibited at galleries around the world. His struggles with mental illness were the subject of the 2005 documentary _[The Devil and Daniel Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_and_Daniel_Johnston "The Devil and Daniel Johnston")_. He died in 2019 of a suspected [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_attack "Heart attack").[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-12)

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Johnston was born in [Sacramento, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento,_California "Sacramento, California"), and grew up in [New Cumberland, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Cumberland,_West_Virginia "New Cumberland, West Virginia").[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-RSOBIT-13) He was the youngest of five children of William Dale "Bill" Johnston (1922–2017) and Mabel Ruth Voyles Johnston (1923–2010). He began recording music in the late 1970s on a $59 [Sanyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanyo "Sanyo") monaural [boombox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boombox "Boombox"), singing and playing piano as well as the [chord organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chord_organ "Chord organ").[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-RSOBIT-13)

Following graduation from [Oak Glen High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Glen_High_School "Oak Glen High School"), Johnston spent a few weeks at [Abilene Christian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abilene_Christian_University "Abilene Christian University") in [West Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Texas "West Texas") before dropping out. He later attended the art program at [Kent State University, East Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University_at_East_Liverpool "Kent State University at East Liverpool"), during which he recorded _[Songs of Pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_of_Pain "Songs of Pain")_ and _[More Songs of Pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_Songs_of_Pain "More Songs of Pain")_.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-14)

## Career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")\]

### 1980s–1990s

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 1980s–1990s")\]

In 1984, Johnston took a job at [McDonald's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's "McDonald's") and passed out tapes in the store.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-:0-9) Later, Johnston moved to [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") with a traveling carnival, working at a [corn dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corn_dog "Corn dog") stand.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-Free_Lance-Star-15) He began to attract the attention of the local press and gained a following by handing out tapes to people he met.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-16) Live performances were well-attended and hotly anticipated.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-17)

His local standing led to him being featured in a 1985 episode of the [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") program _[The Cutting Edge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I.R.S._Records_Presents_The_Cutting_Edge "I.R.S. Records Presents The Cutting Edge")_ featuring performers from Austin's " [new sincerity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_sincerity "New sincerity")" music scene.[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-18)

In 1988, Johnston visited New York City and recorded _[1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_(Daniel_Johnston_album) "1990 (Daniel Johnston album)")_ with producer [Mark Kramer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Kramer "Mark Kramer")[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-19) at his Noise New York studio.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-RSOBIT-13) It was Johnston's first professional recording following a decade of releasing home-made cassette recordings. His mental health deteriorated during the making of _1990_.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-PFOBIT-20) In 1989, he released the album _[It's Spooky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It's_Spooky "It's Spooky")_ in collaboration with singer [Jad Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jad_Fair "Jad Fair") of the band [Half Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_Japanese "Half Japanese").[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-21)

In 1990, Johnston played at a music festival in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"). On the way back to [West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia "West Virginia") on a private two-seater plane piloted by his father Bill, Johnston had a [manic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mania "Mania") psychotic episode; believing he was [Casper the Friendly Ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casper_the_Friendly_Ghost "Casper the Friendly Ghost"), Johnston removed the key from the plane's ignition and threw it outside. His father, a former U.S. Air Force pilot, managed to [successfully crash-land the plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Daniel_Johnston_plane_crash "1990 Daniel Johnston plane crash"), even though "there was nothing down there but trees". Although the plane was destroyed, Johnston and his father emerged with only minor injuries. As a result of this episode, Johnston was involuntarily committed to a mental hospital.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-22)

Interest in Johnston increased when [Kurt Cobain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Cobain "Kurt Cobain") was frequently photographed wearing a T-shirt featuring the cover image of Johnston's album _[Hi, How Are You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hi,_How_Are_You "Hi, How Are You")_ that music journalist [Everett True](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everett_True "Everett True") had given to him.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-RSOBIT-13) Cobain listed _[Yip/Jump Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yip/Jump_Music "Yip/Jump Music")_ as one of his favorite albums in his journal, in 1993.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-joyfulnoiserecordings.com-23)

Johnston's profile was further raised by [Kathy McCarty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_McCarty "Kathy McCarty")'s 1994 album _[Dead Dog's Eyeball: Songs of Daniel Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Dog's_Eyeball:_Songs_of_Daniel_Johnston "Dead Dog's Eyeball: Songs of Daniel Johnston")_.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-24)[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-25) It contained 19 renditions of songs written by Johnston.[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-26) McCarty became aware of Johnston when he gave her one of his cassette tapes after a performance of her band, Glass Eye, in 1985; this led to him later opening for the band.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-Free_Lance-Star-15)[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-27) McCarty supported the album with a North American tour,[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-28) and also issued an EP of additional Johnston material, _Sorry Entertainer_ in 1995.[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-29)

Despite Johnston's having been a resident in a [mental hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_hospital "Mental hospital") at the time, there was a bidding war to sign him. He refused to sign a multi-album deal with [Elektra Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektra_Records "Elektra Records") because [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica "Metallica") was on the label's roster and he was convinced that they were [Satanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satan "Satan") and would hurt him,[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-PFOBIT-20) also dropping his longtime manager, Jeff Tartakov, in the process.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-30) Ultimately he signed with new manager Tom Gimbel, who subsequently negotiated a deal with [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records") in February 1994 and that September released _[Fun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fun_(Daniel_Johnston_album) "Fun (Daniel Johnston album)")_, produced by [Paul Leary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Leary "Paul Leary") of [Butthole Surfers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butthole_Surfers "Butthole Surfers").[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-31) That same year, Danny Goldberg, who had championed the signing of Johnston, left Atlantic; in June 1996, with Goldberg no longer making A&R decisions, Atlantic dropped Johnston from the label.[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-PFOBIT-20)

In 1993, the Sound Exchange record store in Austin, Texas commissioned Johnston to paint a [mural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mural "Mural") of the _Hi, How Are You?_ frog (also known as "Jeremiah the Innocent") from the album's cover.[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-32) After the record store closed in 2003, the building remained unoccupied until 2004, when the Mexican grill franchise [Baja Fresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baja_Fresh "Baja Fresh") took ownership and decided they would remove the wall that held the mural. A group of people who lived in the neighborhood convinced the managers and contractors to keep the mural intact.[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-33) In 2018, the building housed a [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_food "Thai food") restaurant called Thai, How Are You;[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-34) the restaurant closed permanently in January 2020.[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-35) In April 2023 the building was razed, but the wall with the mural was left standing. The owner said there were no current plans for development, but that the wall would be integrated into any future designs.[\[36\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-36)

### 2000s

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2000s")\]

In 2004, Johnston released _[The Late Great Daniel Johnston: Discovered Covered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Great_Daniel_Johnston:_Discovered_Covered "The Late Great Daniel Johnston: Discovered Covered")_, a two-disc compilation. The first disc featured covers of his songs by artists including [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits "Tom Waits"), [Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beck "Beck"), [TV on the Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_on_the_Radio "TV on the Radio"), [Jad Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jad_Fair "Jad Fair"), [Eels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eels_(band) "Eels (band)"), [Bright Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Eyes_(band) "Bright Eyes (band)"), [Calvin Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Johnson_(musician) "Calvin Johnson (musician)"), [Death Cab for Cutie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Cab_for_Cutie "Death Cab for Cutie"), [Sparklehorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparklehorse "Sparklehorse"), [Mercury Rev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Rev "Mercury Rev"), [The Flaming Lips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flaming_Lips "The Flaming Lips") and [Starlight Mints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starlight_Mints "Starlight Mints"), with the second disc featuring Johnston's original recordings of the songs.[\[37\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-37) In 2005, Texas-based theater company [Infernal Bridegroom Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infernal_Bridegroom_Productions "Infernal Bridegroom Productions") received a Multi-Arts Production/ [MAP Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAP_Fund?action=edit&redlink=1 "MAP Fund (page does not exist)") grant to work with Johnston to create a [rock opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_opera "Rock opera") based on his music, titled _Speeding Motorcycle_.[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-38)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Daniel_Johnston_at_Emos_1.jpg/250px-Daniel_Johnston_at_Emos_1.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Daniel_Johnston_at_Emos_1.jpg) Johnston in 2006

In 2006, [Jeff Feuerzeig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Feuerzeig "Jeff Feuerzeig") released a documentary about Johnston, _[The Devil and Daniel Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_and_Daniel_Johnston "The Devil and Daniel Johnston")_; the film, four years in the making, collated some of the vast amount of recorded material Johnston (and in some case, others) had produced over the years to portray his life and music.[\[39\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-39) The film won high praise, receiving the Director's Award at the 2005 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival").[\[40\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-GUOBIT-40) The film also inspired more interest in Johnston's work, and increased his prestige as a touring artist. In 2006, Johnston's label, Eternal Yip Eye Music, released his first greatest-hits compilation, _Welcome to My World_.[\[41\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-41)

Through the next few years Johnston toured extensively across the world, and continued to attract press attention. His artwork was shown in galleries such as in London's [Aquarium Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquarium_Gallery "Aquarium Gallery"), New York's Clementine Gallery and at the [Liverpool Biennial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_Biennial "Liverpool Biennial") in 2006 and 2008,[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-42) and in 2009, his work was exhibited at "The Museum of Love" at Verge Gallery in [Sacramento, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento,_California "Sacramento, California").[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-43) In 2008, Dick Johnston, Johnston's brother and manager, revealed that "a movie deal based on the artist's life and music had been finalized with a tentative 2011 release."[\[44\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-chronicle-44) He also said that a deal had been struck with the [Converse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_shoes "Converse shoes") company for a "signature series" Daniel Johnston shoe.[\[44\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-chronicle-44) Later, it was revealed by Dick Johnston that Converse had dropped the plan.[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-45) In early 2008, a Jeremiah the Innocent collectible figurine was released in limited runs of four different colors.[\[46\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-46) Later in the year, Adjustable Productions released Johnston's first concert DVD, _[The Angel and Daniel Johnston – Live at the Union Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Angel_and_Daniel_Johnston_%E2%80%93_Live_at_the_Union_Chapel "The Angel and Daniel Johnston – Live at the Union Chapel")_, featuring a 2007 appearance in [Islington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islington "Islington"), London.[\[47\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-47)

_[Is and Always Was](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Is_and_Always_Was "Is and Always Was")_ was released on October 6, 2009, on Eternal Yip Eye Music.[\[48\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-48) In 2009, it was announced that [Matt Groening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Groening "Matt Groening") had chosen Johnston to perform at the edition of the [All Tomorrow's Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Tomorrow's_Parties_(music_festival) "All Tomorrow's Parties (music festival)") festival that he curated in May 2010, in [Minehead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minehead "Minehead"), England.[\[49\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-49) Also that year, Dr. Fun Fun and Smashing Studios developed an [iPhone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone "IPhone") [platform game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_game "Platform game") called _[Hi, How Are You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hi,_How_Are_You_(video_game) "Hi, How Are You (video game)")_. The game is similar to _[Frogger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frogger "Frogger")_, but features Johnston's art and music. Johnston played it during its development and liked it, although he was not familiar with the iPhone.[\[50\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-50)

### 2010s

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: 2010s")\]

On March 13, 2012, Johnston released his first comic book _Space Ducks – An Infinite Comic Book of Musical Greatness_[\[51\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-51) at SXSW, published by [BOOM! Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BOOM!_Studios "BOOM! Studios"). The comic book ties-in with the _Space Ducks_ album and an [iOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOS "IOS") [app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_apps "Mobile apps").[\[52\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-52) Johnston collaborated with skateboarding and clothing company [Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_(clothing) "Supreme (clothing)") on numerous collections (consisting of clothing and various accessories) showcasing his artwork.[\[53\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-53)

On March 1, 2012, [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn")-based photographer Jung Kim announced her photo book and traveling exhibition project with Johnston titled _DANIEL JOHNSTON: here_, a collaboration that began in 2008 when Kim first met Johnston and began photographing him on the road and at his home in [Waller, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waller,_Texas "Waller, Texas").[\[54\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-DAZED-54) On March 13, 2013, this photography book was published, featuring five years of documentation on Johnston.[\[54\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-DAZED-54) The opening exhibition at [SXSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SXSW "SXSW") festival featured a special performance by Johnston along with tribute performances led by [Jason Sebastian Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sebastian_Russo "Jason Sebastian Russo") formerly of [Mercury Rev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Rev "Mercury Rev").[\[55\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-55)[\[56\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-56) The second exhibition ran in May and June 2013 in London, England, and featured a special performance by Johnston along with tribute performances by the UK band Charlie Boyer and the Voyeurs with Steffan Halperin of the [Klaxons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaxons_(English_band) "Klaxons (English band)").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-dazed-123-3)[\[57\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-57) On October 10, 2013, [Jason Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Pierce "Jason Pierce") of [Spiritualized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritualized "Spiritualized") hosted the New York City opening of the exhibition, which included special tribute performances led by Pierce and [Glen Hansard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Hansard "Glen Hansard") of [The Swell Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Swell_Season "The Swell Season") and [The Frames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Frames "The Frames").[\[58\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-58)[\[59\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-59)

In November 2015, _[Hi, How Are You Daniel Johnston?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hi,_How_Are_You_Daniel_Johnston%3F "Hi, How Are You Daniel Johnston?")_, a short documentary about Johnston's life, was released featuring Johnston as his 2015 self and [Gabriel Sunday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Sunday "Gabriel Sunday") of _[Archie's Final Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie's_Final_Project "Archie's Final Project")_ as Johnston's 1983 self. The executive producers for the film included [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey "Lana Del Rey") and [Mac Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_Miller "Mac Miller").[\[60\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-60)

In the fall of 2017, Johnston embarked on what he announced would be his final tour, playing twelve concerts.[\[61\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-finaltour-61)[\[62\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-finaltour2-62) Each stop on the tour featured Johnston backed by a group that had been influenced by his music: [The Preservation All-Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preservation_Hall_Jazz_Band "Preservation Hall Jazz Band") in New Orleans; ## Death

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Daniel_Johnston&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Death")\]

On September 11, 2019, Johnston was found dead from a suspected [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_attack "Heart attack") at his home in [Waller, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waller,_Texas "Waller, Texas"). It is believed that he died during the previous night, a day after he had been discharged from hospital after unspecified kidney problems.[\[65\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-65)[\[66\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-66)[\[67\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-67)

Daniel Johnston is buried at Magnolia Cemetery in [Katy, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy,_Texas "Katy, Texas").[\[68\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-68)[\[69\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston#cite_note-69)
