[The Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats "The Mountain Goats") are an American, [Durham, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham,_North_Carolina "Durham, North Carolina")–based band, led by American singer-songwriter [John Darnielle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Darnielle "John Darnielle"). Darnielle began recording in 1991, and is known for his highly literate lyrics and (until 2002) his [lo-fi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo-fi "Lo-fi") recording style.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats_discography#cite_note-am_bio-1) The Mountain Goats' albums have featured a constantly changing line-up of musicians, with Darnielle the only constant; when performing live, the band commonly comprises only Darnielle backed by [Peter Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hughes_(musician) "Peter Hughes (musician)") on bass guitar and [Jon Wurster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Wurster "Jon Wurster") playing drums.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats_discography#cite_note-tmgn_faq-2) Their discography consists of 24 [studio albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_album "Studio album"), four [compilation albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compilation_album "Compilation album"), four [live albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_album "Live album"), six [cassette-based releases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cassette_releases_by_Mountain_Goats "List of cassette releases by Mountain Goats"), four [demo albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demo_album "Demo album"), 18 solo [extended plays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play"), seven collaborative [extended plays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play"), and 24 [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_(music) "Single (music)"). In addition, three unreleased albums circulate amongst collectors, and multiple unreleased or live-only tracks circulate as well.
