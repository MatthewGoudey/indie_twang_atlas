The Transfiguration of Blind Joe Death (1965, Riverboat, John Fahey): Beautiful Linda Getchell, Orinda-Moraga, I Am the Resurrection, On the Sunny Side of the Ocean, Tell Her to Come Back Home, ..., How Green Was My Valley, ...
Requia (1967, Vanguard, John Fahey): Requiem for John Hurt, Requiem for Russell Blaine Cooper, When the Catfish Is in Bloom, Requiem for Molly Pt.1-4, Fight On Christians Fight On.
Into the Light (2016, Marisa Anderson, per Bandcamp): Into the Light, In Waves, Waltz of Shadows, The Old Guard, He Is Without His Guns, Chimes, Resurrection, House of the Setting Sun, The Golden West, End of the Night. NOTE: "The Boys of Sligo" is NOT on this album.
Eli Winter (2022, Three Lobed Recordings -- confirmed via press coverage, per Bandcamp for tracklist): For a Chisos Bluebonnet, Davening in Threes, No Fear, Brain on Ice, Dayenu, Unbecoming. NOTE: "Time Is Not the Time That Was" and "Older" are NOT on this album.
No Depression (1990, Rockville, Uncle Tupelo): Graveyard Shift, That Year, Before I Break, No Depression, Factory Belt, Whiskey Bottle, Outdone, Train, Life Worth Livin', Flatness, So Called Friend, Screen Door.
Still Feel Gone (1991, Rockville, Uncle Tupelo): Gun, Looking for a Way Out, Fall Down Easy, Nothing, Still Be Around, Watch Me Fall, Punch Drunk, Postcard, D. Boon, True to Life, Cold Shoulder, Discarded, If That's Alright.
Anodyne (1993, Sire, Uncle Tupelo): Slate, Acuff-Rose, The Long Cut, Give Back the Key to My Heart, Chickamauga, New Madrid, Anodyne, We've Been Had, Fifteen Keys, High Water, No Sense in Lovin', Steal the Crumbs.
Faithless Street (1995, Mood Food Records, Whiskeytown): Midway Park, Drank Like a River, Too Drunk To Dream, What May Seem Like Love, Faithless Street, Mining Town, If He Can't Have You, Black Arrow Bleeding Heart, Matrimony, Hard Luck Story, Top Dollar, Oklahoma. NOTE: "Excuse Me While I Break My Own Heart Tonight" is on Strangers Almanac (1997), NOT Faithless Street.
Strangers Almanac (1997, Outpost/Geffen, Whiskeytown): Inn Town, Excuse Me While I Break My Own Heart Tonight, Yesterday's News, 16 Days, Everything I Do, Houses on the Hill, Turn Around, Dancing with the Women at the Bar, Waiting to Derail, Avenues, Losering, Somebody Remembers the Rose, Not Home Anymore.
Pneumonia (2001, Lost Highway Records, Whiskeytown): The Ballad of Carol Lynn, Don't Wanna Know Why, Jacksonville Skyline, Reasons to Lie, Don't Be Sad, Sit & Listen to the Rain, Under Your Breath, Mirror Mirror, Paper Moon, What the Devil Wanted, Crazy About You, My Hometown, Easy Hearts, Bar Lights.
Trace (1995, Warner Bros., Son Volt): Windfall, Drown, Breakwaters, Tonight, Lose Your Way, Still in Love, Goodbye, Looking for a Way Out, Too Early, Tim, Ghost, Nothing to Lose. NOTE: "Tear Stained Eye" is NOT on this album (it's on Son Volt's Straightaways, 1997).
Bloomed (1994 Glitterhouse Germany / 1995 Dejadisc US, Richard Buckner): Lil' Wallet Picture (confirmed via Spotify catalog, independent of the WP tracklist scrape which omitted it), Blue and Wonder, Rainsquall, 22, Mud, Six Years, This Is Where, Gauzy Dress in the Sun, Daisychain, Desire, Up North, Surprise AZ, Cradle to the Angel.
