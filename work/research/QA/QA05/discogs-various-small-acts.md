- Mason Proffit "Wanted" (1969) and "Movin' Toward Happiness" (1971): confirmed on Happy Tiger Records via Apple Music bio (https://music.apple.com/us/artist/mason-proffit/78427024): "1969's Wanted! Mason Proffit and 1971's Movin' Toward Happiness were released by Happy Tiger". Matches row.
- Hearts And Flowers "Now Is the Time for Hearts and Flowers": Discogs master https://www.discogs.com/master/321652 confirms 1967.
- Commander Cody and His Lost Planet Airmen "Lost in the Ozone": Discogs artist page https://www.discogs.com/artist/290316-Commander-Cody-And-His-Lost-Planet-Airmen confirms "first album in late 1971, Lost In The Ozone". Matches row (1971, Paramount).
- The Dillards "Wheatstraw Suite": Discogs master https://www.discogs.com/master/269695 confirms Elektra EKS-74035, Dec 1968.
- Chris Hillman "Clear Sailin'": Discogs master https://www.discogs.com/master/250742 confirms Asylum Records, 1977.
- Bobby Charles (self-titled): Discogs master https://www.discogs.com/master/392827 confirms Bearsville BR-2104, US, 1972.
- Gene Parsons "Kindling": Discogs https://www.discogs.com/release/2289944-Gene-Parsons-Kindling confirms Warner Bros. Records BS 2687, 1973.
- Nashville West (self-titled): Discogs artist page https://www.discogs.com/artist/870464-Nashville-West confirms label "Sierra Briar Records, Sierra Records", 1978. Row says "Sierra Records" - close enough (same imprint family), ok.
- Swampwater (self-titled, 1971): CONFLICT — Discogs shows this album under BOTH "King Records (3) – KS 1122" and "RCA Victor – LSP-4572" release entries. Cross-checked with https://www.zeroto180.org/swampwater-country-rock-on-king/ (text: "Swampwater (1970) on RCA Victor"), https://www.burritobrother.com/gib4.htm ("Swampwater album 1971 - SWAMPWATER: Swampwater (RCA LSP4572)"), and an eBay listing for the "1971 RCA Country-Rock LP". Multiple independent sources confirm RCA Victor as the true original label; the "King Records (3)" Discogs entry appears to be a mislabeled/different catalog entry. Row's label "King Records" appears to be WRONG -> should be RCA Victor.
