Brand New Year (1999, Doolittle): Brand New Year, Nancy Sinatra, Alone in Bad Company, I've Been Dying, Sometimes Found, Headed for the Ditch, Helpless, Let Me Know, ... "Nancy Sinatra" confirmed as the first single.
Lean Forward (2009, Bloodshot) full tracklist per No Depression review: The Long Way, Shame On Me, Nothin' But a Driver, Hard Times, Give Me Room, Way It Used to Be, Done It All, Slip Away, Kid Next Door, Open Your Eyes, Solitaire, Get on the Bus.
NOTE: there is NO song called "Lean Forward" on the Lean Forward album (title-only, not a real track).
