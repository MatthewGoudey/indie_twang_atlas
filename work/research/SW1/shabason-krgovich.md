Released 12 June 2026, 7 e.p. (Japanese import CD label; Bandcamp release itself self-distributed). Toronto/Vancouver duo Joseph Shabason and Nicholas Krgovich, with pedal steel (Ian McGimpsey), banjo/fiddle (Sam Amidon). Explicitly cites Neil Young's "Harvest Moon", R.E.M., k.d. lang, and Blue Rodeo's alt-country Five Days in July as touchstones. Tracks: Begin Again; Along the Dance Away; Field Mouse; Midday Sun; No. Two; Road; 43; Boppin' Along; Dry Corner; Little Wind; Time of Your Life.
